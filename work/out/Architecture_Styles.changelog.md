# Architecture Styles: changelog

**New deck:** 217 notes (200 basic, 17 cloze) in 11 subdecks, 65 analogies (30%), 13 hand-drawn SVGs (`media/arch_*.svg`, 60 KB total) on 34 notes.

**Scope:** buildings, not software. Every style gets three angles: how to recognise it on the street, which structural idea made it possible, and what it reacted against. Contrast cards for every commonly confused pair (Doric/Ionic, Greek/Roman temple, pendentive/squinch, Romanesque/Gothic, Renaissance/Baroque, Baroque/Rococo, Neoclassical/Beaux-Arts, Greek Revival/Palladian, Art Nouveau/Art Deco, Arts & Crafts/Art Nouveau, Chinese/Japanese, Wright/European modernism, International Style/Brutalism, Brutalism/High-tech, Postmodern/Decon, CLT/glulam, LEED/Passivhaus).

## Deliberately left out
- Painting, sculpture and interior decoration (an art-history deck can own them); architecture is treated as structure + recognition.
- Dates, patrons and biographies except where the date/name is the vocabulary (1851, 1932 MoMA show, Bauhaus, Suger, Palladio). No "who designed X" cards.
- Mesoamerican, Khmer and Sub-Saharan traditions beyond the vernacular/Kéré cards; Russian, Ottoman and colonial-era regional variants; building services and codes.
- Landscape architecture and detailed urban planning theory (only grid, Haussmann, Garden City, perimeter block vs tower-in-park, CIAM, New Urbanism as anchors).

## Overlap avoided
- Physics for Datacenters: no stress/strain formulas; tension vs compression stated qualitatively only.
- Architect Craft / Software Architecture: no "architecture" metaphors crossing into software; "form follows function", Conway etc. not re-used.
- Adaptive Systems / Economics: Bilbao effect and starchitecture kept to one card each, no diffusion or market theory.
