# Philosophy: changelog

**New deck:** 219 notes in 12 subdecks, chronological then thematic; 17 cloze, 48 analogies (22%), 2 SVGs (`phil_divided_line.svg`, `phil_free_will_map.svg`).

## Subdecks
01 Pre-Socratics & Sophists (10) · 02 Socrates, Plato & Aristotle (19) · 03 Hellenistic Schools & Eastern Anchors (15) ·
04 Medieval: Augustine to Ockham (14) · 05 Rationalists & Empiricists (20) · 06 Kant, Idealism & Hegel (17) ·
07 The 19th Century (17) · 08 Analytic Philosophy & Language (19) · 09 Phenomenology, Existentialism & Pragmatism (16) ·
10 Metaphysics & Mind (21) · 11 Epistemology, Logic & Argument (24) · 12 Ethics & Political Philosophy (27).

## Emphasis
Every major thinker gets at least one "reconstruct the argument" card (premises → conclusion), the standard objection in `back` or `extra`, and a "what did X react against" angle (Parmenides vs Heraclitus, Plato vs Protagoras, Spinoza vs Descartes, Kant vs Hume, Kierkegaard/Marx vs Hegel, Moore/Russell vs idealism, Husserl vs psychologism, Sartre vs essentialism). Famous thought experiments covered: ship of Theseus, Chinese room, Mary's room, zombies, brain in a vat, trolley switch vs footbridge, experience machine, utility monster, transplant surgeon, Frankfurt cases, Parfit's fission, Avicenna's flying man, Leibniz's mill, Twin Earth, drowning child, Wilt Chamberlain. Eastern anchors (Confucius, Laozi, Buddhist no-self and Four Noble Truths) kept to 6 cards.

## Deliberately left out
Biographical/dating trivia; Empedocles, Anaxagoras, Plotinus, Duns Scotus, Maimonides, Malebranche, Reid, Fichte beyond one anchor, Schelling, Comte, Bergson, Whitehead, Carnap's later work, Davidson, Rorty, Levinas, Derrida/Foucault (post-structuralism would be its own deck); philosophy of science and philosophy of religion beyond the three classic theistic arguments and their refutations.

## Overlap with Adoption, Epistemics & Governance avoided
Not restated: Hume's problem of induction, Popper, Kuhn, Lakatos, Duhem–Quine (testing side), underdetermination, Neurath's boat, reliabilism, Peirce's fallibilism, IBE, Occam's razor justifications, Bayes-as-evidence, the three-way consequentialism/deontology/virtue comparison, act vs rule utilitarianism, the two CI formulations, veil of ignorance (what it is/yields), is–ought gap, harm principle, negative/positive liberty, doctrine of double effect. Where this deck touches the same thinker it takes a different angle: Hume on causation/self/passions; Quine's semantic attack in "Two Dogmas" (not the testing holism); Kant's good will, autonomy, rigorism objection and Hegel's formalism charge (not the CI formulations); Rawls's maximin argument, anti-utilitarian objection and reflective equilibrium (not the veil itself); Mill's higher pleasures and liberty-of-thought argument (not the harm principle); trolley as switch-vs-footbridge contrast (not double effect). Four `extra` fields point to the Epistemics deck explicitly.
