# Music History changelog

New deck, **219 notes** (201 basic, 18 cloze), 61 analogies (28%), 9 hand-drawn SVGs (`media/music_*.svg`) used on 14 notes. Generated from a hand-written Python script; `tools/check.py`: 0 errors, 0 warnings.

Subdecks: 01 How Music Works (26) · 02 Instruments & Technology (20) · 03 Ancient & Medieval (16) · 04 Renaissance (13) · 05 Baroque (21) · 06 Classical (19) · 07 Romantic (22) · 08 Late Romantic to Modernism (17) · 09 Twentieth Century after 1945 (14) · 10 Jazz (17) · 11 Popular Music Lineage (19) · 12 Non-Western Traditions (15).

## Scope and emphasis
- Every era opens with a "what can you hear" card; most style cards ask what an innovation solved or reacted against; contrast cards for the usual confusions (Baroque/Classical, idée fixe/transformation/leitmotif, Brahms/Wagner, Verdi/Wagner, Debussy/Ravel, Stravinsky/Schoenberg, Berg/Webern, serialism/Cage, cool/hard bop, swing/bebop/modal, Motown/Stax, Hindustani/Carnatic, Balinese/Javanese).
- Anchors kept to 1–3 works or people per movement (Orfeo, WTC, Eroica, Tristan, Rite, Kind of Blue, Music for 18 Musicians…); dates appear only where they are the vocabulary (1913, 1959, 4'33").
- Tuning arc runs through the deck: Pythagorean → meantone → well → equal temperament, with one MathJax cloze (\(2^{1/12}\), cents).
- SVGs: overtone series, circle of fifths, sonata form, fugue exposition, ADSR envelope, 12-bar blues, three textures, era timeline, polyrhythm/bell pattern. No downloaded images.

## Deliberately left out
- Composer biography, patrons, birthplaces, opus catalogues; Wagner's politics; the "Mozart effect" and similar folklore.
- Notation reading (clefs, note values as a skill), ear-training exercises, instrument technique.
- Many worthy anchors (Monteverdi's Vespers, Purcell, Schumann, Bruckner, Sibelius, Ives, Copland, Britten, Varèse, Boulez's works, Coltrane's sheets of sound, Prince, Radiohead…) to keep 1–3 per movement.
- Film-music, musical theatre, K-pop and Latin pop beyond one lineage card each or none; Chinese, Japanese and Arabic traditions get a single card (maqam) at most.

## Overlap with existing decks (avoided or framed differently)
- Philosophy deck has Pythagoreans and Schopenhauer cards: here Pythagoras is framed purely as tuning ratios and Schopenhauer appears only in an `extra`.
- Art History deck covers Impressionism, Expressionism and Romanticism in painting; the musical cards here stress the *audible* markers and Debussy's dislike of the label, without repeating the art-historical definitions.
- Physics for Datacenters has nothing on acoustics; the overtone/consonance cards stand alone.
