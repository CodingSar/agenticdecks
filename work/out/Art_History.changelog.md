# Art History changelog

New deck, 210 notes (203 basic, 7 cloze), 12 subdecks, 50 analogies (24%), 35 contrast cards, 10 hand-drawn SVGs (`media/art_*.svg`, 2–6 KB each) on 12 cards. All guids `null`.

Subdecks: 01 How to Look (24) · 02 Materials & Techniques (18) · 03 Prehistoric & Ancient Near East (11) · 04 Greek & Roman (15) · 05 Medieval: Byzantine, Romanesque, Gothic (19) · 06 Renaissance: Italian & Northern (21) · 07 Mannerism, Baroque, Rococo (17) · 08 Neoclassicism, Romanticism, Realism (16) · 09 Impressionism & Post-Impressionism (16) · 10 Early Modernism 1905–1940 (25) · 11 Post-war: AbEx to Conceptual (15) · 12 Non-Western Traditions (13).

## Design
- Every movement gets the same three angles where they apply: *how to recognise it*, *what it reacted against*, *what technique or technology enabled it* (oil glazes, canvas, printmaking, paint tubes, synthetic pigments, photography, concrete, pointed arch, flying buttress, silkscreen).
- Anchors limited to 1–3 works/people per movement, named inside the answer rather than asked for; no birth dates, patrons or commission trivia. Dates appear only in `source` labels ("Baroque · 1600–1750").
- Contrast cards (`art::contrast`) for every commonly confused pair: chiaroscuro/tenebrism, linear/atmospheric perspective, buon/secco fresco, engraving/etching, Classical/Hellenistic, Romanesque/Gothic, Giotto/Siena, Italian/Northern Renaissance, disegno/colorito, Rubens/Poussin, Baroque/Rococo, Neoclassicism/Romanticism, Constable/Turner, Degas/Monet, Fauvism/Expressionism, Brücke/Blaue Reiter, Analytic/Synthetic Cubism, Kandinsky/Mondrian, Deco/Bauhaus, action/colour field, Minimalism/AbEx.
- SVGs: one-point perspective, colour wheel, composition (thirds + pyramid), column orders, round vs pointed arch (thrust), Gothic section with flying buttress, kouros vs contrapposto, chiaroscuro sphere + value scale, atmospheric perspective, relief vs intaglio. Rendered and visually checked on white.

## Deliberately left out
- Architecture beyond what explains a style's look (no Palladio, no Le Corbusier/International Style detail, no skyscrapers); photography and film as art forms; Pre-Columbian, African and Oceanic art except via the Primitivism card; Indian and South-East Asian traditions (non-Western subdeck stays at anchor level: Chinese ink, ukiyo-e, Islamic ornament, Persian/Mughal miniatures).
- Movement catalogues (Orphism, Vorticism, Precisionism, Tachisme, Arte Povera, Fluxus, Op/Kinetic, Neo-Expressionism, YBAs) and artist biographies.
- Museum/provenance/market facts, named theorists beyond Alberti, Winckelmann, Wölfflin and Greenberg.

## Overlap with existing decks
- None found: no existing deck covers art, architecture history or aesthetics (grep for Renaissance/Baroque/Gothic/perspective/fresco hit only ArchiMate/BPMN "viewpoint/perspective" senses).
- Mild thematic echoes (avant-garde "framework churn", Salon as app-store gatekeeper, literati scrolls as comment threads) are analogies only, not duplicated cards.
