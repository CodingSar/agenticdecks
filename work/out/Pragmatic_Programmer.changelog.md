## Approach
- Each card focuses on the *why* and *when* behind a tip, not on reciting the tip's wording. The tip itself is often in the Context box as `<b>Tip:</b>`.
- Lists (knowledge portfolio, communication, Design by Contract, event strategies, inheritance alternatives, actor rules, security principles, starter kit, agility recipe) are clozes, one deletion per item.
- `source` gives the book topic number (`Pragmatic Programmer · Topic N`) so you can look it up.
- Left out: author, edition and history trivia, plus the shell and editor how-tos that don't stand alone on a card.
- No guids: these are new notes, so `build.py` derives them from deck, front and back.
