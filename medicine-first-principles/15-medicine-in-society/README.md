# 15 Medicine in Society

> **Layer:** 15 Medicine in Society
> **Children:** [Health systems](01-health-systems/README.md) · [Ethics, law and the profession](02-ethics-law-and-the-profession/README.md) · [Populations and the future](03-populations-and-the-future/README.md)

## What this area is about

Every layer below this one asks how the body works, how it fails, and how a clinician can recognise and reverse the failure. This layer asks what happens when that knowledge meets money, law, values, information systems, inequality, the planet and its own past. It sits at the top because it needs all of the rest: you cannot price a treatment before you know its effect size ([10](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md)) or consent to an operation before you know its risks ([13](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md)).

The unifying idea is that **a clinical decision is never made by one doctor and one patient alone**. Behind every prescription stand a payer who decided it was affordable, a regulator who decided it was safe, a trial that decided it worked, a law that decided who may agree to it, an information system that carried the result, and a society whose arrangements decided whether the patient arrived early or late. The three children look from three distances: the system the clinician works inside, the duties and rules the clinician works under, and the populations and forces that shape the whole enterprise.

## Why this layer sits here

Medicine is applied to people one at a time, but it is organised, paid for, regulated and judged in aggregate. This layer needs the lower layers for its facts and gives back a set of **constraints and values that become requirements** on every clinical decision:

| Lower layer | What this layer takes from it | What it gives back |
|---|---|---|
| [01 Quantitative foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md) | Decision analysis, Bayes, signal and noise, feedback | Health economics is decision analysis with money as one outcome; medical Artificial Intelligence (AI) is probability at scale |
| [04 Genetics and evolution](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md) | Variation, resistance, population thinking | The ethics of genomic data; antimicrobial stewardship as a commons problem |
| [07 Microbiology and immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md) | Transmission, immunity, vaccination | Pandemic preparedness, the economics and ethics of vaccination |
| [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) | Multifactorial causation, environmental injury | Social and planetary determinants as upstream causes |
| [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) | What a drug does and how it was approved | Pricing, regulation, access, pharmacovigilance as a safety system |
| [10 Epidemiology and evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) | Effect sizes, Number Needed to Treat (NNT), surveillance, screening biases | Priority setting, guidelines as policy, outbreak response |
| [11 Clinical method](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) | Reasoning, tests, shared decision-making | Consent law, cognitive safety, the record as an instrument |
| [12–14 Specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) | What can be done for each disease | What should be done, for whom, at what cost, and who decides |

The abstraction this layer contributes is **the system as a patient**: the same vocabulary used for one body (homeostasis, feedback, compensation, supply and demand, injury and repair) applies to a hospital, a health service or a planet, and the same reasoning under uncertainty applies to a budget as to a diagnosis.

Read health systems first: it defines the arena, and its leaf on [health economics](01-health-systems/health-economics-and-priority-setting.md) states the central fact that resources are finite, so every "yes" is a "no" to someone else. Ethics then asks how to say no justly, how to respect the person in front of you, and what a profession is for. Populations widen the lens from the clinic to the society and the planet, and [history](03-populations-and-the-future/history-of-medicine.md) and [the future](03-populations-and-the-future/the-future-of-medicine.md) place the whole tree in time: the ideas in layers 02 to 14 displaced older ideas and will be displaced in turn.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[01 Quantitative and scientific foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md):** cost-effectiveness analysis is expected-value decision theory; quality improvement is [feedback and control](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/feedback-and-control.md) applied to a ward; a diagnostic algorithm's false alarms are [signal and noise](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/information-signals-and-noise.md) at scale.
- **[09 Pharmacology and therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** [drug approval](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/05-drug-development-and-toxicology/from-target-to-approval.md) is where regulation first touches a molecule; pricing and patents decide who receives it.
- **[10 Epidemiology and evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md):** every figure in this layer (a life-expectancy gap, a pandemic's excess mortality, a QALY gain) is an epidemiological measure; [guidelines](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/02-evidence-based-medicine/grading-evidence-and-guidelines.md) are where evidence becomes policy.
- **[11 Clinical method and diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** [shared decision-making](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/prognosis-and-shared-decision-making.md) is the clinical face of consent law; [cognitive bias](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/clinical-reasoning-and-cognitive-bias.md) is the individual face of patient safety.
- **[12 Organ-system specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md):** [antimicrobial stewardship](../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/antimicrobial-stewardship.md) and [infection control](../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/infection-control-and-healthcare-associated-infection.md) are the specialty-level face of global and planetary health.
- **[13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md):** [the surgical decision](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/the-surgical-decision.md) is where consent is most formal; [disaster medicine](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/prehospital-and-disaster-medicine.md) is triage ethics under scarcity.
- **[14 Life course, mind and integrative care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md):** [end-of-life decisions](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/death-dying-and-end-of-life-decisions.md) test capacity and best-interests law; [primary care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/family-medicine-and-the-generalist.md) is where social determinants walk through the door.

## Connections across layers

The flow is two-way. Upward, every lower layer supplies the facts this layer prices, regulates and distributes. Downward, this layer's conclusions become requirements: a cost-effectiveness threshold decides which drug from layer 09 is funded; consent law decides how a surgeon in layer 13 must talk; a planetary-health constraint decides which anaesthetic gas is used. [History](03-populations-and-the-future/history-of-medicine.md) shows the tree itself is a product of this feedback.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Harm and benefit](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): the whole layer is this principle applied to populations and budgets; NNT becomes cost per QALY, and "first, do no harm" becomes patient safety.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): priority setting, pandemic forecasting and AI diagnosis all reason under uncertainty with imperfect data.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): quality improvement, regulation and surveillance are feedback loops on a system; their delays and gains decide whether they stabilise or oscillate.
- [Supply and demand](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): health services match finite staff, beds and money to demand; mismatch is waiting, rationing or collapse.
- [The host and its environment](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#the-host-and-its-environment): social position, income, air and climate are exposures as real as a pathogen.
- [Trade-offs and evolutionary legacy](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#trade-offs-and-evolutionary-legacy): antimicrobial resistance as a tragedy of the commons; every allocation rule trades efficiency against equity.
- [Time courses](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): prevention pays decades later; pandemics move in weeks; ideas in medicine turn over in generations.
