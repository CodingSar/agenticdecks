# Drug development and toxicology

> **Layer:** [09 Pharmacology and Therapeutics](../README.md) › Drug development and toxicology
> **Children:** [From target to approval](from-target-to-approval.md) · [Toxicology and poisoning](toxicology-and-poisoning.md)

## What this area is about

The rest of the layer describes drugs that exist and work at doses that help. This area describes the two boundaries of that territory: how a molecule earns the right to be called a drug, and what happens when any substance, drug or not, is taken past the point where it helps. They belong together because they are two views of the same curve. Development searches the dose–response relationship from the left, looking for the lowest dose that produces benefit and the highest that is tolerable, and spends a decade and a fortune narrowing the gap between them; toxicology starts from the right, where the curve becomes injury and death, and works backwards to thresholds, limits and antidotes. Both rest on the same kinetics and the same receptor theory, and preclinical toxicology is literally the first stage of development.

The unifying idea is that **evidence of benefit and harm is gathered under uncertainty, in stages, at rising stakes**. A drug candidate is a hypothesis tested in cells, animals, volunteers and patients in an order designed so that cheap failures come first; a poisoning is managed by the same reasoning run in minutes rather than years, from mechanism to kinetics to the intervention that each permits. Neither ever reaches certainty: approval leaves rare harms to be found afterwards, and exposure limits are set with safety factors because the human curve is never fully known.

Read development first; it introduces the vocabulary of phases, surrogates and regulation that later layers assume, and it ends with pharmacovigilance, which hands over to toxicology by asking what the drug does at excess and in the wrong patient. Toxicology then closes the layer by applying every previous parent at once: the kinetics of saturation and dialysis, the receptor theory of antidotes, the drug classes read backwards as toxidromes.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Pharmacokinetics](../01-pharmacokinetics/README.md):** phase I exists to measure F, Vd and CL in humans; overdose is the regime where first-order kinetics fail and where Vd and protein binding decide whether dialysis can help.
- **[Pharmacodynamics](../02-pharmacodynamics/README.md):** the therapeutic index that development tries to widen is the ratio toxicology measures; pharmacovigilance is both the fourth phase of development and the epidemiology of low-grade poisoning; pharmacogenomics explains why some volunteers and some poisoned patients fare worse than the median.
- **[Drug classes by mechanism](../03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/README.md):** every class is a target that survived the pipeline, and the examples of failure in development (antiarrhythmics, HDL-raising drugs) are drawn from those classes; toxidromes are the autonomic and central nervous system classes at excess; antidotes are their antagonists.
- **[Non-drug therapeutics](../04-non-drug-therapeutics/README.md):** devices and procedures pass through a weaker evidence pipeline than drugs, a contrast the development leaf draws; radiation protection and the linear no-threshold model are toxicology with a physical agent; fluid and transfusion have their own dose-dependent harms.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Dose–response](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#dose-response): the area's spine, from the first human dose to the median lethal dose.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): approval is a benefit–harm judgement; exposure limits are acceptable-risk judgements.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): phases are sequential experiments under uncertainty; rare harms are bounded, not excluded.
- [Cause and mechanism](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#cause-and-mechanism): surrogate end-points fail when the mechanism is not the whole cause; the toxidrome is mechanism inferred from signs.
- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): latent periods in poisoning, the decade from target to approval, the decades from exposure to cancer.
