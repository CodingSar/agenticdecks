## What this area is about

How vertebrates recognise any molecule they have never met, improve the response while it is happening, and remember it for life. The method is the opposite of innate immunity's: instead of a few inherited receptors for common patterns, each lymphocyte assembles its own receptor at random, producing a repertoire of billions, and antigen selects and expands the few that fit. The response is slow the first time (one to two weeks) and fast every time after, and it is exact enough to tell one strain of influenza from another.

The unifying idea is **specificity with memory, bought at the price of self-recognition**. Random receptors inevitably include ones that bind the body's own molecules, so the system spends most of its developmental effort destroying or restraining them, and it acts only when innate recognition has certified that a real threat is present. The five leaves describe how antigen is displayed (presentation), the two cell lineages that read it (T cells for peptides inside cells, B cells for shapes outside them), what the response leaves behind and how vaccines exploit it (memory), and how the system avoids attacking self (tolerance). Nearly every immune disease in the next area is a failure of one of these five.

```
   innate signal licenses dendritic cell
                 |
      antigen presentation (MHC I: inside; MHC II: eaten)
                 |
            T cells  ---help--->  B cells  --->  antibody
           /       \                 |
     CD8 kill     CD4 direct     germinal centre: affinity maturation
                                      |
          contraction  ---->  memory cells + long-lived plasma cells  (vaccination)
                 |
     throughout: tolerance deletes, anergises or suppresses self-reactive clones
```

Read antigen presentation first, because T cells see nothing without it. T cells come next: they are the decision-makers, and B cells need their help to make good antibody. Memory and vaccination draws on both lineages and is where the area meets public health. Tolerance is last because it is defined against everything before it: the same activation steps, run without co-stimulation, produce silence instead of attack.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Microbes and infection](../01-microbes-and-infection/README.md):** intracellular pathogens are the reason for cytotoxic T cells and MHC class I; polysaccharide capsules are the reason conjugate vaccines exist; antigenic variation in influenza, Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) and malaria is why memory and vaccines sometimes fail; the microbiome is the largest antigen load tolerance must manage.
- **[Innate immunity](../02-innate-immunity/README.md):** adaptive responses start only when innate recognition matures a dendritic cell; the first cytokines choose the helper programme; antibody in turn directs complement and phagocytes and arms Natural Killer (NK) cells.
- **[Immunity in disease](../04-immunity-in-disease/README.md):** hypersensitivity is these effectors misdirected, autoimmunity is tolerance failing, immunodeficiency is one of these cell types missing, and transplantation and tumour immunology are the two cases where medicine tries to reverse the system's natural decision about self and non-self.
