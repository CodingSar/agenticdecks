# Immunity in disease

> **Layer:** [07 Microbiology and Immunology](../README.md) › Immunity in disease
> **Children:** [Hypersensitivity and allergy](hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md) · [Autoimmunity](autoimmunity.md) · [Immunodeficiency](immunodeficiency.md) · [Transplant and tumour immunology](transplant-and-tumour-immunology.md)

## What this area is about

What goes wrong with a system whose job is to tell self from non-self and harmless from dangerous, using receptors that are generated at random and patterns that are only approximately diagnostic. Every immune disease is an error of recognition in one of three directions: a response against something harmless (allergy), a response against self (autoimmunity, rejection of a graft), or no response where one is needed (immunodeficiency, tumour escape). The mechanisms of damage are never new; they are the antibodies, complement, mast cells and T cells of the previous two areas, aimed wrongly or absent.

The unifying idea is that **immune disease is the cost of imperfect recognition, or of its absence**. The same trade-off runs through all four leaves. A repertoire wide enough to recognise anything will recognise self; tolerance strong enough to prevent that will sometimes shelter a tumour; immunosuppression strong enough to protect a graft will let latent viruses out; checkpoint blockade strong enough to kill a tumour will cause colitis. Medicine's interventions in this area, from antihistamines to Chimeric Antigen Receptor (CAR) T cells, are each an attempt to move the balance in one direction and pay the predictable cost in the other.

## How the children fit together

| Child | Its role in the whole | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [Hypersensitivity and allergy](hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md) | The four mechanisms of immune damage; type 2 immunity against harmless antigens | Antibodies, complement, T cells (02, 03) |
| [Autoimmunity](autoimmunity.md) | Those mechanisms against self: tolerance failing at several checkpoints | Hypersensitivity; tolerance, Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) (03) |
| [Immunodeficiency](immunodeficiency.md) | The absence of a component, and the infections that name it | T cells, B cells, complement and phagocytes (02, 03) |
| [Transplant and tumour immunology](transplant-and-tumour-immunology.md) | Suppressing recognition of a graft; releasing recognition of a tumour | MHC, T cells, tolerance (03), Natural Killer (NK) cells (02) |

```
                     too much recognition                  too little
                 -----------------------------         --------------------
   of non-self:  hypersensitivity / allergy             immunodeficiency
                 transplant rejection                   (infection, reactivation)
   of self:      autoimmunity                           tumour escape
                 checkpoint-blockade toxicity           graft tolerance (the goal)
```

Read hypersensitivity first: it defines the four mechanisms of damage that every other leaf reuses. Autoimmunity applies them to self and adds the genetics and triggers that break tolerance. Immunodeficiency is the mirror image, the diseases of absence, and it is also where the pattern of infections teaches what each component does. Transplant and tumour immunology closes the layer by showing the trade-off deliberately manipulated in both directions, with each field's success appearing as the other's complication.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Microbes and infection](../01-microbes-and-infection/README.md):** the opportunistic fungi, latent viruses and encapsulated bacteria of immunodeficiency are described there; molecular mimicry by streptococci and *Campylobacter* triggers autoimmunity; helminths are the proper target of the type 2 response that allergy misdirects; Epstein-Barr virus and papillomavirus are the cancers that immunosurveillance normally prevents.
- **[Innate immunity](../02-innate-immunity/README.md):** complement and phagocytes execute type II and III hypersensitivity; the Toll-like receptor and interferon loop sustains lupus; neutrophil and complement deficiencies have their own infection signatures; NK cells are the backstop against tumours that lose MHC.
- **[Adaptive immunity](../03-adaptive-immunity/README.md):** every leaf here is a failure of something there: of tolerance (autoimmunity, allergy), of T or B cell development (immunodeficiency), of MHC compatibility (rejection), of surveillance (cancer); live vaccines are contraindicated in T-cell deficiency.
