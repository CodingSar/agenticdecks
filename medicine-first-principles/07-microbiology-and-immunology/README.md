# 07 Microbiology and Immunology

> **Layer:** 07 Microbiology and Immunology
> **Children:** [Microbes and infection](01-microbes-and-infection/README.md) · [Innate immunity](02-innate-immunity/README.md) · [Adaptive immunity](03-adaptive-immunity/README.md) · [Immunity in disease](04-immunity-in-disease/README.md)

## What this area is about

This layer describes the boundary of the organism: the microbes that live on it, in it and against it, and the system that decides what to tolerate and what to destroy. It is placed as one layer because the immune system is defined by what it defends against. There is a receptor for lipopolysaccharide because Gram-negative bacteria have outer membranes; there are cytotoxic T cells because viruses replicate inside cells; there is Immunoglobulin E (IgE) because worms are too large to eat; there is a mechanism of tolerance because the gut holds 10^13 bacteria that must not be attacked.

The layer's structure follows one idea from four sides. **Innate immunity is pattern recognition with speed**: a few dozen inherited receptors for conserved microbial motifs, acting in minutes, unable to learn or to tell infection from injury. **Adaptive immunity is specificity with memory**: billions of randomly generated receptors, selected by antigen over one to two weeks, leaving behind cells that respond in hours for the rest of life. **Most immune disease is the cost of imperfect recognition** (allergy, autoimmunity, rejection) **or of its absence** (immunodeficiency, tumour escape). And underneath all three, **pathogenicity is a relationship**: whether a microbe causes disease depends on its weapons, its dose, its route and the state of the host, and much of the damage in infection is inflicted by the defence.

## Why this layer sits here

The layer needs [host-pathogen coevolution](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/02-evolution/host-pathogen-coevolution-and-resistance.md) and [natural selection](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/02-evolution/natural-selection-and-population-genetics.md) from [04 Genetics, Evolution and Development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md), because antibiotic resistance, antigenic variation, Major Histocompatibility Complex (MHC) polymorphism and affinity maturation are all selection, and because the immune system's design is the record of an arms race. It needs [cell signalling](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/04-cell-signalling/README.md) from [02 Biochemistry](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/README.md), since every pattern recognition receptor, cytokine receptor and antigen receptor is a receptor wired to a kinase cascade, and the eukaryotic cell plan of [03 Cell Biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md), against which bacteria and viruses are contrasted and whose proteasome, trafficking and apoptosis machinery antigen presentation and cytotoxic killing borrow. It needs the [vascular and lymphatic architecture](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/02-body-organization/vascular-and-lymphatic-architecture.md) and [blood](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/01-tissues/blood-and-haematopoiesis.md) of [05 Tissues and Anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md), because lymph nodes are where antigen meets lymphocytes, and from [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md) the [microcirculation](../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/microcirculation-and-capillary-exchange.md) (inflammation is a vascular event), [thermoregulation](../06-physiology/01-integrative-principles/thermoregulation-and-energy-balance.md) (fever), the [gut barrier](../06-physiology/06-gastrointestinal-system/the-gut-barrier-and-microbiome.md) and [skin](../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/the-skin-as-barrier-and-sensor.md) (the barriers), and the [placenta](../06-physiology/09-reproduction-and-the-life-course/pregnancy-placenta-and-lactation.md) (maternal antibody and fetal tolerance). From [01 Quantitative Foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md) it takes [exponential processes](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/compartment-models-and-exponential-processes.md) for growth, decay of antibody and transmission.

What the layers above need from it is **the concept of recognition and its consequences**: that the body carries a system which classifies molecules as self or non-self and safe or dangerous, acts on the classification with inflammation or killing, and is wrong in predictable ways. [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) is immunology seen in tissue: acute inflammation, granulomas, sepsis and the immune evasion of tumours. [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) targets the microbial structures described here with antimicrobials and the cytokines, cells and checkpoints with biologics. [10 Epidemiology](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) takes the chain of infection and herd immunity. [11 Clinical Method](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) reads antibody and culture. The specialties of [12](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) (infectious diseases, rheumatology and clinical immunology, haematology, oncology), [13](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) (sepsis in critical care) and [14](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) (childhood infection and vaccination) apply it, and [15](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) runs vaccination programmes and faces pandemics.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[04 Genetics, Evolution and Development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md):** resistance, virulence, MHC polymorphism and affinity maturation are selection; the inherited immunodeficiencies are Mendelian; autoimmunity is a complex trait; the fetus is the one allograft every mother tolerates.
- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** the body's barriers, the microcirculation that inflammation acts on, fever as a change of set point, the gut and its flora, the stress hormones that suppress immunity, and the placenta that passes antibody.
- **[08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md):** inflammation, granulomas, repair and sepsis are this layer's innate mechanisms seen in tissue; immune evasion is a hallmark of cancer; infection is one cause among several of cell injury.
- **[09 Pharmacology and Therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** antimicrobials exploit the structural differences catalogued in 01; anti-inflammatory drugs, immunosuppressants, cytokine antibodies, checkpoint inhibitors and cell therapies act on 02, 03 and 04; vaccines are the oldest biologic.
- **[10 Epidemiology and Evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md):** reproduction numbers, herd immunity, outbreak investigation and vaccine effectiveness turn the chain of infection into measurable population quantities.
- **[11 Clinical Method and Diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** culture, staining, sequencing, antigen detection and serology are how this layer's objects are observed in a patient; C-reactive protein and the neutrophil count read the acute-phase response.
- **[12 Organ-system Specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md):** infectious diseases, rheumatology and clinical immunology are this layer practised; haematology (transfusion, stem-cell transplantation), oncology (immunotherapy), pulmonology (asthma), gastroenterology (inflammatory bowel disease) and dermatology (eczema, psoriasis) each inherit one piece of it.
- **[15 Medicine in Society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md):** vaccination programmes, antimicrobial resistance and pandemics are the layer's population-scale consequences.

## Connections across layers

This layer is where the organism meets everything that is not itself. From below it takes receptors, selection, cells and vessels; to the layers above it hands the concepts of inflammation, recognition and tolerance, and the catalogue of agents that antimicrobials, vaccines and infection control are aimed at.
