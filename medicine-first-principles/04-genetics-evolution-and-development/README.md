# 04 Genetics, Evolution and Development

> **Layer:** 04 Genetics, Evolution and Development
> **Children:** [Inheritance](01-inheritance/README.md) · [Evolution](02-evolution/README.md) · [Development](03-development/README.md)

## What this area is about

Layer 03 described a cell with a repertoire. This layer explains how an organism made of such cells comes to be the way it is, from three directions. *Inheritance* asks what is passed from parent to child and from cell to cell: single genes with Mendel's arithmetic, chromosomes with their dosage, the genome's millions of small variants, and the epigenetic marks that annotate them. *Evolution* asks why the genome is what it is: because selection, drift and mutation acted on populations over generations, favouring reproduction in past environments and leaving the body with mismatches, trade-offs, constraints and enemies that evolve faster than it does. *Development* asks how the genome becomes a body: how position becomes fate in gradients of a few reused signals, how three layers fold into every organ during an eight-week window, and how the body then grows, matures and declines on a schedule selection set.

The unifying idea is that **the organism is a historical object**. Its genes were shaped by what reproduced before; its form was built by a programme that could only modify what already existed; its vulnerabilities are the record of both. Much of pathology looks like bad design until it is read as the shadow of adaptation: ageing as the price of early vigour and cancer suppression, obesity and diabetes as a thrifty metabolism meeting abundance, septic shock as a defence calibrated for a world without antibiotics, antibiotic resistance as the inevitable arithmetic of selection on a population of 10^12. This layer supplies the *why* that the mechanism-based layers above it otherwise lack, and it supplies the practical tools of genetic counselling, prenatal diagnosis, cancer genomics, pharmacogenomics and antimicrobial stewardship.

## Why this layer sits here

This layer needs [03 Cell Biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md) for [meiosis](../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/mitosis-meiosis-and-chromosome-segregation.md), which is the mechanism of Mendel's laws and of aneuploidy; for [differentiation and stem cells](../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/stem-cells-differentiation-and-regeneration.md), which development is made of; for [senescence](../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/senescence-and-telomeres.md) and [apoptosis](../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/cell-death-apoptosis-necrosis-and-autophagy.md), which are the model trade-offs and the sculptors of form; and for [adhesion](../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-adhesion-and-the-extracellular-matrix.md) and [cell communication](../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-to-cell-communication-and-junctions.md), which are how an embryo's cells move and decide. It needs [02 Biochemistry and Molecular Biology](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/README.md) for the mutation rate set by [replication and repair](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/03-genetic-information-flow/dna-replication-and-repair.md), for [gene regulation and chromatin](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/03-genetic-information-flow/gene-regulation-and-epigenetic-mechanisms.md), for the [receptors](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/04-cell-signalling/receptors-and-ligands.md) that read morphogens, and for the [sequencing techniques](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/03-genetic-information-flow/molecular-techniques.md) that read genomes. It needs [01 Quantitative and Scientific Foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md) more than any layer so far: [probability](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/01-probability-and-measurement/probability.md) for segregation and drift, [Bayes](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/01-probability-and-measurement/bayesian-reasoning.md) for counselling, [variance and regression](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/02-inference-and-causation/regression-adjustment-and-survival-analysis.md) for heritability, [causal inference](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/02-inference-and-causation/causal-inference.md) for Mendelian randomisation and teratology, and [exponential growth](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/compartment-models-and-exponential-processes.md) for microbial and tumour populations. From [00](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md) it takes [diffusion](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/diffusion-osmosis-and-transport.md) for gradients and [allometry](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/scale-and-allometry.md) for growth.

What the layers above need from it is **the organism as a product of history, with the risk arithmetic that follows**. [05 Tissues and Anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md) takes its tissue classification and its body plan from the germ layers. [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md) takes the reproductive axis, the placenta and the life course. [07 Microbiology and Immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md) takes the arms race as the frame for both pathogens and the immune system. [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) takes single-gene, chromosomal and multifactorial disease, cancer as somatic evolution, malformation as development disturbed, and ageing as trade-off. [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) takes pharmacogenomics, teratogenic risk and the evolutionary logic of combination antimicrobial and anticancer therapy. [10 Epidemiology](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) takes polygenic risk and Mendelian randomisation. The abstraction this layer contributes is **variation, selection and position**: the organism varies because inheritance is particulate and imperfect, it is shaped because variants reproduce unequally, and its form arises because cells read their position and their moment.

Read inheritance first: it supplies alleles, frequencies and modes of transmission that the other two assume. Evolution comes next because it explains the frequencies and introduces the trade-offs that development's final leaf (growth, puberty and ageing) relies on. Development is last; it uses imprinting and chromosomes from inheritance and constraints and life history from evolution, and it ends at the point where [05 tissues and anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md) begins.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[03 Cell Biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md):** supplies the mechanisms: meiosis for inheritance, differentiation and apoptosis for development, senescence for ageing. The boundary is scale and time: 03 describes one cell dividing or dying; 04 describes what a population of alleles or a sheet of cells does over generations or weeks.
- **[05 Tissues and Anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md):** is the output of development. Tissues are germ-layer derivatives; body cavities, dermatomes, the arrangement of the great vessels and the course of the recurrent laryngeal nerve are embryology and evolutionary history made solid.
- **[07 Microbiology and Immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md):** is the arms race described from both sides; the diversity of the immune repertoire and of the MHC, antigenic variation and resistance are evolutionary facts first.
- **[08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md):** [genetic, metabolic and environmental disease](../08-pathology/04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/README.md) catalogues inheritance's failures; [neoplasia](../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/README.md) is somatic mutation and clonal evolution reusing developmental pathways; [multifactorial disease](../08-pathology/05-disease-as-process/multifactorial-disease-and-risk.md) and the [diseases of ageing](../08-pathology/04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/diseases-of-aging-and-degeneration.md) are mismatch and trade-off.
- **[09 Pharmacology and Therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** [pharmacogenomics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/02-pharmacodynamics/pharmacogenomics-and-individual-variation.md), teratogenic [adverse reactions](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/02-pharmacodynamics/adverse-drug-reactions-and-pharmacovigilance.md), the combination logic of [antimicrobials](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/antimicrobials.md) and [anticancer drugs](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anticancer-drugs.md), and gene and cell therapies all rest here.
- **[10 Epidemiology and Evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md):** polygenic [risk prediction](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/risk-prediction-and-stratification.md), Mendelian randomisation as causal evidence, and pathogen evolution in [infectious disease dynamics](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/01-epidemiology/infectious-disease-dynamics.md).

## Connections across layers

This layer turns the cell's repertoire into an organism with a history and a risk profile. From below it takes meiosis, differentiation, signalling, mutation and the mathematics of probability and populations; to the layers above it gives the body plan, the rules of inheritance, the evolutionary "why" behind chronic disease and ageing, and the tools of genomic medicine. Three ideas introduced here recur at every higher level: that a risk is a probability computed from inheritance and exposure, that a population under pressure (microbes, tumour cells, humans) will evolve, and that a structure's vulnerabilities are explained by how and when it was built.
