## Essence

In the third week the two-layered disc rearranges into three layers by gastrulation, and in the following five weeks those layers fold, bud, migrate and hollow into every organ. Ectoderm gives skin and nervous system; mesoderm gives muscle, bone, blood, heart, kidney and gonad; endoderm gives the lining of gut and airways and their glands. By the end of week 8 the embryo is ~3 cm long and every organ is present in rudimentary form. The one idea is **a small repertoire of cell behaviours (migrate, fold, bud, branch, fuse, die) applied in sequence to sheets of cells** produces all anatomical structure, and because the same sequence runs in every vertebrate the body plan is conserved and its failures predictable.

## Core tenets

1. **Gastrulation makes three layers by migration through the primitive streak.** Epiblast cells at the streak lose E-cadherin ([adhesion](../../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-adhesion-and-the-extracellular-matrix.md)), ingress and spread as mesoderm or displace the hypoblast as endoderm; those that stay on the surface are ectoderm. Cells that stop at the midline form the notochord, which induces the nervous system ([patterning](morphogens-patterning-and-developmental-genes.md)).

2. **Ectoderm makes the nervous system by folding a plate into a tube, and failures of that fold are the commonest serious malformations.** The notochord induces the overlying ectoderm to form the neural plate, whose edges rise and fuse into the neural tube (days 22–28). Cells at the crest detach as neural crest, a migratory population forming peripheral nerves, melanocytes and most of the face. Failure of closure at the head is anencephaly, at the tail spina bifida; neural tube defects affect ~1 in 1,000 pregnancies and are cut by about 70% by folate taken around conception.

3. **Mesoderm is partitioned by distance from the midline, and each partition has a destiny.** Paraxial mesoderm segments into ~42 pairs of somites, each forming a vertebra, the muscles of one segment and the dermis over it; this is why spinal nerves repeat and a dermatome maps to a spinal level ([segmental innervation](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/02-body-organization/segmental-innervation-dermatomes-and-referred-pain.md)). Intermediate mesoderm forms kidney and gonad. Lateral plate mesoderm splits into a layer lining the body wall and one covering the gut, with the body cavity between ([body cavities](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/02-body-organization/body-cavities-fascia-and-compartments.md)).

4. **The heart forms first, folds and septates, and each step can fail.** Two endothelial tubes fuse into a beating tube by day 22, before any other organ works, because the embryo is now too large for diffusion ([scale](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/scale-and-allometry.md)). The tube loops, septa partition atria and ventricles, and a spiral septum divides the outflow into aorta and pulmonary trunk. Congenital heart disease (~8 per 1,000 births, the commonest major malformation) is this sequence interrupted: septal defects, tetralogy of Fallot and transposition when the outflow septum misaligns.

5. **Endoderm forms the gut tube and everything that buds from it.** Folding wraps the endoderm into a tube; along its length, patches bud outward in response to mesodermal signals: thyroid, pharyngeal pouches, lung, liver, pancreas. Each bud branches under reciprocal induction between epithelium and mesenchyme: the airway tree completes ~23 generations, the kidney ~10^6 nephrons. Failed recanalisation gives atresia; the yolk stalk persists in ~2% of people as a Meckel's diverticulum.

6. **Organogenesis is a window, and the time of an insult predicts the malformation.** Each organ has a critical period when its primordium forms, mostly weeks 3–8: heart days 20–50, neural tube 22–28, limbs 24–36. An insult before week 3 usually kills the embryo or is repaired; one after week 8 slows growth or damages function but does not alter gross form ([teratogens](congenital-malformations-and-teratogens.md)). This is why the first trimester is the period of teratogenic risk.

7. **Where a tissue comes from predicts what tumours it forms.** Carcinomas arise from epithelia; sarcomas from mesodermal connective tissue; gliomas and neuroblastomas from neural ectoderm and crest; germ cell tumours from primordial germ cells, which migrate from yolk sac to gonad and occasionally stop en route, which is why teratomas occur in the midline. Tumours keep their lineage markers, which is how an unknown primary is traced ([tumour classification](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/tumour-classification-grading-and-staging.md)).

8. **The body plan is deeply conserved, which makes the embryo an evolutionary document and animal models valid.** Every vertebrate passes through a stage with pharyngeal arches, a notochord and a tail. Human arches become jaw, ear bones and larynx; the arch arteries explain the looping recurrent laryngeal nerve. The same genes build the same structures across vertebrates ([developmental genes](morphogens-patterning-and-developmental-genes.md)), so a mouse mutation predicts a human malformation, and the detours are the constraints of [evolutionary medicine](../02-evolution/evolutionary-medicine.md).

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Gastrulation | Formation of three germ layers by ingression through the primitive streak |
| Ectoderm / mesoderm / endoderm | Outer, middle and inner germ layers and their derivatives |
| Notochord | Midline rod that organises the axis and induces the neural plate |
| Neurulation / neural crest | Folding of the neural plate into a tube / migratory cells released from its crest |
| Somite | Segmental block of paraxial mesoderm forming vertebra, muscle and dermis |
| Critical period | The window during which an organ's form can be disrupted |
| Pharyngeal arches | Segmental neck structures forming face, jaw, ear, larynx and great vessels |

## Common misconceptions

- **"The embryo develops by growing bigger."** During organogenesis form comes from rearrangement and folding; the embryo is ~3 cm at the end of it.
- **"Each germ layer makes a separate set of organs."** Nearly every organ is a collaboration (endodermal epithelium with mesodermal stroma in the lung), which is why malformations cluster.
