# 05 Tissues and Anatomy

> **Layer:** 05 Tissues and Anatomy
> **Children:** [Tissues](01-tissues/README.md) · [Body organization](02-body-organization/README.md) · [Regional anatomy](03-regional-anatomy/README.md)

## What this area is about

This layer is the body as a built thing: the four tissues it is made of, the rules by which they are packaged, supplied, wired and loaded, and the particular arrangement of them in each region. It sits between the cell and the working organism: below it, cells have junctions, matrix, motors and excitable membranes; above it, physiology describes organs pumping, filtering and signalling. Anatomy says what shape those organs have, what they lie against and how they are connected, and so what they can and cannot do.

The governing idea is that **structure constrains function**. The heart can only pump because the valves open one way and the fibrous ring insulates atria from ventricles; the lung can only ventilate because a sealed pleural film couples it to a moving wall; the gut's pain can only be felt in the midline because its afferents enter the cord with the skin's. Run backwards, the same idea gives the layer's second theme: **every breach of a structural relation is a disease**. Compartment syndrome is a fascial envelope that cannot stretch; portal hypertension is two capillary beds in series when the second clogs; a watershed infarct is the border between two arterial trees at low pressure; a hernia is a wall with a hole; referred pain is a shared dorsal-horn neuron; the hoarse voice of a lung cancer is a nerve that loops under the aorta because its artery did in the embryo. The layer reads anatomy for what it lets the body do and what goes wrong when it is breached, not as a catalogue.

## Why this layer sits here

From below, the layer takes its materials and its plan. [03 Cell Biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md) supplies the [junctions](../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-to-cell-communication-and-junctions.md), [adhesion and matrix](../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-adhesion-and-the-extracellular-matrix.md), [cytoskeletal motors](../03-cell-biology/01-cell-structure/the-cytoskeleton-and-cell-motility.md) and [stem-cell hierarchies](../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/stem-cells-differentiation-and-regeneration.md) from which tissues are assembled. [04 Genetics, Evolution and Development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md) supplies the body plan: the [germ layers, somites, coelom and limb buds](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/03-development/germ-layers-and-organogenesis.md) and the [patterning genes](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/03-development/morphogens-patterning-and-developmental-genes.md) that explain why organs lie where they do and why the odd relations (the recurrent laryngeal nerve, the inguinal canal, the spiralling dermatomes) exist at all. [00 Physical and Chemical Foundations](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md) supplies the physics: [pressure and flow](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/fluids-pressure-and-flow.md) for compartments and vessels, [mechanics of materials](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/mechanics-and-materials.md) for bone and tendon, [diffusion](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/diffusion-osmosis-and-transport.md) for the thickness of epithelia and the spacing of vessels.

From above, every layer that describes the body working or failing assumes this one: [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md) cannot state the cardiac cycle without the valves or ventilation without the pleural coupling; [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) maps infarction onto arterial territories; [11 Clinical Method and Diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) examines and images the body in the planes defined here; [13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) acts on this anatomy with knives, needles and tubes.

The one abstraction the layer contributes to the stack is **the relation**: a statement of the form "A lies against B, inside C, supplied by D, innervated by segment E", from which the consequences of pressure, occlusion, inflammation, injury and surgery can be predicted before they are observed.

Read the tissues first: each leaf ends with the diseases its design makes inevitable. Read body organization next; its five leaves are the rules. Read the regions last, as applications: a reader who has the rules should predict most of what each region contains.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[00 Physical and Chemical Foundations](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md):** the pressure–volume curve of a closed compartment, the fourth-power law of flow, the stress–strain behaviour of bone and the diffusion limit are taken from here and applied without re-derivation.
- **[03 Cell Biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md):** tissues are cells in community; polarity, junctions, matrix and the stem-cell compartments of epithelium, marrow and muscle are the cell-biological facts that this layer arranges into organs.
- **[04 Genetics, Evolution and Development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md):** the embryo explains nearly every strange relation in regional anatomy, and evolutionary legacy (bipedal loading, a retained segmental plan) is why the design has its compromises.
- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** the direct consumer; each system's physiology is a quantitative account of a structure described here, stated once before the function that uses it.
- **[08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md):** general pathology's [infarction](../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md), [oedema](../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/edema-and-fluid-imbalance.md), [repair and fibrosis](../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/tissue-repair-regeneration-and-fibrosis.md) and [tumour classification](../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/tumour-classification-grading-and-staging.md) are each stated tissue by tissue and territory by territory.
- **[11 Clinical Method and Diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** examination is applied surface anatomy and segmental innervation; imaging displays the body in the planes and compartments defined here; histopathology reads the tissues.
- **[13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md):** surgery, anaesthesia, trauma and emergency care act on breached compartments, torn vessels, compressed nerves and failed levers; the airway, the acute abdomen and orthopaedics are regional anatomy under time pressure.
- **[12 Organ-system specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md):** stroke is the cerebral arterial map, ischaemic heart disease the coronary map, liver disease the portal vein, and the specialties cite these relations rather than restating them.

## Connections across layers

This is the first layer at which the body has a shape; it converts the physics and cell biology below into the vocabulary of organs, cavities, vessels and segments. Upward it is cited more than extended: almost no higher leaf adds anatomy, but nearly every one assumes it.
