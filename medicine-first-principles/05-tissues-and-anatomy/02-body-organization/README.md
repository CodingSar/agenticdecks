## What this area is about

Between the tissues and the regions lies a set of organising principles that hold for the whole body: a coordinate system for describing it, a scheme of closed compartments that packages it, a vascular tree and lymphatic drain that supply and clear every part of it, a segmental wiring diagram inherited from the embryo, and a mechanical design of levers and bearings. None of these belongs to one region. The compartment rule that explains tamponade in the chest explains compartment syndrome in the leg; the watershed that infarcts the splenic flexure infarcts the cerebral cortex; the segmental map that puts cardiac pain in the arm puts diaphragmatic pain in the shoulder.

The unifying idea is that **the body's plan is a small set of rules applied everywhere**. Learn the rules once and regional anatomy becomes a list of instances; learn the regions without the rules and anatomy is a thousand unconnected facts. These five leaves are the rules.

Read the language leaf first; the other four are written in its vocabulary. Compartments and vessels form a pair: vessels run in the septa between compartments, and a compartment kills its contents by squeezing their capillaries. Segmental innervation is read next because it is needed for every clinical examination and for the two kinds of pain that the serous membranes produce. Biomechanics comes last because it uses all the tissues and all the vocabulary and leads directly into the regional leaves on the limbs and spine.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Tissues](../01-tissues/README.md):** supplies the materials. Deep fascia and the serous membranes are connective tissue and epithelium; the vessel wall is endothelium, smooth muscle and elastin; dermatomes are nervous tissue following somitic mesoderm; the stress–strain behaviour of bone, tendon and cartilage is what biomechanics calculates with.
- **[Regional anatomy](../03-regional-anatomy/README.md):** applies the rules. The skull as a closed box, the pericardium, the inguinal canal, the forearm and leg compartments, the portal anastomoses, the coronary end arteries, the cord–vertebra mismatch and the hip's lever system each appear there as a worked instance of a principle stated here.
