## What this area is about

The sense organs are small, anatomically dense and functionally precious: a few cubic centimetres of tissue carry sight, hearing, balance, smell, voice, swallowing and the upper airway. Ophthalmology and Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT) surgery are the specialties built around them, and they sit in this layer rather than in [12](../../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) for two reasons. They are predominantly surgical and device-based: cataract extraction is the most frequently performed operation in the world, and hearing is restored by machines (hearing aids, cochlear implants) more often than by drugs. And their emergencies are time-critical in the same way as the rest of this layer: an occluded retinal artery, an acute angle-closure glaucoma, a retinal detachment, a sudden sensorineural hearing loss and an obstructed upper airway each have a clock measured in minutes to hours.

The unifying idea is **physics applied to a transducer**. The eye is an optical instrument whose failures are refractive (the image falls in the wrong place), transmissive (the media are opaque), pressure-related (the fluid system that keeps its shape damages the nerve) or neural (the retina or optic nerve fails). The ear is an acoustic impedance-matcher feeding a frequency analyser, and its failures are conductive or sensorineural. The nose and throat are the shared entrance to the respiratory and digestive tracts, where a narrow, collapsible tube makes obstruction the common emergency. In each organ the clinician reasons from the physics of the signal to the site of the fault, and most of the treatments are structural: a lens, a laser, a drain, a prosthesis, a tube.

Read the eye first: it is the cleanest example of physics-to-fault reasoning and introduces the pressure-in-a-closed-space problem that recurs in the ear and in [neurosurgery](../01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md). ## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Surgery](../01-surgery/README.md):** both are surgical subspecialties by origin; head and neck cancer surgery, facial trauma and skull-base work are shared with plastic surgery, maxillofacial surgery and neurosurgery ([surgical subspecialties](../01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md)).
- **[Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md):** the shared airway. ENT operates in the space the anaesthetist needs; the obstructed upper airway, the tracheostomy and the front-of-neck rescue are joint territory ([airway management](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/airway-and-ventilation-management.md)). Eye surgery is a large user of regional and topical anaesthesia.
- **[Critical care](../03-critical-care/README.md):** ENT performs the tracheostomy that weans a ventilated patient and manages the airway complications of a long stay.
- **[Emergency medicine](../04-emergency-medicine/README.md):** the red eye, sudden vision loss, the chemical splash, epistaxis, the foreign body, the peritonsillar abscess and the stridulous child all arrive at the emergency department first ([triage](../04-emergency-medicine/triage-and-the-abcde-approach.md)).

## Interfaces with other specialties

- **[Endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md):** [diabetic](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/diabetes-mellitus.md) retinopathy is a leading cause of blindness in working-age adults and the reason for retinal screening; [thyroid eye disease](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/thyroid-disease.md) threatens the optic nerve; thyroid and parathyroid surgery is done by ENT and endocrine surgeons.
- **[Neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md):** the eye is the visible part of the brain: optic neuritis in [demyelinating disease](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/demyelinating-and-neuromuscular-disease.md), papilloedema, visual field loss in [stroke](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/stroke.md), and the shared problem of distinguishing vestibular from central vertigo.
- **[Cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md) and [hypertension](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/hypertension-and-vascular-disease.md):** retinal artery and vein occlusions are vascular events of the eye; the fundus shows the state of the arterioles; epistaxis is worse in the hypertensive and the anticoagulated.
- **[Rheumatology and clinical immunology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** uveitis, scleritis and giant cell arteritis ([vasculitis](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/connective-tissue-disease-and-vasculitis.md)), which blinds within days if untreated; [allergic](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/allergy-and-immunodeficiency-in-practice.md) rhinitis and conjunctivitis.
- **[Infectious diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** orbital cellulitis, endophthalmitis, otitis and mastoiditis, deep neck space infections and the [tropical](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/tuberculosis-and-tropical-infections.md) causes of blindness (trachoma, onchocerciasis).
- **[Oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/README.md):** head and neck cancer is managed by a multidisciplinary team of ENT, oncology, [radiotherapy](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/radiotherapy-and-radiation-biology.md) and plastic surgery; ocular melanoma and retinoblastoma are ophthalmology's cancers.
- **[Dermatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/11-dermatology/README.md):** [skin cancers](../../12-organ-system-specialties/11-dermatology/skin-cancer-and-skin-infections.md) of the eyelid, ear and face are shared with both specialties.
- **[Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md):** amblyopia and squint must be treated in the critical period of visual development; otitis media and glue ear are among the commonest [childhood illnesses](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/common-childhood-illness.md); newborn hearing screening and the stridulous child are shared.
- **[Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** visual and hearing loss are [geriatric syndromes](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/geriatric-syndromes.md) that cause falls, isolation and accelerate cognitive decline; cataract surgery and hearing aids are among the highest-value interventions in older people.
- **[Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** sees most ear, nose, throat and eye complaints and must recognise the few that are emergencies; [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md) of sensory loss and [occupational medicine](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/sports-occupational-and-lifestyle-medicine.md) for noise-induced hearing loss.

## Connections across layers

These specialties take their physics from [00](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md) ([optics and acoustics](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/waves-sound-light-and-radiation.md), [pressure](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/fluids-pressure-and-flow.md)), their transduction from [06](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/sensory-systems.md), their anatomy from [05](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/03-regional-anatomy/head-neck-and-spine.md), and their devices from [09](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/devices-implants-and-biomaterials.md). They give upward the clearest examples of [screening](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/screening-principles-and-biases.md) that works (diabetic retinopathy, newborn hearing), and to [15](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) the global burden of avoidable blindness and deafness (over a billion people with vision impairment, about half avoidable, World Health Organization, 2019) as a measure of health system failure.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Structure determines function](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#structure-determines-function): the eye's optics and the ear's mechanics are geometry; most treatments change the geometry.
- [Gradients and flow](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#gradients-and-flow): aqueous humour production against drainage sets intraocular pressure; endolymph pressure sets Ménière's disease; airflow through a narrow tube sets stridor.
- [Signalling and information](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#signalling-and-information): the retina and cochlea encode a signal; loss is read as a fault in encoding, transmission or decoding.
- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): retinal ischaemia blinds in hours, glaucoma over decades; the clock decides the specialty's emergencies.
- [Barriers and compartments](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#barriers-and-compartments): the globe, the orbit and the middle ear are closed spaces where pressure and infection behave as they do in the skull.
- [Scale and surface to volume](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#scale-and-surface-to-volume): the child's airway is narrow, so the same oedema that is trivial in an adult obstructs it.
