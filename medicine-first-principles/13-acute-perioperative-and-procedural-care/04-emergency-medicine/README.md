## What this area is about

Emergency medicine is defined not by an organ or a disease but by a time window and a state of ignorance. The patient arrives undifferentiated: a collapse, a pain, a breathlessness, with no diagnosis, often no history, and a clock that may be running in seconds. The question this area answers is **how do you keep someone alive and sort them correctly when you do not yet know what is wrong?** Its answer inverts the usual order of [clinical method](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md): treat the failing physiology first, in a fixed order, and let the diagnosis follow.

The unifying idea is **triage of time-critical physiology**. Every patient is sorted twice: between patients, by how soon they will die without intervention (triage), and within a patient, by which failure kills first (airway before breathing before circulation). The ABCDE sequence is a priority queue built from the time constants of hypoxia, exsanguination and brain injury. Cardiac arrest is the limit case in which every clock has run out at once; poisoning is the case in which the insult is a dose; and prehospital and disaster medicine apply the same sorting with fewer hands, less information and more patients than the system can serve.

```
   many undifferentiated patients ---triage---> order of attention
                                                     |
   one patient: A -> B -> C -> D -> E   (fix each as found, then reassess)
                 |            |
          no output: resuscitation (02)      a dose: poisoning (03)
                                                     |
   outside the walls, or more patients than hands: prehospital and disaster (04)
```

Read triage first; it is the method every other leaf uses. Cardiac arrest is the ABCDE sequence with all three of A, B and C failed. Poisoning shows the method applied to a cause with a known mechanism and kinetics. Prehospital and disaster medicine is the method stripped of the hospital.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Surgery](../01-surgery/README.md):** the emergency department is the front door for [the acute abdomen](../01-surgery/the-acute-abdomen.md) and [trauma](../01-surgery/trauma-and-haemorrhage-control.md); the primary survey decides who goes to theatre without a scan.
- **[Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md):** shares the [airway](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/airway-and-ventilation-management.md), procedural sedation and the emergency induction of the unfasted, unassessed patient.
- **[Critical care](../03-critical-care/README.md):** receives the resuscitated patient; the first hour of [shock](../03-critical-care/shock-and-haemodynamic-support.md) and [sepsis](../03-critical-care/sepsis-and-multi-organ-failure.md) happens in the emergency department, and post-arrest care is shared.
- **[Ophthalmology and Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT)](../05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md):** the painful red eye, sudden vision loss, epistaxis, the foreign body and the obstructed upper airway all present first to emergency medicine.

## Interfaces with other specialties

- **[Cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md):** chest pain is the commonest high-stakes presentation; the time from door to reperfusion in [myocardial infarction](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/ischemic-heart-disease.md) is a shared metric, and [arrhythmia](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/arrhythmias.md) is the mechanism of most arrests.
- **[Neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md):** [stroke](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/stroke.md) thrombolysis and thrombectomy are a race run jointly; [status epilepticus](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/epilepsy.md), [headache](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/headache.md) with a dangerous cause, and neurological prognostication after cardiac arrest.
- **[Pulmonology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/README.md):** acute [asthma and exacerbated chronic obstructive pulmonary disease](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/asthma-and-copd.md), [pneumonia](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/pneumonia-and-respiratory-infections.md) and pulmonary embolism.
- **[Infectious diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** [sepsis](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/fever-and-sepsis.md) recognition and the first antimicrobial; [infection control](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/infection-control-and-healthcare-associated-infection.md) at the door during outbreaks.
- **[Gastroenterology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/acid-peptic-disease-and-gi-bleeding.md) resuscitated in the department and scoped by the gastroenterologist.
- **[Endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md):** diabetic ketoacidosis, hypoglycaemia, adrenal and thyroid crises ([diabetes](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/diabetes-mellitus.md)).
- **[Nephrology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md):** hyperkalaemia and the [electrolyte emergencies](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/electrolyte-and-acid-base-disorders.md); dialysis for the poisoned.
- **[Haematology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md):** the anticoagulated bleeder, massive [transfusion](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/transfusion-medicine.md), sickle crisis, neutropenic sepsis.
- **[Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md):** a quarter or more of emergency attendances are children; [common childhood illness](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/common-childhood-illness.md), the febrile infant and [safeguarding](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/adolescent-health-and-safeguarding.md) are shared duties.
- **[Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md):** the pregnant woman with pain, bleeding or collapse; ectopic pregnancy and the emergency delivery.
- **[Psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md):** self-harm and overdose ([addiction and substance use](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/addiction-and-substance-use.md)), acute psychosis, the agitated patient and the assessment of risk and capacity after medical stabilisation.
- **[Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** falls, delirium and [frailty](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/frailty-multimorbidity-and-polypharmacy.md) are the commonest reasons an older person attends, and the decision about ceilings of treatment is often first made at the door.
- **[Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** the two share the undifferentiated patient and dispute the border; who should attend where is a system question ([how the specialties interact](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/how-the-specialties-interact.md)).

## Connections across layers

Emergency medicine takes from [06](../../06-physiology/README.md) the time constants of hypoxia, haemorrhage and cerebral ischaemia; from [08](../../08-pathology/README.md) the mechanisms of [shock](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/hemorrhage-shock-and-sepsis.md) and [infarction](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md); from [09](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) [toxicology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/05-drug-development-and-toxicology/toxicology-and-poisoning.md) and the resuscitation drugs; from [10](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) and [11](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) the [decision rules](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/risk-prediction-and-stratification.md) and [test thresholds](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/using-tests-pretest-probability-and-thresholds.md) that let it discharge safely. It gives to [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) the resuscitation of the newborn, the child and the frail, and to [15](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) the department as the barometer of a health system, the ethics of treating without consent, and disaster triage as [priority setting](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/health-economics-and-priority-setting.md) in its rawest form.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): the whole specialty is ordered by how soon each failure kills.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): decisions on incomplete information; discharge is a bet on pre-test probability.
- [Supply and demand](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): oxygen delivery in the patient, and beds and staff in the department.
- [Dose–response](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#dose-response): poisoning is the dose–response curve followed past its therapeutic range.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): triage is the search for compensation about to fail.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): resuscitating a patient who would not have wanted it is a harm; disaster triage accepts individual harm for collective benefit.
