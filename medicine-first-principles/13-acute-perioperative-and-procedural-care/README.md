# 13 Acute, Perioperative and Procedural Care

> **Layer:** 13 Acute, Perioperative and Procedural Care
> **Children:** [Surgery](01-surgery/README.md) · [Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) · [Critical care](03-critical-care/README.md) · [Emergency medicine](04-emergency-medicine/README.md) · [Ophthalmology and ENT](05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md)

## What this area is about

The organ-system specialties of layer 12 are organised by *which* system fails. This layer is organised by *how fast* it fails and by *what is done with the hands*. Its five branches share one subject: **physiology under stress, measured against time**. A ruptured spleen, an obstructed airway, a septic patient and an acute angle-closure glaucoma are different diseases in different organs, but each has a clock, and in each the clinician's job is to buy time by taking over a function the body can no longer perform, then to hand that function back.

The unifying idea is **supply and demand on a deadline**. Cells need oxygen and substrate delivered at the rate they consume them. Injury, infection, haemorrhage and obstruction break the delivery chain somewhere between the air and the mitochondrion, and the tempo of the break decides the discipline: seconds (airway, arrest) belong to emergency medicine and anaesthesia; hours (haemorrhage, the acute abdomen) to surgery; days (sepsis, multi-organ failure) to critical care. Surgery itself is a *controlled injury*: it deliberately provokes the wound-healing and stress responses described in [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) and [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md), and anaesthesia exists so that the body tolerates that injury without experiencing it. The sense organs sit here because their specialties are built around procedures and devices, and because their emergencies (vision loss, airway obstruction) are also races against time.

## Why this layer sits here

Everything here is applied physiology at speed. Shock is [haemodynamics](../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/hemodynamics-and-blood-pressure-regulation.md) and [capillary exchange](../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/microcirculation-and-capillary-exchange.md) failing; respiratory failure is [gas exchange](../06-physiology/04-respiratory-system/gas-exchange-and-transport.md) and [ventilation–perfusion matching](../06-physiology/04-respiratory-system/ventilation-perfusion-matching.md) failing; the stress response is the [adrenal axis](../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/thyroid-adrenal-and-stress-hormones.md) and [autonomic nervous system](../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/the-autonomic-nervous-system.md) at full output. The mechanisms of injury come from [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md), above all [haemorrhage, shock and sepsis](../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/hemorrhage-shock-and-sepsis.md), [acute inflammation](../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/acute-inflammation.md) and [repair](../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/tissue-repair-regeneration-and-fibrosis.md). The tools come from [09 Pharmacology and Therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md): [anaesthetic and analgesic drugs](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/central-nervous-system-drugs.md), [vasoactive drugs](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/autonomic-and-cardiovascular-drugs.md), [fluids and blood products](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/fluids-nutrition-and-blood-products.md), [the principles of surgery and wound care](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/principles-of-surgery-and-wound-care.md) and [devices](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/devices-implants-and-biomaterials.md). The way of looking comes from [11 Clinical Method and Diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md), compressed: bedside [ultrasound](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/ultrasound.md), [computed tomography](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/radiography-and-computed-tomography.md), arterial blood gases and lactate.

It sits above [12 Organ-system Specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) because a resuscitation is cardiology, pulmonology, nephrology and haematology performed simultaneously and quickly, and because the acute patient is handed back to those specialties once the clock has been stopped. What the layers above need from it: [14 Life Course, Mind and Integrative Care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) applies the same resuscitation, anaesthesia and surgery to the newborn, the labouring woman and the frail older adult, and asks when intensive treatment stops serving the patient; [15 Medicine in Society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) sees here the costliest beds, the sharpest consent and capacity dilemmas, and the clearest examples of systems engineering (checklists, trauma networks, disaster triage). The abstraction this layer contributes is **organ support as a bridge**: a machine or drug replaces a function temporarily, and the whole art is deciding when the bridge is needed, what it costs, and when the body can take the load back.

```
                      time since insult
   seconds ---------- minutes ---------- hours ---------- days ---------- weeks
   airway, arrest     triage, ABCDE      haemorrhage      sepsis, MOF     rehabilitation
   (04, 02)           (04)               acute abdomen    ventilation     hand-back to 12/14
                                         (01)             (03)
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   anaesthesia (02) makes the operation (01) tolerable; critical care (03) carries
   the patient through the aftermath; the sense organs (05) run the same loop in
```

Read surgery first, because the surgical decision (benefit of the operation against the cost of the injury it inflicts) frames everything else. Anaesthesia follows: it is what makes the decision executable. Critical care is the aftermath of both and of medical catastrophes, and introduces the organ-support abstraction formally. Emergency medicine is read fourth because its content is the first minutes of every other child; it is placed after them so that the reader knows what the emergency physician is triaging *towards*. Ophthalmology and ENT close the layer as self-contained procedure-centred specialties that reuse the same anaesthetic, surgical and emergency logic on small, precise organs.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** the layer is physiology monitored in real time: arterial pressure, oxygen saturation, end-tidal carbon dioxide, urine output and lactate are the controlled variables of [homeostasis](../06-physiology/01-integrative-principles/homeostasis-and-set-points.md) read off a screen.
- **[08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md):** shock, sepsis, haemorrhage, infarction, acute inflammation and wound repair are taught there once and applied here on every patient.
- **[09 Pharmacology and Therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** anaesthesia is pharmacokinetics in minutes rather than days; critical care is drug therapy with organ clearance changing hourly; surgery and devices are the non-drug half of that layer.
- **[11 Clinical Method and Diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** the acute patient inverts the usual order: treat the physiology before the diagnosis is known, then use tests to narrow it. [Pre-test probability](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/using-tests-pretest-probability-and-thresholds.md) still rules which test is worth the time.
- **[12 Organ-system Specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md):** the source of the patients (a bleeding ulcer, a myocardial infarction, a pneumonia) and their destination after the acute phase; [cardiology](../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md), [pulmonology](../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/README.md), [nephrology](../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md) and [haematology](../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md) are consulted hourly in an intensive care unit.
- **[14 Life Course, Mind and Integrative Care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md):** [obstetrics](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) and [paediatrics](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) have their own anaesthesia, surgery and resuscitation; [geriatrics and palliative care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) decide with critical care when organ support has stopped being a bridge; [rehabilitation](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md) is where survivors of trauma and intensive care go next.
- **[15 Medicine in Society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md):** [patient safety](../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/patient-safety-and-quality-improvement.md) was born in anaesthesia and the operating theatre; [consent and capacity](../15-medicine-in-society/02-ethics-law-and-the-profession/consent-capacity-and-confidentiality.md) are tested hardest in the unconscious patient; disaster triage is [resource allocation](../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/health-economics-and-priority-setting.md) made explicit.

## Connections across layers

From below this layer takes the physiology of oxygen delivery, the pathology of injury and the pharmacology of reversible control; it adds the clock. To the layers above it gives the idea that a function can be replaced by a machine or a drug for a time, and that the decision to start, continue and stop that replacement is a clinical, ethical and economic one. The same loop (recognise the failing variable, support it, treat the cause, withdraw the support) reappears in neonatal intensive care, in obstetric haemorrhage and in the hospice decision not to start it.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Supply and demand](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): oxygen delivery against consumption is the one equation behind shock, respiratory failure, the surgical stress response and triage.
- [Time courses](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): the layer is organised by tempo; the same haemorrhage is survivable or fatal depending on the hour.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): a normal blood pressure in a bleeding young adult is compensation, not safety; the fall, when it comes, is sudden.
- [Injury and repair](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#injury-and-repair): surgery deliberately wounds and relies on repair; trauma and sepsis are the same response running out of control.
- [Gradients and flow](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#gradients-and-flow): pressure gradients drive ventilation, perfusion, intracranial and intraocular physiology, and every tube, drain and pump in the unit.
- [Harm and benefit](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): every operation, anaesthetic and intensive care admission is an injury accepted for a greater gain, and the gain is not always there.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): triage, perioperative risk scores and prognosis after cardiac arrest are decisions made before the facts are in.
- [Redundancy and reserve](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#redundancy-and-reserve): functional reserve decides who tolerates an operation or an intensive care stay; frailty is its absence.
