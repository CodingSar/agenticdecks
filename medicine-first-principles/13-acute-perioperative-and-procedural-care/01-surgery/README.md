## What this area is about

Surgery treats disease by changing anatomy: removing what is diseased, repairing what is broken, draining what is contained, bypassing what is blocked, replacing what is lost. The price is that every operation is itself an injury. An incision triggers the same [acute inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/acute-inflammation.md), [repair](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/tissue-repair-regeneration-and-fibrosis.md) and [neuroendocrine stress response](../../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/thyroid-adrenal-and-stress-hormones.md) as a stab wound, differing only in that it is sterile, planned and limited. The question this area answers is therefore always the same: **does the anatomical change gain more than the injury costs, for this patient, now?**

The unifying idea is **controlled injury against a clock**. Elective surgery controls the injury (preparation, sterility, haemostasis, anaesthesia) and chooses the moment. Emergency surgery cannot choose the moment: in the acute abdomen and in trauma, the disease is running its own clock (ischaemia of bowel, exsanguination, peritoneal contamination), and the surgeon's decision is when the injury of operating has become cheaper than the injury of waiting.

Read the surgical decision first; it supplies the cost side of every later equation. The acute abdomen and trauma are the two emergency patterns, one driven by contamination and ischaemia from within, the other by energy from without. Orthopaedics spans both (a hip fracture is trauma in a frail body; a joint replacement is elective) and introduces the material science of bone. The subspecialty map comes last so that the reader can place vascular, cardiothoracic, neurological and transplant surgery against the principles rather than learning them as a list.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md):** the two are one team. The anaesthetist's [preoperative risk assessment](../02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/perioperative-risk-and-complications.md) is half of the surgical decision, and most deaths after surgery are medical (myocardial infarction, pneumonia, thromboembolism) rather than technical.
- **[Critical care](../03-critical-care/README.md):** receives the patient after major or emergency surgery; [damage-control surgery](trauma-and-haemorrhage-control.md) exists because the intensive care unit can correct physiology better than the operating theatre can.
- **[Emergency medicine](../04-emergency-medicine/README.md):** the front door for the acute abdomen and for trauma; the [primary survey](../04-emergency-medicine/triage-and-the-abcde-approach.md) decides who goes straight to theatre.
- **[Ophthalmology and ENT](../05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md):** surgical specialties in their own right, built on the same wound-healing and anaesthetic principles applied to small, functionally dense organs.

## Interfaces with other specialties

- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** shares the acute abdomen and [gastrointestinal bleeding](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/acid-peptic-disease-and-gi-bleeding.md); the border is drawn by what endoscopy and interventional radiology can do without an incision. [Pancreatitis and biliary disease](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/pancreatic-and-biliary-disease.md) are managed medically until obstruction, necrosis or a gallbladder needs removal.
- **[Oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/README.md):** most solid tumours are cured, if at all, by resection; the multidisciplinary tumour board decides the sequence of surgery, [systemic therapy](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/systemic-anticancer-therapy.md) and [radiotherapy](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/radiotherapy-and-radiation-biology.md) from the [stage](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/tumour-classification-grading-and-staging.md).
- **[Cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md):** refers for coronary bypass and [valve surgery](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md) and in turn clears patients for non-cardiac surgery; percutaneous techniques keep moving the border.
- **[Haematology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md):** [transfusion medicine](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/transfusion-medicine.md) runs the massive transfusion that trauma depends on, and [bleeding and clotting disorders](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/bleeding-and-clotting-disorders.md) decide who can safely be cut and who needs anticoagulation afterwards.
- **[Neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md) and neurosurgery:** [stroke](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/stroke.md) is a shared emergency (thrombectomy, decompressive craniectomy, carotid surgery); head injury in trauma pulls neurosurgery in from the first minutes.
- **[Rheumatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** [osteoarthritis](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/osteoarthritis-gout-and-regional-pain.md) and [inflammatory arthritis](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/inflammatory-arthritis.md) reach orthopaedics when a joint is destroyed; a hot joint is a shared emergency (septic arthritis versus crystal arthritis).
- **[Infectious diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** source control is the surgical half of sepsis treatment; surgical site infection and [healthcare-associated infection](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/infection-control-and-healthcare-associated-infection.md) are the shared failure.
- **[Obstetrics and gynaecology](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md):** a surgical specialty in its own right; the acute abdomen in a woman of reproductive age is shared (ectopic pregnancy, ovarian torsion) and caesarean section is the commonest major operation worldwide.
- **[Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) and [geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** the same operations on bodies with different reserve; the hip fracture pathway (orthopaedics plus geriatrics plus anaesthesia) is the model joint service, and [frailty](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/frailty-multimorbidity-and-polypharmacy.md) is the best predictor that an operation will harm.
- **[Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** refers the hernia, the gallstone and the suspicious lump, and receives the patient back for wound care and the [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md) that decides whether the operation achieved its purpose.

## Connections across layers

Surgery takes its anatomy from [05](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md) (planes, fascia and [compartments](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/02-body-organization/body-cavities-fascia-and-compartments.md) are what a surgeon dissects along), its physiology of bleeding and stress from [06](../../06-physiology/README.md), its injury and repair from [08](../../08-pathology/README.md), and its craft principles (asepsis, haemostasis, the wound) from [09](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/principles-of-surgery-and-wound-care.md). It gives upward the evidence that an anatomical cure is possible (oncology's resection margins), the model of the perioperative pathway that [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) adapts to each life stage, and, to [15](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md), the surgical safety checklist as the archetype of [patient safety](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/patient-safety-and-quality-improvement.md).

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Injury and repair](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#injury-and-repair): the operation is a wound; healing, infection and scarring decide its outcome.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): the surgical decision is the explicit weighing of a certain injury against a probable gain.
- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): ischaemic bowel, exsanguination and the compartment syndrome each have a window measured in hours.
- [Structure determines function](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#structure-determines-function): surgery is the specialty that treats by changing structure.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): haemorrhage hides behind a normal blood pressure until the reserve is gone.
- [Redundancy and reserve](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#redundancy-and-reserve): what can be removed (a kidney, a lung lobe, most of the liver) is set by the reserve of the remainder.
