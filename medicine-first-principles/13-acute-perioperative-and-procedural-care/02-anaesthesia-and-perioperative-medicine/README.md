# Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine

> **Layer:** [13 Acute, Perioperative and Procedural Care](../README.md) › Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine
> **Children:** [General and regional anaesthesia](general-and-regional-anaesthesia.md) · [Airway and ventilation management](airway-and-ventilation-management.md) · [Perioperative risk and complications](perioperative-risk-and-complications.md)

## What this area is about

Before 1846 an operation was limited by how long a conscious patient could bear it. Anaesthesia removed that limit and, in doing so, created a new one: a patient who does not feel also does not breathe adequately, protect their airway, or keep their blood pressure up, because the same drugs that abolish consciousness and pain depress the brainstem reflexes and the autonomic tone that keep a body alive. The question this area answers is therefore double: **how do you switch off consciousness, pain, reflexes and movement reversibly, and how do you keep the body alive while they are off?** The second half grew into perioperative medicine, the discipline of measuring a patient's reserve before surgery, protecting it during, and recognising its failure after.

The unifying idea is **reversible pharmacological control of physiology**. An anaesthetic is a titrated, continuously monitored, deliberately reversible poisoning: a drug whose [dose–response](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/02-pharmacodynamics/dose-response-and-therapeutic-index.md) curve for the desired effect (unconsciousness) lies close to the curve for the lethal one (apnoea, circulatory collapse), given by someone who takes over the functions it removes. That is why anaesthesia is the specialty that invented monitoring, the checklist and the modern approach to [patient safety](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/patient-safety-and-quality-improvement.md).

Read the drugs first: everything else follows from what they switch off. The airway leaf is the immediate consequence (the first thing a hypnotic removes is the airway, within a minute). Perioperative risk brackets both: it is the reason the anaesthetist sees the patient the day before and the week after, and it is where anaesthesia becomes a branch of internal medicine applied to the operated patient.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Surgery](../01-surgery/README.md):** the two halves of one procedure. The anaesthetist's assessment of reserve is the cost side of [the surgical decision](../01-surgery/the-surgical-decision.md); the surgeon's injury is what the anaesthetic has to make tolerable.
- **[Critical care](../03-critical-care/README.md):** grew out of anaesthesia (the 1952 Copenhagen polio epidemic, when anaesthetists hand-ventilated patients for weeks) and uses the same airway skills, ventilators, sedatives and vasoactive drugs for days rather than hours.
- **[Emergency medicine](../04-emergency-medicine/README.md):** shares the airway and the [resuscitation](../04-emergency-medicine/resuscitation-and-cardiac-arrest.md); the emergency anaesthetic in a patient who is bleeding, full of food and unassessed is the hardest case of the specialty.
- **[Ophthalmology and ENT](../05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md):** the shared airway: ENT operates inside the space the anaesthetist needs, and the obstructed upper airway is a joint emergency.

## Interfaces with other specialties

- **[Cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md):** perioperative [myocardial infarction](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/ischemic-heart-disease.md) is the leading cause of death after non-cardiac surgery; cardiology quantifies the risk, decides whether coronary disease should be treated first, and manages the [heart failure](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/heart-failure.md) and [arrhythmias](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/arrhythmias.md) that the stress response unmasks.
- **[Pulmonology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/README.md):** [asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/asthma-and-copd.md) and sleep apnoea decide how a patient tolerates the anaesthetic and whether they can be extubated; postoperative [pneumonia](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/pneumonia-and-respiratory-infections.md) is the commonest pulmonary complication.
- **[Nephrology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md):** perioperative [acute kidney injury](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/acute-kidney-injury.md) from hypotension, contrast and nephrotoxic drugs; the dialysis patient's potassium and fluid balance on the day of surgery.
- **[Endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md):** [diabetes](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/diabetes-mellitus.md) meets the stress response (hyperglycaemia impairs wound healing and immunity); [adrenal insufficiency](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/adrenal-pituitary-and-gonadal-disorders.md) and long-term corticosteroids need cover for the surge the patient cannot mount.
- **[Haematology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md):** anticoagulants and antiplatelet drugs must be stopped or bridged ([bleeding and clotting disorders](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/bleeding-and-clotting-disorders.md)); preoperative [anaemia](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/anaemias.md) is corrected because transfusion carries its own risk ([transfusion medicine](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/transfusion-medicine.md)).
- **[Neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md):** postoperative delirium and cognitive dysfunction, perioperative [stroke](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/stroke.md), and the [neuromuscular diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/demyelinating-and-neuromuscular-disease.md) that interact with muscle relaxants.
- **[Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md):** obstetric anaesthesia is the largest regional-anaesthesia practice (epidural analgesia in [labour](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/labour-delivery-and-the-puerperium.md), spinal anaesthesia for caesarean section) and the pregnant airway is the most feared.
- **[Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md):** the child's airway, oxygen consumption and drug handling differ by [scale](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#scale-and-surface-to-volume); neonatal anaesthesia is a subspecialty.
- **[Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** [frailty](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/frailty-multimorbidity-and-polypharmacy.md) predicts perioperative harm better than age; shared decisions about whether to operate at all, and about [limits of treatment](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/death-dying-and-end-of-life-decisions.md), are made with the anaesthetist.
- **[Psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md):** [substance use](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/addiction-and-substance-use.md) alters tolerance to opioids and sedatives; electroconvulsive therapy is delivered under general anaesthesia; chronic pain services sit between anaesthesia and psychiatry.
- **[Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** supplies the medication list and the comorbidities, receives the patient with their new analgesics, and increasingly runs the preoperative optimisation (smoking, anaemia, glycaemia, exercise) that changes outcomes more than anything done on the day.

## Connections across layers

Anaesthesia takes from [06 Physiology](../../06-physiology/README.md) the nervous system it switches off and the respiratory and cardiovascular systems it must then run by hand; from [09 Pharmacology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) its [pharmacokinetics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/01-pharmacokinetics/distribution-and-protein-binding.md) compressed into minutes (induction is a distribution phenomenon, emergence is redistribution, not elimination) and its [receptor theory](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/02-pharmacodynamics/receptor-theory-agonists-and-antagonists.md); from [10](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) and [11](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) the [risk scores](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/risk-prediction-and-stratification.md) and [functional tests](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/functional-testing-spirometry-exercise-and-sleep.md) that quantify reserve. It gives upward the airway and ventilation skills that [critical care](../03-critical-care/README.md) and [resuscitation](../04-emergency-medicine/resuscitation-and-cardiac-arrest.md) depend on, the model of the perioperative pathway that [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) adapts to each age, and to [15](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) the safety culture (monitoring standards, the surgical checklist, incident reporting) that other specialties copied.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Dose–response](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#dose-response): the anaesthetic is a drug titrated to an effect that sits close to a lethal one; the whole specialty lives on the steep part of the curve.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): the drugs remove the body's controllers (chemoreceptor drive, baroreflex, thermoregulation) and the anaesthetist becomes the controller.
- [Supply and demand](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): preoxygenation, oxygen delivery and the stress response are all oxygen supply against consumption.
- [Redundancy and reserve](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#redundancy-and-reserve): perioperative risk is reserve measured before it is tested.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): no anaesthetic is given for its own sake; its only benefit is the operation it enables.
- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): induction in seconds, emergence in minutes, complications over days.
