# 08 Pathology

> **Layer:** 08 Pathology
> **Children:** [Cell and tissue injury](01-cell-and-tissue-injury/README.md) · [Haemodynamic disorders](02-hemodynamic-disorders/README.md) · [Neoplasia](03-neoplasia/README.md) · [Genetic, metabolic and environmental disease](04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/README.md) · [Disease as process](05-disease-as-process/README.md)

## What this area is about

Pathology is the study of disease as a biological process: what causes it, how the cause produces changes in cells and tissues, what those changes look like, and how they produce the symptoms a patient feels. This layer is *general* pathology. It does not describe diseases of the heart or kidney; it describes the small set of mechanisms that produce disease in every organ, so that the specialty leaves above can cite one explanation instead of repeating it forty times. Those mechanisms are: a cell adapts, is injured or dies; death and infection provoke inflammation, which defends and damages; damaged tissue is regenerated or scarred; fluid, blood and clot go where they should not; a clone escapes the controls on growth; and the genome, diet, environment and time each supply their own injuries. The fifth area describes the shape of any disease in time and the logic of diseases with many causes.

The idea that unifies the layer is that **disease is normal physiology pushed past its reserve, and the body's response to injury is itself the larger part of most disease**. Every mechanism here is a normal process (inflammation, clotting, cell division, fluid filtration, immune recognition, the stress response) acting in the wrong amount, place or duration. Knowing which parameter tells you what a drug, an operation or a change of behaviour can reverse.

## Why this layer sits here

Pathology needs the healthy body already described. From [03 Cell biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md) it takes the cell cycle, apoptosis, senescence and the stress response, because injury, cancer and aging are those programmes failing. From [02 Biochemistry](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/README.md) it takes energy metabolism (why hypoxia kills), DNA repair (why mutations accumulate), lipoprotein biology and enzyme kinetics (why inborn errors are recessive). From [04 Genetics, evolution and development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md) it takes inheritance, mutation, complex traits and the evolutionary reasons that aging exists. From [05 Tissues and anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md) it takes the normal tissue against which a tumour is classified and the vascular architecture that shapes an infarct. From [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md) it takes Starling's forces, haemostasis and blood pressure regulation; oedema, thrombosis and shock are these balances tipped. From [07 Microbiology and immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md) it takes the machinery that inflammation deploys and the microbes that trigger it. From [01 Quantitative foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md) it takes causal logic and probability, because risk and natural history are statistical ideas.

What the layers above need from it is **a mechanism for every disease**. [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) is drugs that act on steps named here: anti-inflammatories on mediators, anticoagulants on Virchow's triad, statins on the plaque, anticancer drugs on the hallmarks. [10 Epidemiology](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) measures the risks and natural histories defined here. [11 Clinical method](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) reasons backwards along the aetiology–pathogenesis–manifestation chain and reads the lesions in histopathology. Every disease leaf in [12](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md), [13](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) and [14](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) opens with a link into this layer. The abstraction this layer contributes is **the lesion**: a structural or functional change in tissue that stands between cause and symptom, can be seen, measured and classified, and is what a treatment either reverses or does not.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[03 Cell biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md):** supplies the programmes (death, cycle, senescence, stress, regeneration) whose misuse is the content of 01, 03 and the aging leaf.
- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** every haemodynamic disorder is a physiological balance (Starling, Virchow, cardiac output, energy balance) with one term moved; compensation and decompensation are physiology's reserve running out.
- **[07 Microbiology and immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md):** inflammation is the immune system seen through a microscope; sepsis is cytokines acting on every vessel; hypersensitivity and autoimmunity cause much chronic inflammation; viruses and *Helicobacter* cause cancer.
- **[09 Pharmacology and therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** drug classes are organised by the pathological step they reverse, and toxicology is environmental injury applied to medicines.
- **[10 Epidemiology and evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md):** risk factors, natural history, screening windows and the biases of early detection are introduced here and measured there.
- **[11 Clinical method and diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** histopathology and molecular diagnostics are this layer practised on specimens; clinical chemistry measures the enzymes that leak from injured cells.
- **[12 Organ-system specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md), [13 Acute care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) and [14 Life course](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md):** each disease leaf cites a mechanism here. Ischaemic heart disease is atherosclerosis plus thrombosis plus infarction; stroke is infarction in the brain; sepsis in critical care is this layer's shock leaf at the bedside; cancers are the neoplasia area in one organ; paediatric genetic disease and geriatric frailty are area 04.

## Connections across layers

Pathology is the hinge of the tree. Below it, seven layers describe matter, information and the healthy organism; above it, seven describe how medicine recognises and treats failure. It converts the physiology of health into the mechanisms of disease and hands them upward as the targets of therapy, the content of diagnosis and the subject of every specialty.
