## What this area is about

Cells live within a fraction of a millimetre of a capillary and depend on blood arriving, exchanging and leaving. This area describes what goes wrong with the delivery system: fluid in the wrong compartment (oedema), blood that clots inside vessels and the tissue that dies when the clot blocks or embolises (thrombosis and infarction), too little circulating volume or pressure to perfuse anything (haemorrhage and shock, including the vasodilated, leaky circulation of sepsis), and the arterial wall disease that underlies most arterial thrombosis (atherosclerosis). Together these account for the leading causes of death in the world: ischaemic heart disease, stroke, pulmonary embolism and septic shock are all haemodynamic failures.

The unifying idea is **a balance of forces across a wall or along a vessel**. Starling's forces decide whether fluid stays in the capillary. Virchow's triad decides whether blood stays fluid. Cardiac output, volume and vascular tone decide whether pressure reaches the tissue. Shear stress and lipoprotein concentration decide whether the arterial wall stays clean. Each disease in this area is one of these balances tipped, and each specialty leaf that cites it is the same balance tipped in a particular organ.

Oedema comes first because Starling's balance is the simplest and the vocabulary of exudate and transudate is used by the others. Thrombosis follows: it is haemostasis in the wrong place, and infarction is the cell-injury leaf applied to a territory. Shock is the circulation failing as a whole, and sepsis belongs here because its lethal mechanism is haemodynamic (vasodilatation, leak, microthrombosis) rather than microbial. Atherosclerosis closes the area because it needs both thrombosis and chronic inflammation, and because it is the single lesion from which most arterial infarction springs.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cell and tissue injury](../01-cell-and-tissue-injury/README.md):** infarction is necrosis; the leaky vessel of inflammation is a cause of oedema; sepsis is acute inflammation gone systemic; atherosclerosis is chronic inflammation in a wall; the healing infarct is repair by scar.
- **[Neoplasia](../03-neoplasia/README.md):** cancer is a major acquired cause of thrombosis (tumour tissue factor), tumours embolise and cause lymphatic obstruction, and they grow by recruiting the same angiogenesis the healing wound uses.
- **[Genetic, metabolic and environmental disease](../04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/README.md):** inherited thrombophilias and familial hypercholesterolaemia are single-gene causes of these disorders; obesity and diabetes drive atherosclerosis; aging stiffens arteries and raises the thrombosis baseline.
- **[Disease as process](../05-disease-as-process/README.md):** atherosclerosis is the model multifactorial disease, and the risk-factor arithmetic there is derived from it.
