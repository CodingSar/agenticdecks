## What this area is about

The examination reaches as far as the senses; the laboratory needs a sample. This area is how the body is observed from outside without cutting it open: by sending energy through it and reading what comes back (X-rays, sound, radio waves), by placing an emitter inside and tracking it (nuclear medicine), by recording the electrical signals it generates (the Electrocardiogram (ECG), the Electroencephalogram (EEG), nerve studies), and by loading its systems and measuring how they respond (spirometry, exercise, sleep). Each method is a piece of applied physics from layer 00 pointed at a piece of physiology from layer 06, and each produces an image or a trace that is read, like every other test, against a pre-test probability.

The unifying idea is that **each modality measures one physical property, and what it can show is what differs in that property**. Computed Tomography (CT) shows attenuation, so it shows bone, air, blood and iodine but not grey from white matter; ultrasound shows impedance boundaries, so it shows fluid against solid and flow against stillness, but nothing behind air or bone; Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) shows proton relaxation, so it shows water content and molecular environment, soft tissue in all its variety, but not cortical bone or aerated lung; nuclear imaging, including Positron Emission Tomography (PET), shows where a labelled molecule went, so it shows function at the resolution of a decaying atom. The electrical and functional tests measure physiology directly, in time rather than in space. Knowing the property each modality measures tells you which to order, what its blind spots are, and why they are fused.

Read the four imaging children first, in the order given: CT establishes attenuation and the radiation cost; ultrasound and MRI show two radiation-free ways to get contrast CT lacks; nuclear medicine moves from structure to function. The electrical and functional children then show physiology measured directly rather than pictured. The sibling contrasts matter as much as the content: the right test is the one whose measured property differs between the diagnoses in question.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[The clinical encounter](../01-the-clinical-encounter/README.md):** imaging is the examination's instruments extended (ultrasound as palpation with sound, the ECG as the pulse written down, spirometry as the breath measured), and every image is interpreted against the probability the encounter produced. The threshold decision of whether to image at all, and the incidental findings that follow imaging at low pre-test probability, come from there. Radiation justification is that threshold logic made a legal duty.
- **[Laboratory medicine](../02-laboratory-medicine/README.md):** the laboratory says what and how much; imaging says where and how large; the two are combined in staging, in the D-dimer-then-scan pathway for embolism, and in the pairing of ECG with troponin for infarction. Nuclear medicine and molecular pathology label the same molecules, one in the living patient and one on a slide, and the predicted values of functional tests follow the same logic as reference ranges.

## Connections across layers

From below, this area is the most direct consumer of [00 Physical and Chemical Foundations](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md) in the clinical half of the tree: [radiation, sound and electromagnetic waves](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/waves-sound-light-and-radiation.md), [electricity in excitable tissue](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/electricity-and-excitable-membranes.md), [pressure and flow](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/fluids-pressure-and-flow.md) and the nuclear properties of [hydrogen in water](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/02-chemistry-of-life/water-solutions-and-concentration.md). It takes [exponential decay](../../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/compartment-models-and-exponential-processes.md) and [signal processing](../../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/information-signals-and-noise.md) from 01, [regional anatomy](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/03-regional-anatomy/README.md) as the map every image is read against, [physiology](../../06-physiology/README.md) as the function every trace records, [radiation biology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/radiotherapy-and-radiation-biology.md) for the cost of X-rays and isotopes, and [diagnostic accuracy studies](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/02-evidence-based-medicine/diagnostic-accuracy-studies.md) for each modality's sensitivity and specificity per indication.

To the layers above it gives the images and traces that define disease in nearly every specialty: CT and MRI in [stroke](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/stroke.md), echocardiography in [valvular](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md) and [heart failure](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/heart-failure.md), the ECG in [arrhythmias](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/arrhythmias.md), spirometry in asthma and Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease ([asthma and COPD](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/asthma-and-copd.md)), PET-CT in [oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/common-solid-tumours.md), whole-body CT in [trauma](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/trauma-and-haemorrhage-control.md), bedside ultrasound in [shock](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/shock-and-haemodynamic-support.md), obstetric ultrasound in [pregnancy](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/pregnancy-and-its-complications.md); and to [15](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) it hands the questions of cost, access and the machine-learning readers of [health informatics](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/health-informatics-and-artificial-intelligence.md).

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Structure determines function](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#structure-determines-function): imaging shows structure and infers function; functional testing measures function and infers structure; the two halves of this parent are the two directions of that inference.
- [Gradients and flow](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#gradients-and-flow): Doppler, perfusion imaging, contrast phases and spirometric flow all measure movement down a gradient.
- [Supply and demand](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): stress testing and stress perfusion imaging exist to reveal a supply that is adequate at rest and fails under load.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): ionising radiation, gadolinium, incidental findings and cost are the prices of seeing; justification weighs them.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): every image is read against a pre-test probability, and inter-observer variation is a measured quantity.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): functional reserve hides disease at rest; loading the system is how the hidden limit is found.
