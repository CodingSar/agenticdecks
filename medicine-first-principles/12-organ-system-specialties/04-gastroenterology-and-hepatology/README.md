## What this area is about

Gastroenterology and hepatology cover a nine-metre tube and the three glands that drain into it. The tube has one job in two halves: break food down with acid, enzymes and bile, and absorb the products across an epithelium a single cell thick while keeping the trillions of bacteria on the other side out. The liver receives everything the gut absorbs through the portal vein, detoxifies it, stores it, builds the plasma proteins and clotting factors from it, and makes the bile that goes back to the gut. The specialty's question is: when a patient has pain, bleeding, diarrhoea, jaundice or weight loss, which part of this system has failed, is the failure in the lumen, the wall or the gland, and can an endoscope reach it?

The unifying idea is **a barrier under chemical and microbial attack from its own contents**. The stomach must hold acid at pH 1–2 without digesting itself; the intestine must hold back a microbiome while admitting nutrients; the liver must process portal blood full of bacterial products without inflaming. Acid-peptic disease, inflammatory bowel disease, cirrhosis and pancreatitis are each a barrier that has been breached or an organ digesting itself, and the specialty's second defining feature is that the tube can be looked at and sampled directly, so endoscopy is both its main diagnostic instrument and much of its therapy.

Read the acid-peptic leaf first: it introduces the mucosal barrier, the endoscope and the approach to bleeding, which is the specialty's commonest emergency. Inflammatory bowel disease and malabsorption extend the barrier idea down the tube and introduce the immune and microbial dimension. Liver disease is the largest leaf and the one with the most interfaces, because the liver's failure is systemic. Pancreatic and biliary disease closes the set and sits on the border with surgery.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** antiplatelet and anticoagulant drugs are the commonest precipitant of upper gastrointestinal bleeding, and the two specialties negotiate when to stop and restart them; cirrhosis produces a high-output circulation, and right heart failure congests the liver.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** cirrhosis causes a functional kidney failure (hepatorenal syndrome); diarrhoea and vomiting are the commonest causes of electrolyte disorders.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** metabolic-dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease is the hepatic face of the metabolic syndrome and now the commonest liver disease; the pancreas is both digestive gland and endocrine organ, and pancreatitis or resection causes diabetes.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** the liver makes the clotting factors, so liver failure is a coagulopathy; iron deficiency anaemia is a gastrointestinal bleed until proved otherwise; the spleen enlarges in portal hypertension.
- **[Oncology](../07-oncology/README.md):** colorectal, gastric, oesophageal, pancreatic and liver cancers are among the commonest and most lethal; gastroenterology screens for, finds and biopsies them.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** viral hepatitis, *Helicobacter pylori*, *Clostridioides difficile* and infectious diarrhoea are shared, as is the question of which traveller's or immunosuppressed patient's diarrhoea is infective.
- **[Rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** inflammatory bowel disease shares genes, drugs and a spondyloarthritis with rheumatology; autoimmune hepatitis and primary biliary cholangitis are autoimmune diseases of the liver.

## Interfaces with other specialties

The border with [general surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) is the specialty's longest and most contested. Gallstones, appendicitis, perforation and obstruction are [the acute abdomen](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/the-acute-abdomen.md) and belong to surgery; pancreatitis is medical until it necroses and infects; a bleeding ulcer is endoscopic until endoscopy fails; a colitis is medical until it perforates or stops responding; a cancer is found by the gastroenterologist and removed by the surgeon. Each of these has a defined hand-over point, and the joint ward round or multidisciplinary meeting (gastroenterologist, surgeon, radiologist, pathologist, oncologist) is where it is negotiated. Interventional [radiology](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/README.md) sits between them: it embolises bleeding vessels, drains abscesses and stents bile ducts that neither endoscope nor scalpel can reach. [Transplant surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) is hepatology's final hand-over, and the assessment of who should receive a scarce liver, including the person who drinks, is made jointly and against explicit criteria. [Emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) resuscitate the exsanguinating variceal bleed and manage acute liver failure, in which the decision about transplantation must be made within days. [Poisoning](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/poisoning-and-overdose.md) with paracetamol is the commonest cause of acute liver failure in high-income countries and is shared with toxicology and [psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md).

In layer 14, [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) manages the large volume of dyspepsia, reflux, irritable bowel syndrome and fatty liver, and decides who needs an endoscope; the referral criteria for suspected cancer (weight loss, dysphagia, bleeding, iron deficiency, age) are the interface. [Psychiatry and addiction services](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/addiction-and-substance-use.md) share alcohol-related liver disease, where the liver cannot recover unless the drinking stops. [Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) owns childhood coeliac disease, inflammatory bowel disease of early onset and biliary atresia. [Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares the liver diseases of pregnancy and the pregnant woman with inflammatory bowel disease. [Palliative care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/palliative-care-and-symptom-control.md) manages the ascites, itch and encephalopathy of end-stage liver disease and the obstruction of advanced cancer.

## Connections across layers

From [06 Physiology](../../06-physiology/06-gastrointestinal-system/README.md) the specialty takes motility, secretion, absorption, the liver's metabolism and the gut barrier; from [07](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/01-microbes-and-infection/the-human-microbiome.md) the microbiome and mucosal immunity; from [08 Pathology](../../08-pathology/README.md) inflammation, fibrosis, haemorrhage and neoplasia; from [09 Pharmacology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) acid suppression, immunomodulators, biologics and the fact that the liver metabolises most drugs ([drug metabolism](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/01-pharmacokinetics/drug-metabolism-and-interactions.md)); from [11](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) endoscopy, ultrasound, computed tomography and liver chemistry. It gives [13](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) the physiology behind the acute abdomen and bleeding, and [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) its commonest functional complaints.
