## Essence

The intestinal wall must do two opposite things across one cell layer: absorb nutrients and exclude a microbial population of some 10^13 cells. Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) is what happens when the immune system beneath that layer loses tolerance of the microbes above it and mounts a chronic inflammatory attack on the wall; coeliac disease is the same loss of tolerance directed at a dietary protein, gluten. Malabsorption is the functional consequence of any disease that destroys the absorptive surface, removes the digestive enzymes or bile, or shortens the bowel: weight loss, diarrhoea and the specific deficiencies of whatever was absorbed in the damaged segment. The unifying idea is that where in the tube the damage lies predicts both the symptoms and the deficiency, because absorption is regional.

## Core tenets

1. **The gut is tolerant by design, and IBD is tolerance lost toward the microbiome.** A single epithelial layer, its mucus, antimicrobial peptides, secretory immunoglobulin A and a regulatory T-cell population keep the immune system quiet in the presence of trillions of bacteria ([gut barrier](../../06-physiology/06-gastrointestinal-system/the-gut-barrier-and-microbiome.md), [tolerance](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/tolerance-and-self-recognition.md)). In genetically susceptible people (over 200 risk loci, including genes for bacterial sensing such as *NOD2* and for autophagy), an environmental change lets the barrier leak, antigen-presenting cells meet bacterial products, and effector T cells drive [chronic inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/chronic-inflammation-and-granulomas.md) that never resolves because the antigen never leaves. Incidence rose with industrialisation and is now rising in newly industrialised countries, which argues that the trigger is environmental ([the host and its environment](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#the-host-and-its-environment)).

2. **Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis are two patterns of that attack, and the pattern predicts the complications.** Ulcerative colitis inflames the mucosa only, continuously from the rectum upward: bloody diarrhoea, urgency, and in severe cases a dilated colon that may perforate; its long-term risk is colorectal cancer, proportional to extent and duration, so surveillance colonoscopy is routine. Crohn's disease inflames the full thickness of the wall anywhere from mouth to anus, in patches, with granulomas: the transmural inflammation causes strictures (obstruction), fistulae (abnormal tracts to skin, bladder or other bowel) and abscesses, and its favourite site, the terminal ileum, causes malabsorption of vitamin B12 and bile salts. Each is diagnosed by endoscopy with biopsy, supported by faecal calprotectin, a neutrophil protein that separates inflammatory from functional diarrhoea.

3. **IBD is a systemic disease that happens to be loudest in the gut.** The same inflammation produces fever, weight loss, anaemia (from blood loss, iron malabsorption and the cytokine suppression of marrow), raised C-reactive protein, and extra-intestinal disease: a spondyloarthritis and peripheral arthritis, uveitis, erythema nodosum and pyoderma gangrenosum of the skin, and primary sclerosing cholangitis of the bile ducts ([inflammatory arthritis](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/inflammatory-arthritis.md)). In children the inflammation stunts growth, and growth velocity is a disease activity measure ([growth and development](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/growth-development-and-the-child-as-patient.md)).

4. **Treatment suppresses the immune response at successively more specific points.** Aminosalicylates act locally on the colonic mucosa in mild colitis. Corticosteroids induce remission fast but cannot maintain it without unacceptable harm. Thiopurines (azathioprine) and methotrexate deplete proliferating lymphocytes. Monoclonal antibodies block tumour necrosis factor (infliximab), the integrins that let lymphocytes home to the gut (vedolizumab), or interleukins 12 and 23 (ustekinumab), and small molecules inhibit Janus kinase signalling ([immunomodulatory drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anti-inflammatory-and-immunomodulatory-drugs.md), [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md)). Each works by removing a signal in the loop; each trades inflammation for infection and, for some, lymphoma. Mucosal healing on endoscopy, not just symptom relief, is the target because it predicts fewer strictures, operations and cancers. Surgery removes the colon in ulcerative colitis (which cures it) and resects strictures and fistulae in Crohn's (which does not, because the disease recurs).

5. **Coeliac disease is an immune reaction to gluten that flattens the small intestine.** In people carrying HLA-DQ2 or DQ8, gluten peptides deamidated by tissue transglutaminase are presented to T cells, which destroy the villi of the proximal small intestine; antibodies to transglutaminase are the serological test and a duodenal biopsy showing villous atrophy the proof ([antigen presentation](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/antigen-presentation-and-mhc.md)). It affects about 1% of European-ancestry populations, most undiagnosed, and presents as anything from classic malabsorption to isolated iron deficiency, osteoporosis or infertility. A strict gluten-free diet restores the mucosa, which makes it the one autoimmune disease treated by removing the antigen.

6. **Malabsorption follows the map of absorption.** Iron, folate and calcium are absorbed in the duodenum and jejunum, so proximal disease (coeliac) causes iron deficiency and osteomalacia; vitamin B12 and bile salts are absorbed in the terminal ileum, so ileal Crohn's or resection causes B12 deficiency and, through bile salt loss, fat malabsorption ([digestion and absorption](../../06-physiology/06-gastrointestinal-system/secretion-digestion-and-absorption.md)). Fat that is not absorbed reaches the colon and produces pale, bulky, floating stool (steatorrhoea) and the loss of fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E and K; unabsorbed carbohydrate is fermented to gas and osmotically active acids. Pancreatic exocrine failure and bile duct obstruction cause malabsorption with an intact mucosa ([pancreatic and biliary disease](pancreatic-and-biliary-disease.md)), and bacterial overgrowth in a stagnant loop consumes nutrients before the host can.

7. **The diagnosis of chronic diarrhoea works by mechanism.** Osmotic diarrhoea (unabsorbed solute) stops when the patient fasts; secretory diarrhoea (toxins, hormones, bile acids) does not. Blood and calprotectin mark inflammation; fat marks malabsorption; nocturnal symptoms and weight loss mark organic disease. Irritable bowel syndrome, altered motility and visceral sensitivity without structural disease, is the commonest cause of chronic abdominal symptoms and is diagnosed on positive features once the few tests that exclude coeliac disease, IBD and cancer are done, not by exhausting every investigation ([clinical reasoning](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/clinical-reasoning-and-cognitive-bias.md)).

8. **Who else is involved.** [Colorectal surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) shares every IBD patient, for strictures, fistulae, perforation and colectomy, in a joint clinic. [Rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/inflammatory-arthritis.md) shares the arthritis and most of the drugs; [dermatology](../11-dermatology/inflammatory-skin-disease.md) and ophthalmology the skin and eye disease. [Haematology](../06-haematology/anaemias.md) sees the anaemia. [Dietetics and nutrition](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/fluids-nutrition-and-blood-products.md) manage the gluten-free diet, exclusive enteral nutrition (which induces remission in paediatric Crohn's) and parenteral nutrition in short bowel. [Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) owns early-onset disease and its effect on growth; [obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares the pregnant patient, in whom active disease is more dangerous than most of the drugs.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Oral tolerance | Active immune non-response to food antigens and commensal microbes |
| Ulcerative colitis | Continuous mucosal inflammation of the colon from the rectum upward |
| Crohn's disease | Patchy transmural granulomatous inflammation anywhere in the gut |
| Faecal calprotectin | Neutrophil protein in stool; marks intestinal inflammation |
| Fistula | Abnormal tract between bowel and another surface, from transmural inflammation |
| Mucosal healing | Endoscopic resolution of inflammation; the modern treatment target |
| Coeliac disease | T-cell-mediated villous atrophy triggered by gluten in HLA-DQ2/DQ8 carriers |
| Steatorrhoea | Fat in stool from fat malabsorption; pale, bulky, floating |
| Irritable bowel syndrome | Chronic abdominal pain with altered bowel habit and no structural cause |

## Common misconceptions

- **"IBD and irritable bowel syndrome are versions of the same thing."** IBD is structural inflammation with bleeding, weight loss and raised calprotectin; irritable bowel syndrome has no inflammation and a different mechanism.
- **"Coeliac disease is a childhood disease with diarrhoea."** Most cases are diagnosed in adults and many have no gut symptoms, presenting with iron deficiency, osteoporosis or fatigue.
- **"Removing the colon cures Crohn's disease."** It cures ulcerative colitis; Crohn's recurs elsewhere in the bowel after any resection.
- **"Steroids are the treatment for IBD."** They induce remission but cannot maintain it; maintenance needs immunomodulators or biologics.
