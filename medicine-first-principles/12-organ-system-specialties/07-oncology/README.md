## What this area is about

Oncology is the one organ-system specialty defined by a disease process rather than an organ. Cancer can arise in any tissue, so oncology cuts across every other specialty in this layer, and what it owns is the method: diagnose by tissue, stage by spread, decide by intent, treat with a combination of local and systemic therapy, and measure the outcome honestly. The biology, a clone that has acquired the [hallmarks of cancer](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/hallmarks-of-cancer.md) and evolves under treatment, is taken from general pathology and not repeated here.

The idea that unifies the three children is **selective toxicity under uncertainty**: every treatment exploits some difference between tumour and host (proliferation, hormone dependence, a driver mutation, foreignness to the immune system), every difference is partial, and so every decision is a trade between the probability of benefit and the certainty of harm, made with a patient whose goals must be known.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** anthracyclines, trastuzumab and chest radiotherapy injure the heart; cardio-oncology decides whether the heart or the cancer is the greater threat.
- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** lung cancer is diagnosed by bronchoscopy; radiation pneumonitis and checkpoint-inhibitor pneumonitis are respiratory problems of treatment.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** platinum drugs, tumour lysis and obstruction by pelvic tumours injure the kidney; kidney function limits dosing.
- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** gut and liver cancers are found at endoscopy; liver metastases and drug hepatitis cross the boundary.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** hormone-dependent tumours are treated by manipulating endocrine axes; checkpoint inhibitors cause hypophysitis, thyroiditis and diabetes.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** shares cytotoxics, transplantation and the neutropenic patient; owns leukaemia, lymphoma and myeloma.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** neutropenic sepsis, reactivation of latent viruses under immunosuppression, and the viral causes of cancer (papillomavirus, hepatitis B and C, Epstein–Barr virus).
- **[Rheumatology and clinical immunology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** immune-related adverse events are autoimmune disease by another route; paraneoplastic syndromes mimic rheumatic disease.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** brain metastases, cord compression, chemotherapy neuropathy and paraneoplastic encephalitis.
- **[Dermatology](../11-dermatology/README.md):** skin cancer is the commonest cancer; targeted drugs cause characteristic rashes.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Oncology **receives** almost every patient from somewhere else: [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) recognises the symptom and refers; an organ specialty or [surgical subspecialty](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) obtains the tissue and often does the first, local treatment; [radiology](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/README.md) and [pathology](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/README.md) stage and classify. The multidisciplinary team meeting is the formal mechanism by which these specialties make a joint decision; it is where territory (operate or irradiate, surgery first or chemotherapy first) is settled.

Oncology **hands over** in two directions. Downstream, [palliative care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) is involved early rather than at the end, and primary care inherits the survivor with late effects and the fear of recurrence. Sideways, the toxicities of treatment belong to the organ specialties: cardio-oncology with [cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md), immune colitis with [gastroenterology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md), endocrinopathies with [endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md).

**Emergencies cross the boundary** constantly. Neutropenic sepsis is treated by [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) with [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md); cord compression needs [neurosurgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) or radiotherapy within hours; bowel obstruction is a [surgical](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) problem with an oncological prognosis attached. Whether an intensive-care bed is appropriate for a patient with incurable cancer is a joint decision between oncology, critical care and the patient. [Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) assesses fitness in the old, who form the majority of patients; [paediatric oncology](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) is a separate specialty because childhood cancers are different diseases with much higher cure rates. [Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares cancer in pregnancy and the gynaecological cancers.

## Connections across layers

Oncology takes its disease model from the whole of [neoplasia](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/README.md), its drugs from [anticancer drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anticancer-drugs.md), [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md) and [radiotherapy](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/radiotherapy-and-radiation-biology.md), its evidence culture from [randomised trials](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/02-evidence-based-medicine/randomized-controlled-trials.md) and [screening](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/screening-principles-and-biases.md), and its diagnostics from [histopathology and molecular diagnostics](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/histopathology-cytology-and-molecular-diagnostics.md) and [nuclear medicine](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/nuclear-medicine-and-molecular-imaging.md). It gives upward the decision framework that [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/the-surgical-decision.md) and [palliative care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/palliative-care-and-symptom-control.md) extend, and the costliest drugs that [health economics](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/health-economics-and-priority-setting.md) must ration.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): every oncological decision is a number needed to treat against a certain toxicity.
- [Probability and uncertainty](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#probability-and-uncertainty): micrometastasis is treated on probability; survival is a distribution, not a number.
- [Dose-response](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#dose-response): log-kill, dose intensity and the narrow therapeutic index of cytotoxics.
- [Trade-offs and evolutionary legacy](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#trade-offs-and-evolutionary-legacy): resistance is natural selection inside one patient.
- [Specificity and recognition](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#specificity-and-recognition): targeted drugs and checkpoint inhibitors depend on, and fail by, recognition.
- [Time-courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): indolent versus aggressive disease, lead time, late effects decades after cure.
