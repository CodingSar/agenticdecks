## What this area is about

Dermatology is the medicine of the largest organ and the only one that can be examined completely without instruments. The skin is a barrier, a sensor, an immune organ and a display: it keeps water in and the world out, it is the first immune tissue that most antigens meet, and it reports internal disease, drug reactions and the patient's genetics and exposures to anyone who knows how to read it. The specialty's three children follow from this: a grammar for describing what is seen and inferring the level and process behind it; the diseases in which the skin's own barrier and immune functions go wrong; and the diseases inflicted by the two hazards the skin alone faces directly, ultraviolet light and microbes.

The unifying idea is that **the skin is a layered structure whose morphology is a direct readout of its pathology**. Depth of lesion, distribution over the body and colour each have a physical or biological meaning, so visual description is not a preliminary to diagnosis but most of it. A second idea follows: because the organ is accessible, the skin was where immunology became targeted therapy at scale, where cancer prevention is visibly possible, and where biopsy is cheap enough to settle most arguments.

Read morphology first; the other two are unreadable without its vocabulary. Inflammatory skin disease and skin cancer and infections can then be read in either order, but together they show the two sides of immunosuppression: it treats the first group and provokes the second.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** psoriasis and chronic inflammation raise cardiovascular risk; xanthelasma and tendon xanthomata mark hyperlipidaemia; emboli and endocarditis leave splinter haemorrhages and Janeway lesions.
- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** sarcoidosis, cutaneous tuberculosis and the paraneoplastic skin signs of lung cancer.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** uraemic pruritus, calciphylaxis, and the transplant recipient whose squamous cell carcinoma risk needs lifelong skin surveillance.
- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** erythema nodosum and pyoderma gangrenosum in inflammatory bowel disease, dermatitis herpetiformis in coeliac disease, the spider naevi and palmar erythema of liver disease, and the interleukin-17 trade-off between skin and gut.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** acanthosis nigricans and insulin resistance, necrobiosis lipoidica and diabetic foot ulcers, the skin and hair changes of thyroid disease, acne and hirsutism under androgen drive.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** purpura and petechiae as the first sign of thrombocytopenia or vasculitis; cutaneous T-cell lymphoma; pruritus in lymphoma and polycythaemia.
- **[Oncology](../07-oncology/README.md):** melanoma's systemic therapy, the rashes of targeted drugs and checkpoint inhibitors, and paraneoplastic dermatoses.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** cellulitis, necrotising fasciitis, the exanthems, zoster, syphilis and the skin signs of HIV; screening before biologics.
- **[Rheumatology and clinical immunology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** psoriatic arthritis, lupus, dermatomyositis, scleroderma, vasculitis, urticaria and eczema are all shared, and the skin biopsy often makes the systemic diagnosis.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** zoster along a dermatome and post-herpetic neuralgia; the neurocutaneous syndromes; the skin findings of leprosy and neuropathic ulceration.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Dermatology is both a **referral specialty** and a **consulting service**. Most skin disease is managed in [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md), where rashes and lesions are among the commonest reasons for consultation; dermatology receives the suspicious pigmented lesion through urgent cancer pathways, the severe or unresponsive inflammatory disease, and the undiagnosed rash, increasingly by photograph. Teledermatology has made this one of the first specialties to triage remotely at scale. In the other direction, the dermatologist is called to the ward to read the skin for [rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md), [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md), [haematology](../06-haematology/README.md) and [oncology](../07-oncology/README.md), and the skin biopsy is often the quickest route to a systemic diagnosis.

**Border conditions** have joint clinics. Psoriatic arthritis and connective tissue disease are shared with rheumatology; the surgical treatment of skin cancer is shared with [plastic surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md), ENT and ophthalmic surgeons ([ophthalmology and ENT](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md)) for lesions of the face, eyelid and ear, and with oncology for advanced melanoma; the skin cancer multidisciplinary team includes all of them and histopathology. Vulval and genital dermatology sits between dermatology, [gynaecology](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) and sexual health; paediatric dermatology between dermatology and [paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md), where eczema, birthmarks and the genetic skin diseases present; wound care and leg ulcers between dermatology, vascular surgery, [geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) and community nursing. Occupational contact dermatitis is shared with [occupational medicine](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/sports-occupational-and-lifestyle-medicine.md), and the psychological burden of visible disease with [psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md).

**Emergencies cross the boundary.** Toxic epidermal necrolysis and staphylococcal scalded skin are managed with [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) and burns units, because a skin that has died in sheets fails as a barrier exactly as a burn does; necrotising fasciitis belongs to [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) within the hour; angio-oedema and anaphylaxis to [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and [anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md); non-blanching purpura with fever to the sepsis pathway. Dermatology's role in each is to recognise the pattern early and name the mechanism.

## Connections across layers

Dermatology rests on [the skin as barrier and sensor](../../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/the-skin-as-barrier-and-sensor.md) and [epithelium](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/01-tissues/epithelium.md) for structure, on [cell adhesion](../../03-cell-biology/03-cells-in-community/cell-adhesion-and-the-extracellular-matrix.md) for the blistering diseases, on [light and radiation](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/waves-sound-light-and-radiation.md) and [DNA repair](../../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/03-genetic-information-flow/dna-replication-and-repair.md) for skin cancer, on [hypersensitivity](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md) and [T cells](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/t-cells.md) for inflammatory disease, on the [microbes](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/01-microbes-and-infection/README.md) for infection, and on [immunomodulatory drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anti-inflammatory-and-immunomodulatory-drugs.md), [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md) and [histopathology](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/histopathology-cytology-and-molecular-diagnostics.md) for treatment and confirmation. It gives upward the reconstructive problems of [plastic surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md), the chronic-disease self-management of [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/chronic-disease-and-self-management.md), and the sun-protection and vaccination policies of [public health](../../15-medicine-in-society/03-populations-and-the-future/environmental-and-planetary-health.md).
