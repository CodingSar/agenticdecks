## Essence

The inflammatory skin diseases are the skin's two jobs, barrier and immune sentinel, going wrong together. Atopic eczema is a leaky barrier plus an immune system biased to the allergic (type 2) arm; psoriasis is an intact barrier plus an interleukin-23/17 drive that makes the epidermis proliferate ten times too fast; urticaria is mast-cell release in the dermis; contact dermatitis is a T-cell response to a chemical that has crossed the barrier. Each is chronic and relapsing, and each is now treatable at the level of its mechanism, because the cytokines that drive them have been identified and blocked one at a time. The skin was the first organ in which immunology became targeted therapy at scale, and quality of life, not survival, is the outcome that matters.

## Core tenets

1. **Atopic eczema starts with a defective barrier.** The stratum corneum is a wall of flattened keratinocytes mortared with lipids; filaggrin, the protein that organises keratin and generates the skin's natural moisturisers, is mutated in a large fraction of patients, and inflammation weakens the barrier further ([the skin as barrier and sensor](../../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/the-skin-as-barrier-and-sensor.md), [barriers and compartments](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#barriers-and-compartments)). Water leaves and allergens, irritants and *Staphylococcus aureus* enter. The response is a type 2 immune reaction (interleukins 4, 13 and 31, immunoglobulin E) that produces itch, and scratching further breaks the barrier ([hypersensitivity and allergy](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md)). The itch–scratch cycle is the disease, so emollients that repair the barrier are treatment, not cosmetics. It affects roughly one in five children in high-income countries and often resolves as the barrier matures.

2. **Psoriasis is an immune-driven acceleration of epidermal turnover.** Dendritic cells releasing interleukin-23 sustain T helper 17 cells that release interleukin-17, which drives keratinocytes to proliferate and transit the epidermis in days rather than weeks ([T cells](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/t-cells.md), [cytokines](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/02-innate-immunity/cytokines-interferons-and-nk-cells.md)). The result is the thick, well-demarcated, silvery-scaled plaque on elbows, knees, scalp and sacrum. HLA associations (HLA-C*06:02), triggers such as streptococcal infection and beta-blockers, and new lesions at sites of injury (Koebner phenomenon) reflect an immune system primed to react in the skin. Psoriasis is systemic: a third develop psoriatic arthritis ([inflammatory arthritis](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/inflammatory-arthritis.md)), and cardiovascular and metabolic disease are more frequent in proportion to its severity.

3. **Urticaria is mast-cell degranulation in the dermis and lasts hours; its timing distinguishes it from everything else.** Histamine from mast cells dilates dermal vessels and leaks plasma, producing itchy wheals that come and go within 24 hours; deeper leakage is angio-oedema ([hypersensitivity and allergy](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md)). Acute urticaria is often allergic or infective; chronic spontaneous urticaria, lasting over six weeks, is usually autoimmune, with antibodies activating the mast cell directly, and is not an allergy to anything. Antihistamines and anti-immunoglobulin E antibody treat it; allergy testing is rarely useful. A wheal that lasts days and bruises is urticarial vasculitis and needs biopsy.

4. **Contact dermatitis is delayed-type hypersensitivity to a small molecule, and its pattern maps the exposure.** Nickel, fragrances, preservatives and plant allergens penetrate the skin, bind self-proteins and are presented to T cells; on re-exposure, sensitised T cells produce an eczematous reaction over one to three days (type IV hypersensitivity) exactly where the chemical touched ([antigen presentation and MHC](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/antigen-presentation-and-mhc.md)). Irritant dermatitis from detergents and solvents needs no sensitisation and is the commonest occupational skin disease. Patch testing identifies the allergen, and avoidance cures ([sports, occupational and lifestyle medicine](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/sports-occupational-and-lifestyle-medicine.md)).

5. **Acne is a disease of the pilosebaceous unit under androgen drive.** At puberty, androgens enlarge sebaceous glands and increase sebum; follicular keratinocytes shed abnormally and block the duct; *Cutibacterium acnes* proliferates in the trapped lipid and triggers inflammation ([reproductive endocrinology](../../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/reproductive-endocrinology.md), [the human microbiome](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/01-microbes-and-infection/the-human-microbiome.md)). Treatments act at each step: retinoids normalise keratinisation, benzoyl peroxide and antibiotics reduce the bacteria, hormonal therapy reduces sebum, and oral isotretinoin shrinks the gland itself, which is why it alone produces lasting remission and why it is teratogenic. Scarring and psychological harm are the reasons to treat early.

6. **Treatment is matched to mechanism and to the surface area involved.** Topical glucocorticoids suppress inflammation locally but thin skin with prolonged use; ultraviolet phototherapy kills infiltrating T cells ([radiotherapy and radiation biology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/radiotherapy-and-radiation-biology.md)). For widespread disease, systemic immunosuppressants such as methotrexate give way to biologics blocking tumour necrosis factor, interleukin-17 or interleukin-23 in psoriasis, and interleukin-4/13 or Janus kinase signalling in eczema, each clearing the skin in most patients ([anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anti-inflammatory-and-immunomodulatory-drugs.md), [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md)). The specificity of the response confirms the mechanism: interleukin-17 blockers clear psoriasis and worsen inflammatory bowel disease, where the same cytokine protects the gut barrier ([specificity and recognition](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#specificity-and-recognition)).

7. **Who else is involved.** [Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) manages most eczema, acne and psoriasis and teaches the daily self-care they depend on ([chronic disease and self-management](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/chronic-disease-and-self-management.md)). [Rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md) shares psoriatic arthritis and the biologics; [gastroenterology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md) the inflammatory bowel disease that accompanies psoriasis; [clinical immunology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/allergy-and-immunodeficiency-in-practice.md) the atopic march; [paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) infantile eczema; [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md) the infections that complicate eczema and immunosuppression; [psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md) the depression that visible skin disease causes and that worsens it; [obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) the teratogenic drugs in women of reproductive age.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stratum corneum | Outer layer of dead keratinocytes and lipid forming the water and antigen barrier |
| Filaggrin | Structural protein of the barrier; loss-of-function variants predispose to eczema |
| Type 2 inflammation | Interleukin-4, -13 and -31 driven response with IgE and eosinophils; the eczema and allergy pathway |
| Interleukin-23/17 axis | Cytokine pathway driving psoriatic keratinocyte proliferation |
| Wheal | Transient dermal oedema from mast-cell release; the lesion of urticaria |
| Type IV hypersensitivity | Delayed T-cell-mediated reaction, as in allergic contact dermatitis |
| Patch test | Application of allergens to skin for two days to identify contact sensitisation |
| Pilosebaceous unit | Hair follicle with its sebaceous gland; the site of acne |

## Common misconceptions

- **"Eczema is an allergy."** It is a barrier defect with a type 2 immune bias; most flares are not caused by an identifiable allergen, and allergy testing rarely changes management.
- **"Psoriasis is a skin disease."** It is a systemic inflammatory disease with joint, vascular and metabolic consequences, and it is treated accordingly.
