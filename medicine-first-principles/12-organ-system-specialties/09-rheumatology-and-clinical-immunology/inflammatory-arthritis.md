## Essence

Inflammatory arthritis is the synovium turned into a chronically inflamed, invasive tissue that destroys the joint it was meant to lubricate. Rheumatoid arthritis is the model: an autoimmune process, primed years before symptoms by antibodies to modified self-proteins, settles in the synovial lining of many small joints symmetrically, and the inflamed tissue erodes cartilage and bone. The spondyloarthritides (ankylosing spondylitis, psoriatic and reactive arthritis) inflame where tendons meet bone and the spine, and respond to the body's repair with new bone rather than erosion. The clinical tasks follow from the biology: distinguish inflammatory from mechanical joint pain at the bedside, confirm with markers of inflammation and autoantibodies, and suppress the immune process early, because joint damage once done is permanent and the window in which treatment prevents it is measured in months.

## Core tenets

1. **Inflammatory joint pain is worse with rest and better with movement; mechanical pain is the reverse.** Inflammatory synovitis produces prolonged morning stiffness (over an hour), swelling that is soft and warm, and improvement with activity, because inflammatory exudate accumulates in a resting joint ([acute inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/acute-inflammation.md)). Mechanical pain from osteoarthritis stiffens briefly and worsens with load. This history distinction, with the pattern of joints involved, sorts most arthritis before any test.

2. **Rheumatoid arthritis begins in the immune system years before it reaches the joint.** Citrullination of proteins (conversion of arginine to citrulline, increased by smoking and periodontal infection) creates neo-antigens; in people carrying particular Human Leucocyte Antigen (HLA) DR4 alleles, these are presented to T cells and anti-citrullinated-protein antibodies appear, often a decade before symptoms ([autoimmunity](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/autoimmunity.md), [antigen presentation and MHC](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/antigen-presentation-and-mhc.md)). What finally localises the process to synovium is not fully known. Rheumatoid factor and anti-citrullinated-protein antibodies are the diagnostic serology, the latter more specific and predictive of erosive disease.

3. **The pannus erodes bone because inflammation hijacks the remodelling machinery.** Inflamed synovium thickens into pannus, a mass of macrophages, fibroblasts and lymphocytes producing tumour necrosis factor, interleukin-6 and interleukin-1. These cytokines induce RANKL on synovial fibroblasts, which drives osteoclast formation and bone erosion at the joint margin, and induce matrix metalloproteinases that digest cartilage ([bone remodelling and joints](../../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/bone-remodelling-and-joints.md), [chronic inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/chronic-inflammation-and-granulomas.md)). Erosions on radiographs are therefore the footprint of the disease; ultrasound and magnetic resonance imaging show synovitis before erosion ([magnetic resonance imaging](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/magnetic-resonance-imaging.md)). The same cytokines circulate: fatigue, anaemia of chronic disease, accelerated atherosclerosis and raised C-reactive protein are the systemic disease.

4. **The spondyloarthritides are a different anatomy and a different repair response.** They inflame the entheses (tendon and ligament insertions), the sacroiliac joints and the spine, associate strongly with HLA-B27 and with gut and skin inflammation (psoriasis, inflammatory bowel disease), and are seronegative. Where rheumatoid inflammation erodes, enthesitis heals with new bone, fusing the spine (ankylosis) and producing the bamboo spine of long-standing ankylosing spondylitis. Interleukin-17 and interleukin-23 dominate the cytokine profile, which is why drugs blocking them work here and not in rheumatoid arthritis ([specificity and recognition](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#specificity-and-recognition)).

5. **A single hot, swollen joint is septic arthritis until the fluid says otherwise.** Bacteria in a joint destroy cartilage within days, so acute monoarthritis demands aspiration: the fluid's cell count, crystals under polarised light and culture distinguish infection, gout and inflammatory flare ([microbiological diagnosis](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/microbiological-and-serological-diagnosis.md)). Patients on immunosuppression for arthritis are both more prone to joint infection and less able to show the usual signs.

6. **Treatment works by interrupting the cytokine network early, and the timing is what matters.** Glucocorticoids suppress inflammation broadly but at the cost of every tissue; conventional disease-modifying drugs such as methotrexate slow progression by mechanisms only partly understood; biologics block tumour necrosis factor, interleukin-6, T-cell co-stimulation or B cells; small molecules inhibit the Janus kinases through which cytokine receptors signal ([anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anti-inflammatory-and-immunomodulatory-drugs.md), [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md)). Trials of early aggressive treatment aimed at remission show less erosion and disability than delayed treatment, because erosion is irreversible and remission is attainable. The price is infection, including reactivated tuberculosis, which is why screening precedes biologics.

7. **Who else is involved.** Suspected inflammatory arthritis is referred urgently from [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md), which then shares drug monitoring. [Orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md) replaces destroyed joints and drains septic ones. [Dermatology](../11-dermatology/README.md) and [gastroenterology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md) share psoriatic and enteropathic arthritis; [ophthalmology](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md) treats the uveitis of spondyloarthritis. [Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md) matters because chronic inflammation doubles cardiovascular risk; [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md) because immunosuppression invites it; [pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md) because rheumatoid disease and methotrexate both affect the lung; [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md) and occupational therapy preserve function.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Synovitis | Inflammation of the synovial lining; the lesion of inflammatory arthritis |
| Pannus | Invasive inflamed synovial tissue that erodes cartilage and bone |
| Anti-citrullinated-protein antibodies | Autoantibodies against citrullinated self-proteins; specific for rheumatoid arthritis |
| Rheumatoid factor | Antibody against the constant region of immunoglobulin G; sensitive but not specific |
| Erosion | Loss of marginal bone on imaging; irreversible footprint of rheumatoid disease |
| Enthesitis | Inflammation where tendon or ligament inserts into bone; the lesion of spondyloarthritis |
| HLA-B27 | MHC class I allele strongly associated with ankylosing spondylitis |
| Seronegative | Lacking rheumatoid factor and anti-citrullinated-protein antibodies |
| Disease-modifying anti-rheumatic drug | Drug that slows structural damage, conventional or biologic |
| Treat-to-target | Escalating therapy until a measured remission or low-activity state is reached |

## Common misconceptions

- **"Rheumatoid arthritis is a disease of joints."** It is a systemic autoimmune disease whose commonest target is synovium; its excess mortality is cardiovascular.
- **"A negative rheumatoid factor excludes rheumatoid arthritis."** Up to a third of patients are seronegative, and the spondyloarthritides are seronegative by definition.
- **"Anti-inflammatory painkillers treat arthritis."** Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs relieve symptoms but do not slow erosion; only disease-modifying therapy changes the outcome.
