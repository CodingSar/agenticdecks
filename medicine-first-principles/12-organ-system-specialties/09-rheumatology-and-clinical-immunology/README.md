# Rheumatology and clinical immunology

## What this area is about

Rheumatology began as the medicine of painful joints and became the medicine of the immune system attacking the self, because the commonest cause of destructive joint disease turned out to be autoimmune. Clinical immunology extends the same expertise to the immune system's other two failures: attacking the harmless (allergy) and failing to attack the dangerous (immunodeficiency). The specialty spans the many with mechanical and crystal joint pain, whose problem is load and chemistry, and the fewer with systemic autoimmune disease, whose problem may be in any organ.

The unifying idea is that **the immune system has a set point, and disease is that set point lying in the wrong place for the target in front of it**. Too high against self is autoimmunity; too high against the environment is allergy; too low is immunodeficiency; and the drugs that correct one direction push toward the other, so every immunosuppressed autoimmune patient is an immunodeficient one. A second idea, for the joint leaves, is that structure on imaging and symptoms diverge, so the clinician treats the mechanism rather than the picture.

```
                          immune set point
      too low <-----------------+-----------------> too high
   immunodeficiency                        against self      against environment
   (primary, or from drugs)               autoimmunity           allergy
                                       /            \
                              synovium, enthesis    nucleus, vessel, fibroblast
                            inflammatory arthritis   CTD and vasculitis
                                        |
                        immunosuppression ---> pushes left (back to deficiency)

   separate axis: mechanical load and crystal chemistry
   osteoarthritis, gout, regional pain (no immune set-point fault)
```

Read inflammatory arthritis first for the distinction between inflammatory and mechanical disease and the model of early immunosuppression. Connective tissue disease extends the logic across organs. Osteoarthritis, gout and regional pain is the deliberate contrast: most joint pain is not immune. Allergy and immunodeficiency completes the set-point picture.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** chronic inflammation accelerates atherosclerosis, so rheumatoid and lupus patients die of vascular disease; pericarditis and Libman–Sacks valve lesions are lupus; giant cell arteritis causes aortic aneurysm.
- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** interstitial lung disease in systemic sclerosis and rheumatoid arthritis, pulmonary haemorrhage in vasculitis, asthma as allergy.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** lupus nephritis and ANCA glomerulonephritis are co-managed and biopsied by nephrologists; gout and kidney disease cause each other.
- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** inflammatory bowel disease shares spondyloarthritis and the anti-tumour-necrosis-factor drugs; gut dysmotility is systemic sclerosis.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** glucocorticoid-induced osteoporosis and diabetes; autoimmune thyroid and adrenal disease cluster with other autoimmunity.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** autoimmune cytopenias, antiphospholipid syndrome, and the differential of lymphoma for a swollen, feverish patient.
- **[Oncology](../07-oncology/README.md):** checkpoint inhibitors cause autoimmune disease; paraneoplastic syndromes mimic rheumatic disease; some immunosuppressants raise cancer risk.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** septic arthritis versus flare, infection in the immunosuppressed, screening for latent tuberculosis and hepatitis before biologics, reactive arthritis after infection.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** mononeuritis multiplex in vasculitis, neuropsychiatric lupus, myasthenia and myositis.
- **[Dermatology](../11-dermatology/README.md):** psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis, the lupus rash, scleroderma skin, palpable purpura and eczema are all shared ground, and the skin biopsy often makes the diagnosis.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Rheumatology **receives** suspected inflammatory arthritis urgently from [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md), because the window for preventing erosion is months, and sends back the stable patient for shared drug monitoring. It receives the undiagnosed multisystem patient from [nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md), [dermatology](../11-dermatology/README.md) and [ophthalmology](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md), each of whom has seen one organ of a systemic disease. It **refers** end-stage joints to [orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md) and function to [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md).

**Border conditions** have formal joint clinics: lupus nephritis and ANCA vasculitis with nephrology; psoriatic arthritis with dermatology; uveitis with ophthalmology; interstitial lung disease with [pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md); lupus and antiphospholipid pregnancy with [obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md). Juvenile arthritis and primary immunodeficiency begin in [paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md); chronic widespread pain is shared with [psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md); giant cell arteritis with [geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md).

**Emergencies cross the boundary**: the hot joint goes to [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) for aspiration; visual loss in giant cell arteritis to ophthalmology; the pulmonary–renal syndrome to [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md); anaphylaxis to emergency medicine and [anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md). The immunosuppressed patient with fever belongs to [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md) as much as to rheumatology.

## Connections across layers

The specialty rests on [adaptive immunity](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/README.md) and [immunity in disease](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/README.md) more directly than any other: tolerance, hypersensitivity, autoimmunity and immunodeficiency are its textbook. From physiology it takes [bone remodelling and joints](../../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/bone-remodelling-and-joints.md) and [pain](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/pain.md); from pathology [chronic inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/chronic-inflammation-and-granulomas.md) and [fibrosis](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/tissue-repair-regeneration-and-fibrosis.md); from pharmacology [anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anti-inflammatory-and-immunomodulatory-drugs.md) and [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md); from diagnostics the interpretation of autoantibodies against pre-test probability ([using tests](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/using-tests-pretest-probability-and-thresholds.md)). It gives upward the joint replacement that [orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md) performs, the chronic-disease model that [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/chronic-disease-and-self-management.md) runs, and the disability that [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md) addresses.
