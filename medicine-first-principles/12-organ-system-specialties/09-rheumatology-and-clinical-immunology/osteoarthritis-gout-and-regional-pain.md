## Essence

Most musculoskeletal pain is not autoimmune. Osteoarthritis is the failure of a joint as a mechanical organ: cartilage worn faster than it is repaired, bone remodelling in response, and pain from the innervated tissues around a cartilage that has none. Gout is a crystal disease: urate, the end product of purine metabolism that humans cannot break down further, precipitates in cool joints and triggers one of the most intense acute inflammatory reactions in medicine. Regional pain (back pain, tendinopathy, fibromyalgia) is mostly a disorder of load and pain processing in which imaging misleads. These account for most joint complaints, and the recurring lesson is that structural change on an image predicts pain poorly, so treatment addresses load, inflammation and the nervous system rather than the picture.

## Core tenets

1. **Osteoarthritis is failed repair of a whole joint under load, not simple wear.** Articular cartilage is avascular and its chondrocytes repair slowly; when mechanical stress (obesity, malalignment, previous injury) or age-related matrix change exceeds repair, the cartilage fibrillates and thins ([bone remodelling and joints](../../06-physiology/10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/bone-remodelling-and-joints.md), [diseases of aging](../../08-pathology/04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/diseases-of-aging-and-degeneration.md)). Subchondral bone thickens and forms osteophytes and the synovium is mildly inflamed. Radiographs show joint-space narrowing, sclerosis and osteophytes; the symptom is mechanical pain, worse with use, with brief stiffness, in knees, hips, hands and spine.

2. **Cartilage has no nerves, so osteoarthritic pain comes from everything else and tracks structure poorly.** Pain arises from bone, synovium, capsule and muscle, and is amplified centrally in long-standing disease ([pain](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/pain.md)). A quarter or more of people with radiographic knee osteoarthritis have no pain. Treatment therefore begins with the modifiable loads, weight loss and muscle strengthening, which improve pain as much as drugs, and joint replacement is offered for pain and function, not for the X-ray ([orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md)).

3. **Gout is a disease of urate solubility.** Humans lack uricase, so purine breakdown ends at urate, which circulates close to its solubility limit of roughly 0.4 mmol/L at 37 °C and is less soluble in cooler peripheral joints ([amino acid and nitrogen metabolism](../../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/02-metabolism/amino-acid-and-nitrogen-metabolism.md), [water, solutions and concentration](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/02-chemistry-of-life/water-solutions-and-concentration.md)). Nine in ten cases are renal under-excretion, worsened by diuretics and kidney disease. Crystals form in the joint over years; an attack is triggered when they are shed, often after dietary excess, surgery or a sudden change in urate level in either direction.

4. **The gout attack is the innate immune system treating a crystal as a pathogen.** Macrophages engulf urate crystals, which activate the NLRP3 inflammasome and release interleukin-1 beta, recruiting neutrophils within hours ([barriers and pattern recognition](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/02-innate-immunity/barriers-and-pattern-recognition.md), [acute inflammation](../../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/acute-inflammation.md)). The result is a joint, classically the first metatarsophalangeal, so painful and red that it mimics infection; the diagnosis is negatively birefringent needle-shaped crystals in aspirated fluid. The attack is self-limiting as the crystals are coated with masking proteins. Untreated over years, tophi erode bone and the kidney forms stones.

5. **Treating the attack and preventing the next are different drugs with different targets.** Colchicine, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, glucocorticoids and interleukin-1 blockers quell the attack. Preventing recurrence requires lowering serum urate below saturation for years so that crystals dissolve: xanthine oxidase inhibitors such as allopurinol block synthesis, uricosurics increase excretion ([endocrine and metabolic drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/endocrine-and-metabolic-drugs.md)). The common failure is to treat attacks forever without lowering urate, in a disease curable in principle. Calcium pyrophosphate deposition (pseudogout) is the other crystal arthritis, commoner in the old, with rhomboid positively birefringent crystals.

6. **Regional and chronic widespread pain is mostly a problem of load and pain processing, and imaging often harms.** Most acute low back pain is mechanical, resolves within weeks, and shows age-related disc changes on imaging that are equally common in people without pain; imaging is reserved for red flags (cancer, infection, fracture, cauda equina compression) because incidental findings drive unnecessary intervention ([using tests](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/using-tests-pretest-probability-and-thresholds.md)). Tendinopathy is overload and responds to graded loading, not rest. Fibromyalgia, widespread pain with fatigue and poor sleep, is amplified central pain processing ([pain](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/pain.md)); it responds to exercise and centrally acting drugs, not anti-inflammatories, and labelling it as damage worsens it.

7. **Who else is involved.** These conditions live mainly in [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) and [rehabilitation](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/rehabilitation-and-disability.md). [Orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md) replaces end-stage joints; [nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md) shares gout, which kidney disease causes and whose drugs affect the kidney; [cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md) and [endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md) share gout's company of hypertension and the metabolic syndrome; [geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) manages osteoarthritis as a driver of falls; [sports and occupational medicine](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/sports-occupational-and-lifestyle-medicine.md) manages load; [psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md) and pain services share chronic widespread pain.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Osteoarthritis | Whole-joint failure with cartilage loss, subchondral bone change and osteophytes |
| Mechanical pain | Pain worse with use and relieved by rest; brief stiffness |
| Hyperuricaemia | Serum urate above its solubility limit; necessary but not sufficient for gout |
| Monosodium urate crystal | Needle-shaped, negatively birefringent; diagnostic of gout in joint fluid |
| Inflammasome | Cytosolic sensor complex that activates interleukin-1 beta in response to crystals |
| Tophus | Chronic deposit of urate crystals in soft tissue or bone |
| Pseudogout | Calcium pyrophosphate crystal arthritis, rhomboid and positively birefringent |
| Central sensitisation | Amplification of pain signalling in the spinal cord and brain, as in fibromyalgia |

## Common misconceptions

- **"Osteoarthritis is wear and tear, so exercise wears it out."** Loading is what cartilage needs to stay nourished and muscle needs to protect the joint; inactivity worsens pain and function.
- **"Gout is caused by diet."** Diet shifts urate modestly; the dominant cause is genetically determined renal under-excretion, and diet alone rarely controls it.
- **"Back pain needs a scan."** In the absence of red flags, imaging finds changes that are normal for age, does not improve outcomes, and increases surgery and worry.
