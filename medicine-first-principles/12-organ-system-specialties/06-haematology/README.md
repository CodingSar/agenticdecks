## What this area is about

Haematology is the medicine of a liquid organ. Blood is made continuously in the marrow, destroyed and replaced at a rate that keeps its composition constant, and carries a clotting system poised to seal a wound and inhibitors poised to stop it. The questions are about steady states and balances: why there is too little of a cell type (production, destruction or loss), why blood clots where it should not or fails to clot where it should, what happens when a blood-forming clone escapes control, and how one person's blood can safely be given to another.

The idea that unifies the four children is that blood is a **high-turnover tissue read directly through the laboratory**. Because the cells are accessible with a needle, the mechanism of disease can be seen in a film, counted in a machine and sequenced in a day. Haematology is where general pathology ([neoplasia](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/README.md), [haemodynamic disorders](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/README.md)) meets the bedside with the least inference between mechanism and measurement.

```
            marrow (stem cell -> progenitors -> mature cells)
                 |                      |
      too few / too fragile         clone escapes
      (anaemia, cytopenias)      (leukaemia, lymphoma, myeloma)
                 \                      /
                  \---- marrow failure ----> transfusion support
                                 |
         plasma: platelets + cascade <-> inhibitors + fibrinolysis
                 bleeding  <----  balance  ---->  thrombosis
```

Read anaemias first: it establishes the production-versus-destruction method the other leaves reuse. Bleeding and clotting disorders apply the same balance to haemostasis. Haematological malignancies explain the commonest cause of marrow failure. Transfusion medicine is the common rescue for all three and the leaf where its costs are counted.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** anticoagulation for atrial fibrillation and antiplatelet therapy after stents are shared decisions; anaemia worsens angina and heart failure by lowering oxygen delivery.
- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** pulmonary embolism is the lung's share of venous thrombosis; chronic hypoxia drives secondary polycythaemia.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** the kidney makes erythropoietin, so kidney failure causes anaemia; myeloma light chains and haemolytic microangiopathies injure the kidney.
- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** the gut is the usual source of occult iron loss; the liver makes most clotting factors and clears bilirubin from haemolysis.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** iron overload from transfusion causes diabetes and hypogonadism; hypothyroidism causes a mild anaemia.
- **[Oncology](../07-oncology/README.md):** shares chemotherapy, radiotherapy, transplantation and trial infrastructure; solid tumours cause anaemia, thrombosis and marrow infiltration.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** neutropenic sepsis, infection in asplenia, malaria as a cause of haemolysis, and the donor screening that keeps blood safe.
- **[Rheumatology and clinical immunology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** autoimmune haemolysis, immune thrombocytopenia and the antiphospholipid syndrome sit between the two.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** stroke is thrombosis in the brain; B12 deficiency damages the spinal cord as well as the marrow.
- **[Dermatology](../11-dermatology/README.md):** purpura and petechiae are the skin's report of platelet and vessel problems; cutaneous lymphoma straddles both.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Haematology is both a clinical specialty and a laboratory service to everyone else. It **receives referrals** from [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) for unexplained anaemia, abnormal counts and bruising, from [gastroenterology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md) once a bleeding source is excluded, and from [obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) for thrombophilia and cytopenias in pregnancy. It **refers out** to gastroenterology and gynaecology to find the iron leak, to [nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md) when myeloma threatens the kidney, and to [palliative care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) when a malignancy is beyond cure.

**Border conditions** are decided jointly: lymphoma and myeloma are treated by haematologists, but solid-tumour marrow infiltration belongs to [oncology](../07-oncology/README.md); the two share multidisciplinary team meetings, radiotherapy and stem-cell transplant programmes. The anticoagulated patient is co-managed with [cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md) (atrial fibrillation, valves) and [neurology](../10-neurology/README.md) (stroke prevention and the timing of anticoagulation after a bleed). Autoimmune cytopenias and antiphospholipid syndrome are shared with [rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md).

**Emergencies cross the boundary** in both directions. Massive haemorrhage in [trauma surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md), [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and obstetrics is managed by a protocol that the transfusion laboratory runs in real time. Neutropenic sepsis and tumour lysis in a leukaemia patient bring in [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md). [Anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) depends on haematology to manage anaemia and anticoagulants before surgery. In [paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md), haemoglobinopathies, haemophilia and leukaemia are lifelong relationships handed over at adolescence.

## Connections across layers

Haematology takes its cell biology from [blood and haematopoiesis](../../05-tissues-and-anatomy/01-tissues/blood-and-haematopoiesis.md) and [stem cells](../../03-cell-biology/02-the-cell-life-cycle/stem-cells-differentiation-and-regeneration.md), its physiology from the [blood](../../06-physiology/08-blood/README.md) leaves, its immunology from [B cells](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/03-adaptive-immunity/b-cells-and-antibodies.md) and [hypersensitivity](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/hypersensitivity-and-allergy.md), its disease mechanisms from [neoplasia](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/README.md) and [thrombosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md), its drugs from [anticancer drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/anticancer-drugs.md) and [biologics](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/biologics-and-advanced-therapies.md), and its measurements from [haematology and coagulation testing](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/haematology-and-coagulation-testing.md). It gives upward the management of bleeding and anticoagulation that [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) need, the immunosuppressed host that [infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md) must protect, and the model of targeted and cellular therapy that [oncology](../07-oncology/README.md) follows.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): a slow anaemia is tolerated to low values; a fast one is shock.
- [Redundancy and reserve](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#redundancy-and-reserve): bleeding risk rises only when platelets are very low; the cascade has parallel paths.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): erythropoietin, hepcidin and thrombopoietin each close a loop on a measured output.
- [Specificity and recognition](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#specificity-and-recognition): blood-group antibodies, autoimmune cytopenias and lineage-targeted immunotherapy.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): anticoagulation and transfusion both trade one risk for another.
- [Supply and demand](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): anaemia is oxygen supply falling short of tissue demand.
