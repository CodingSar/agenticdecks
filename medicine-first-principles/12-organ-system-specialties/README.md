## What this area is about

This layer is the first in which the tree talks about *diseases* as a clinician meets them: a patient with breathlessness, a raised creatinine, a seizure, a rash. Everything below it is a description of a mechanism; everything here is a mechanism *in a particular person*, producing symptoms, signs and test results, and reversible (or not) by a treatment aimed at one link in the chain. The question each leaf answers is the same: given what we know about how this organ works (06), how it fails (08), what drugs do to it (09) and how we can observe it (11), what does a disease look like at the bedside and why does the treatment work?

The unifying idea is that **every organ-system disease is a physiological variable pushed outside its regulated range, plus the body's compensation for it**. Heart failure is low cardiac output plus salt retention; Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is expiratory airflow limitation plus hyperinflation; chronic kidney disease is lost nephrons plus hyperfiltration in the survivors; type 2 diabetes is insulin resistance plus beta-cell exhaustion. The symptoms are usually the cost of the compensation, the signs and tests measure the variable and the compensation, and the treatments that change outcomes are mostly those that interrupt a compensation that has become harmful. Reading the leaves with that template in mind turns a long list of diseases into a short list of patterns.

## Why this layer sits here

The layer sits directly on four foundations and would be unreadable without them. [06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md) supplies the normal variable and its control loop: the [cardiac cycle](../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/the-cardiac-cycle-and-pump-function.md), [ventilation–perfusion matching](../06-physiology/04-respiratory-system/ventilation-perfusion-matching.md), [glomerular filtration](../06-physiology/05-renal-system/glomerular-filtration-and-clearance.md), [glucose homeostasis](../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/glucose-homeostasis.md). [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) supplies the general mechanisms of failure ([atherosclerosis](../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/atherosclerosis.md), [inflammation](../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/acute-inflammation.md), [fibrosis](../08-pathology/01-cell-and-tissue-injury/tissue-repair-regeneration-and-fibrosis.md), [neoplasia](../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/hallmarks-of-cancer.md)) so each specialty can cite one mechanism rather than restate it. [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) supplies the drug classes by mechanism, and [11 Clinical method](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md) supplies the tests and the Bayesian logic for using them. [10 Epidemiology](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md) sits underneath every claim that a treatment "works": the specialties are where randomised trials are applied to organs.

What the layers above need from here is **the organ-system reasoning that acute care compresses and life-course care integrates**. [13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) is these specialties at speed: a resuscitation is cardiology, pulmonology and nephrology in the same ten minutes, and a surgeon's pre-operative risk is the sum of the patient's organ-system diseases. [14 Life course, mind and integrative care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) meets the same diseases in a child, a pregnant woman or a frail older adult with five of them at once, and primary care is where most of them are actually managed. The one abstraction this layer contributes is the **disease as a named, mechanistically explained, measurable and partly reversible deviation**, the unit in which clinicians, trials and guidelines all speak.

The specialties divide the body in two different ways at once. Children 01–05, 10 and 11 are **organ-defined**: each claims an organ or system and its physiology leaf. Children 07, 08 and 09 are **process-defined**: oncology by neoplasia, infectious diseases by pathogens, rheumatology by misdirected immunity, and each process can appear in any organ. Haematology (06) is both: it owns an organ (the marrow and the blood) and a process (haemostasis) that every other specialty depends on. The division is historical rather than logical, and the overlaps are where patients are shared and territory disputed: a lung cancer belongs to pulmonology until it is diagnosed, then to oncology; pneumonia is pulmonology's organ and infectious diseases' process; lupus nephritis is rheumatology's disease in nephrology's organ; a stroke is cardiology's embolus in neurology's organ.

The first four organ specialties are tightly coupled by a single circulation and a single extracellular fluid volume. The heart, lungs and kidneys fail together so often that each leaf on failure in one cites the other two: heart failure causes renal congestion, kidney disease causes hypertension and fluid overload, lung disease causes right heart failure, and cirrhosis causes both renal failure and a hyperdynamic circulation. Endocrinology sits across all of them as the chemical control system, and its commonest disease, diabetes, is the leading cause of chronic kidney disease, a major cause of ischaemic heart disease, and a reason patients see ophthalmology and vascular surgery. Read 01–05 as one story about the regulation of flow, volume and fuel; read 06–09 as the blood and the three processes that cross organ boundaries; read 10 and 11 as the two systems whose diseases are localised by *where* the lesion is.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** every disease leaf here is the failure of a variable defined there. The specialties add nothing to physiology; they apply it to a person whose compensation is running out.
- **[07 Microbiology and immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md):** infectious diseases and rheumatology are this layer's direct heirs; pneumonia, hepatitis, pyelonephritis, endocarditis and skin infection give every organ specialty an infectious chapter.
- **[08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md):** the general mechanisms (injury, inflammation, thrombosis, neoplasia, fibrosis) are stated once there and cited in every leaf here; systemic pathology lives inside this layer rather than in 08.
- **[09 Pharmacology and therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** the drug classes by mechanism become treatments by indication here; each leaf names what a class reverses rather than what to prescribe.
- **[11 Clinical method and diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** the specialties are the heaviest users of imaging, the laboratory and physiological testing, and each leaf says what a test shows and why.
- **[13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md):** critical care and emergency medicine are these specialties compressed in time; surgery takes over when the fix is mechanical (a valve, a stenosed artery, a perforated ulcer, a tumour).
- **[14 Life course, mind and integrative care](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md):** primary care manages most chronic organ-system disease and holds the leaf on [how the specialties interact](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/how-the-specialties-interact.md); paediatrics and geriatrics meet the same diseases in different bodies.

## Connections across layers

From below this layer takes a working model of each organ, the general mechanisms of injury, the pharmacological levers and the tests. To the layers above it gives named diseases with known natural histories and known responses to treatment, which acute care must recognise fast, surgery must operate around, and life-course care must manage over decades. The recurring lesson across all eleven children is that a specialty's boundary is an administrative convenience: the patient has one circulation, one immune system and one set of kidneys, and the border conditions (cardiorenal syndrome, hepatopulmonary syndrome, diabetic nephropathy, paraneoplastic syndromes) are where the best clinicians think across the line.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Compensation and decompensation](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): the defining theme of the layer; heart failure, kidney disease, COPD and cirrhosis are all compensation that has run out.
- [Supply and demand](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): ischaemic heart disease, stroke, respiratory failure and acute kidney injury are mismatches between perfusion or oxygen and need.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): endocrine disease is a set point moved or a loop broken; diagnosis reads the loop by measuring both hormone and its controller.
- [Injury and repair](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#injury-and-repair): fibrosis is the common end of interstitial lung disease, cirrhosis, glomerulosclerosis and myocardial scar.
- [Gradients and flow](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#gradients-and-flow): stenoses, pressure gradients and flow limitation define valvular disease, airway disease and portal hypertension.
- [Specificity and recognition](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#specificity-and-recognition): autoimmunity, allergy and targeted cancer therapy are recognition gone wrong or put to use.
- [Time courses](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): acute versus chronic versions of the same failure (acute kidney injury versus chronic kidney disease, asthma attack versus COPD) differ in reversibility.
- [Harm and benefit](../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): anticoagulation, immunosuppression and chemotherapy trade one risk against another in every specialty.
