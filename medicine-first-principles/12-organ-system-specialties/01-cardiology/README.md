## What this area is about

Cardiology is the medicine of a pump and its pipes. The heart has four jobs that can fail separately: it must be *perfused* (coronary flow), it must *contract and relax* (pump function), it must *keep time* (rhythm) and it must *direct flow one way* (valves and septa); the arterial tree it feeds must carry that flow at a pressure high enough to perfuse the brain and kidney but low enough not to destroy itself. Each child owns one of these failures. The question the specialty answers is: when a patient has chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, syncope or a raised blood pressure, which of the four jobs has failed, how far has compensation gone, and which link in the chain can a drug, a device or a procedure reverse?

The unifying idea is **supply, demand and compensation applied to one muscle**. Myocardium that is under-perfused dies ([ischaemia](ischemic-heart-disease.md)); muscle that is lost or overloaded is compensated by the neurohormonal reflexes that eventually destroy it ([heart failure](heart-failure.md)); scarred or stretched muscle conducts abnormally ([arrhythmias](arrhythmias.md)); a valve that leaks or narrows imposes a volume or pressure load that drives the same compensation ([valvular disease](valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md)); and a chronically raised pressure is the load that starts most of it ([hypertension](hypertension-and-vascular-disease.md)).

## How the children fit together

| Child | Its role in the whole | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [Ischaemic heart disease](ischemic-heart-disease.md) | Coronary supply fails to meet myocardial demand; the commonest cause of everything below | [Atherosclerosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/atherosclerosis.md), [thrombosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md) |
| [Heart failure](heart-failure.md) | The pump cannot meet demand at normal filling pressure; the final common pathway | Ischaemia, valvular disease, hypertension; [cardiac cycle](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/the-cardiac-cycle-and-pump-function.md) |
| [Arrhythmias](arrhythmias.md) | Electrical timing fails; the heart stops, races or fibrillates | [Cardiac electrophysiology](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/cardiac-electrophysiology.md); scar from ischaemia and failure |
| [Valvular and structural heart disease](valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md) | Mechanical faults that impose a load and are fixed mechanically | Cardiac cycle, [fluids and flow](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/fluids-pressure-and-flow.md) |
| [Hypertension and vascular disease](hypertension-and-vascular-disease.md) | The pressure load on the heart and the arterial wall; aneurysm and dissection | [Blood pressure regulation](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/hemodynamics-and-blood-pressure-regulation.md), [renal volume regulation](../../06-physiology/05-renal-system/sodium-water-and-volume-regulation.md) |

```
  hypertension --> LV pressure load --+
  atherosclerosis --> ischaemia -----> infarction, scar --+--> HEART FAILURE --> arrhythmia
  valve stenosis / regurgitation --> pressure or volume load ---+        ^              |
                                                                 |        |              v
                                                     neurohormonal compensation      sudden death
                                                      (sympathetic, RAAS, ADH)
```

Read hypertension and ischaemic heart disease first: they are the two commonest inputs. Heart failure is the common output and should be read next, because its neurohormonal story explains why drugs that lower blood pressure or block the sympathetic system also prolong life in failure. Arrhythmias follow, since most dangerous rhythms arise in ischaemic or failing muscle. Valvular disease stands slightly apart: it is the one place where the fix is mostly mechanical and the specialty meets cardiac surgery.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** the two circulations share the lungs. Left heart failure floods the alveoli; chronic lung disease and pulmonary emboli load the right ventricle ([pulmonary vascular disease](../02-pulmonology/pulmonary-vascular-disease-and-respiratory-failure.md)); breathlessness is the shared symptom that both specialties must disentangle.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** the kidney sets blood pressure and volume, and is damaged by the hypertension and low output it responds to; cardiorenal syndrome is the border condition.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** diabetes and dyslipidaemia drive atherosclerosis; thyroid disease causes arrhythmia; obesity and the metabolic syndrome are the upstream of most cardiology.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** antiplatelet and anticoagulant therapy are the two specialties' shared tool and shared hazard; anaemia precipitates angina and failure.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** infective endocarditis, rheumatic fever and myocarditis are infection in cardiology's organ.
- **[Rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** vasculitis, systemic lupus and chronic inflammation accelerate atherosclerosis and cause pericarditis.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** atrial fibrillation and carotid atherosclerosis cause stroke; syncope is disputed territory.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Cardiology shares patients in both directions with the acute and surgical specialties. [Emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) triages chest pain and runs [resuscitation](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/resuscitation-and-cardiac-arrest.md); the hand-over to cardiology is the decision to open an artery. [Critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) manages cardiogenic shock with inotropes and mechanical support, and the two specialties jointly decide when support is bridging to recovery and when it is futile. [Cardiac surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) is the mechanical arm: bypass grafting, valve repair and replacement, and aortic repair are decided in a joint "heart team" meeting, which is the model multidisciplinary team in cardiology. The border has moved as catheter techniques (coronary stenting, transcatheter valve implantation) do by wire what once needed a sternotomy. [Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) asks cardiology to quantify risk before non-cardiac surgery, and the honest answer is usually "optimise what is reversible, and do not delay the operation for tests that will not change it".

Across layer 14, [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) detects and manages most hypertension, most stable angina and most chronic heart failure, referring for a diagnosis, a procedure or a decompensation. [Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares women with congenital or valvular disease, in whom pregnancy's 40–50% rise in cardiac output is a stress test. [Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) owns congenital heart disease until adolescence, and the transition to adult congenital services is a hand-over that often fails. [Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) manages the same diseases in people whose frailty changes the harm–benefit balance of every drug and device, and palliative care takes over when a failing heart is the terminal illness; deactivating a defibrillator at the end of life is a decision made jointly. The emergency that crosses every boundary is cardiac arrest, which can start in any ward and ends in cardiology or critical care.

## Connections across layers

From [06 Physiology](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/README.md) cardiology takes the cardiac cycle, electrophysiology and blood pressure regulation; from [08 Pathology](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/README.md) atherosclerosis, thrombosis, infarction and oedema; from [09 Pharmacology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/autonomic-and-cardiovascular-drugs.md) the autonomic and cardiovascular drug classes; from [11 Diagnostics](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/electrocardiography-and-electrophysiological-testing.md) the Electrocardiogram (ECG), echocardiography and troponin. It gives [13](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) the physiology of shock and arrest, and [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) the chronic disease that primary care most often manages.

- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): the neurohormonal response to low output sustains it for years and then kills.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): baroreceptors and the renin–angiotensin system set blood pressure; hypertension is a set point moved.

- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): anticoagulation in atrial fibrillation weighs stroke against bleeding, patient by patient.
