## What this area is about

Cardiology is the medicine of a pump and its pipes. The heart has four jobs that can fail separately: to be *perfused* (coronary flow), to *contract and relax* (pump function), to *keep time* (rhythm) and to *direct flow one way* (valves and septa); the arterial tree must carry that flow at a pressure high enough to perfuse brain and kidney but low enough not to destroy itself. Each child owns one of these failures. The question the specialty answers is: when a patient has chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, syncope or a raised blood pressure, which job has failed, how far has compensation gone, and which link can a drug, a device or a procedure reverse?

The unifying idea is **supply, demand and compensation applied to one muscle**. Under-perfused myocardium dies ([ischaemia](ischemic-heart-disease.md)); lost or overloaded muscle is compensated by neurohormonal reflexes that eventually destroy it ([heart failure](heart-failure.md)); scarred or stretched muscle conducts abnormally ([arrhythmias](arrhythmias.md)); a leaking or narrowed valve imposes a load that drives the same compensation ([valvular disease](valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md)); and raised pressure is the load that starts most of it ([hypertension](hypertension-and-vascular-disease.md)).

## How the children fit together

| Child | Its role in the whole | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [Ischaemic heart disease](ischemic-heart-disease.md) | Coronary supply fails to meet myocardial demand; the commonest cause of everything below | [Atherosclerosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/atherosclerosis.md), [thrombosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md) |
| [Heart failure](heart-failure.md) | The pump cannot meet demand at normal filling pressure; the final common pathway | Ischaemia, valvular disease, hypertension; [cardiac cycle](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/the-cardiac-cycle-and-pump-function.md) |
| [Arrhythmias](arrhythmias.md) | Electrical timing fails; the heart stops, races or fibrillates | [Cardiac electrophysiology](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/cardiac-electrophysiology.md); scar from ischaemia and failure |
| [Valvular and structural heart disease](valvular-and-structural-heart-disease.md) | Mechanical faults that impose a load and are fixed mechanically | Cardiac cycle, [fluids and flow](../../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/01-physics-of-the-body/fluids-pressure-and-flow.md) |
| [Hypertension and vascular disease](hypertension-and-vascular-disease.md) | The pressure load on the heart and the arterial wall; aneurysm and dissection | [Blood pressure regulation](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/hemodynamics-and-blood-pressure-regulation.md), [renal volume regulation](../../06-physiology/05-renal-system/sodium-water-and-volume-regulation.md) |

```
  hypertension --> LV pressure load --+
  atherosclerosis --> ischaemia -----> infarction, scar --+--> HEART FAILURE --> arrhythmia
  valve stenosis / regurgitation --> pressure or volume load ---+        ^              |
                                                                 |        |              v
                                                     neurohormonal compensation      sudden death
                                                      (sympathetic, renin-angiotensin, antidiuretic hormone)
```

Read hypertension and ischaemic heart disease first: they are the two commonest inputs. Heart failure is the common output and comes next, because its neurohormonal story explains why drugs that lower blood pressure or block the sympathetic system prolong life in failure. Arrhythmias follow, since most dangerous rhythms arise in ischaemic or failing muscle. Valvular disease stands apart: the fix is mostly mechanical and the specialty meets cardiac surgery.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Pulmonology](../02-pulmonology/README.md):** left heart failure floods the alveoli; chronic lung disease and pulmonary emboli load the right ventricle ([pulmonary vascular disease](../02-pulmonology/pulmonary-vascular-disease-and-respiratory-failure.md)); breathlessness is the shared symptom to disentangle.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** the kidney sets blood pressure and volume, and is damaged by the hypertension and low output it responds to; cardiorenal syndrome is the border condition.
- **[Endocrinology](../05-endocrinology/README.md):** diabetes and dyslipidaemia drive atherosclerosis; thyroid disease causes arrhythmia; obesity is upstream of most cardiology.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** antiplatelet and anticoagulant therapy are the shared tool and hazard; anaemia precipitates angina and failure.
- **[Infectious diseases](../08-infectious-diseases/README.md):** [infective endocarditis](../08-infectious-diseases/common-bacterial-infections-by-site.md), rheumatic fever and myocarditis are infection in cardiology's organ.
- **[Rheumatology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** vasculitis, lupus and chronic inflammation accelerate atherosclerosis and cause pericarditis.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** atrial fibrillation and carotid atherosclerosis cause stroke; syncope is disputed territory.

## Interfaces with other specialties

[Emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) triages chest pain and runs [resuscitation](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/resuscitation-and-cardiac-arrest.md); the hand-over to cardiology is the decision to open an artery. [Critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) manages cardiogenic shock, and the two jointly decide when support is a bridge to recovery and when it is futile. [Cardiac surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) is the mechanical arm: bypass grafting, valve surgery and aortic repair are decided in a joint "heart team", the model multidisciplinary team. The border moves as catheter techniques (stenting, transcatheter valves) do by wire what once needed a sternotomy. [Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) asks cardiology to quantify risk before non-cardiac surgery; the useful answer is to optimise what is reversible and not delay the operation for tests that will not change it.

[Primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) manages most hypertension, stable angina and chronic heart failure, referring for a diagnosis, a procedure or a decompensation. [Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares women with congenital or valvular disease, for whom pregnancy's 40–50% rise in output is a stress test. [Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) owns congenital heart disease until adolescence; the transition to adult congenital services is a hand-over that often fails. [Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) manages the same diseases in people whose frailty changes the harm–benefit balance of every drug and device, and palliative care takes over when a failing heart is the terminal illness; deactivating a defibrillator at the end of life is a joint decision. The emergency that crosses every boundary is cardiac arrest, which can start on any ward.

## Connections across layers

From [06 Physiology](../../06-physiology/03-cardiovascular-system/README.md) cardiology takes the cardiac cycle, electrophysiology and pressure regulation; from [08 Pathology](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/README.md) atherosclerosis, thrombosis, infarction and oedema; from [09 Pharmacology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/autonomic-and-cardiovascular-drugs.md) the cardiovascular drug classes; from [11 Diagnostics](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/electrocardiography-and-electrophysiological-testing.md) the Electrocardiogram (ECG), echocardiography and troponin. It gives [13](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) the physiology of shock and arrest, and [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) the chronic disease primary care most often manages.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Supply and demand](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#supply-and-demand): coronary flow versus myocardial oxygen consumption is the whole of ischaemic heart disease.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): the neurohormonal response to low output sustains it for years and then kills.
- [Feedback and homeostasis](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): baroreceptors and the renin–angiotensin system set blood pressure; hypertension is a set point moved.
- [Gradients and flow](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#gradients-and-flow): a stenotic valve is a pressure gradient, measured by Doppler as velocity.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): anticoagulation in atrial fibrillation weighs stroke against bleeding, patient by patient.
