## What this area is about

Endocrinology is the medicine of chemical control. Hormones are signals released into the blood by one tissue to set the behaviour of others, and each is governed by a feedback loop: a sensor measures the controlled variable or the hormone itself, and a controller (often the hypothalamus and pituitary) adjusts secretion. The diseases are therefore of three kinds: too little hormone (a gland destroyed, usually by autoimmunity), too much (a gland or tumour secreting autonomously, outside the loop), and resistance (the target tissue no longer answers, as in type 2 diabetes). The specialty's question is: which loop is broken, where in the loop, and is the deviation primary (the gland) or secondary (its controller)?

The unifying idea is **the feedback loop read by measuring two points at once**. A low thyroxine with a high thyroid-stimulating hormone means the thyroid has failed and the pituitary is responding correctly; a low thyroxine with a low stimulating hormone means the pituitary has failed. A high cortisol that falls when the axis is suppressed is still under control; one that does not is autonomous. Nearly every endocrine diagnosis is a paired measurement or a dynamic test of this kind, and nearly every treatment is one of two things: replace the missing hormone, or remove, block or destroy the source of the excess. Diabetes and obesity dominate the specialty by numbers and sit at the border with cardiology, nephrology and primary care; the rarer axis disorders are where the loop logic is clearest.

Read thyroid disease first, even though diabetes is commoner, because the thyroid axis shows the loop logic in its purest form and every other leaf applies it. Diabetes and the obesity–lipid leaf form a pair: one is the mechanism, the other its commonest cause and companion. The adrenal–pituitary–gonadal leaf extends the loop logic to the hardest cases, where the controller itself fails. Bone and calcium stands slightly apart, with its own hormones and a skeleton that is also the concern of rheumatology and orthopaedics.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Cardiology](../01-cardiology/README.md):** diabetes, dyslipidaemia and obesity drive atherosclerosis; thyrotoxicosis causes atrial fibrillation; aldosterone and catecholamine excess cause hypertension. Much of cardiology's prevention is endocrinology's disease.
- **[Nephrology](../03-nephrology/README.md):** diabetes is the leading cause of kidney failure; the kidney activates vitamin D, clears insulin and is the target of aldosterone and antidiuretic hormone; kidney failure causes secondary hyperparathyroidism and bone disease.
- **[Gastroenterology and hepatology](../04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md):** fatty liver is the hepatic face of the metabolic syndrome; pancreatitis and pancreatic surgery cause diabetes; coeliac disease and autoimmune thyroid disease and type 1 diabetes cluster in the same patients.
- **[Haematology](../06-haematology/README.md):** pernicious anaemia clusters with autoimmune thyroid and adrenal disease; haemochromatosis causes diabetes and hypogonadism by iron deposition.
- **[Oncology](../07-oncology/README.md):** endocrine tumours (thyroid, pituitary, adrenal, neuroendocrine) and hormone-driven cancers (breast, prostate) are shared; immune checkpoint inhibitors cause autoimmune hypophysitis, thyroiditis and diabetes; hypercalcaemia of malignancy is an endocrine emergency.
- **[Rheumatology and clinical immunology](../09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md):** corticosteroid treatment causes osteoporosis, diabetes and adrenal suppression; the two specialties share the bone.
- **[Neurology](../10-neurology/README.md):** pituitary tumours compress the optic chiasm; diabetes causes neuropathy; hypoglycaemia and hyponatraemia cause seizures.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Endocrinology's surgical partners are defined by the gland: [endocrine surgeons](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) remove thyroid, parathyroid and adrenal glands, and neurosurgeons remove pituitary tumours through the nose; in each case endocrinology makes the diagnosis, decides that surgery is the right treatment, prepares the patient (rendering a thyrotoxic patient euthyroid, blocking a phaeochromocytoma's catecholamines) and manages the replacement afterwards, so the relationship is a sequence rather than a referral. [Vascular surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/surgical-subspecialties.md) and [orthopaedics](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/orthopaedics-fractures-and-joints.md) share the diabetic foot, where a joint clinic of endocrinologist, surgeon, podiatrist and microbiologist halves amputation rates, and the fragility fracture, where the orthopaedic fixation is followed by endocrine assessment of why the bone broke. [Ophthalmology](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/05-ophthalmology-and-ent/the-eye-and-vision-loss.md) screens and treats diabetic retinopathy and manages thyroid eye disease. [Anaesthesia and perioperative medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) need endocrinology for the diabetic patient fasting for surgery and for anyone on long-term corticosteroids, whose suppressed adrenal cannot mount the stress response and must be covered. [Emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) manage the endocrine emergencies, diabetic ketoacidosis, adrenal crisis, thyroid storm, myxoedema coma and severe hypercalcaemia, before the specialty arrives.

In layer 14, [primary care](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md) manages the large majority of type 2 diabetes, hypothyroidism, obesity and osteoporosis, and [chronic disease and self-management](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/05-primary-care-and-integration/chronic-disease-and-self-management.md) was largely designed around diabetes; referral is for type 1 diabetes, pregnancy, complications and the rarer axis disorders. [Obstetrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) shares gestational diabetes, thyroid disease in pregnancy and polycystic ovary syndrome, and [fertility services](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/fertility-contraception-and-menopause.md) share the gonadal axis. [Paediatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/01-paediatrics/README.md) owns type 1 diabetes, growth and puberty disorders in childhood, with a transition clinic in adolescence that is a known point of failure. [Psychiatry](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/03-psychiatry/README.md) shares eating disorders, the metabolic effects of antipsychotics, and the depression and anxiety that thyroid and adrenal disease mimic. [Geriatrics](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) shares the frail diabetic, in whom hypoglycaemia is more dangerous than hyperglycaemia and the targets are relaxed.

## Connections across layers

From [06 Physiology](../../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/README.md) the specialty takes the axes, glucose homeostasis, calcium metabolism and energy balance; from [01](../../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/feedback-and-control.md) the control theory that makes paired measurements diagnostic; from [02](../../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/02-metabolism/README.md) metabolism and lipid transport; from [07](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/04-immunity-in-disease/autoimmunity.md) the autoimmunity that destroys most glands; from [09 Pharmacology](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/endocrine-and-metabolic-drugs.md) the hormones, their antagonists and the metabolic drugs; from [11](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/02-laboratory-medicine/clinical-chemistry-and-reference-ranges.md) immunoassays and their pitfalls. It gives [13](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) the stress response and its failure, and [14](../../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) its model chronic disease.
