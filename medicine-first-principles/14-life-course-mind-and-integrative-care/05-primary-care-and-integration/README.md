## What this area is about

This area is about the parts of medicine defined by the whole person rather than by any disease: the generalist who is the first contact and the constant across a lifetime, the long management of chronic disease that happens mostly in the patient's own hands, the restoration of function after the lesion is fixed, the exposures of exercise, work and daily living, and, finally, the structure of the specialty graph itself. It answers the question every other specialty leaves open: who holds the whole, and how do the parts connect?

The unifying idea is **integration**. Every specialty in layers 12 and 13 and every other area in layer 14 produces a partial view of a patient; primary care is where the views are reconciled, continuity is kept and the base rates of unselected illness are known. The capstone leaf, [how the specialties interact](how-the-specialties-interact.md), is the knowledge base's own account of its upper layers as a graph rather than a tree, and the whole tree points at it.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Paediatrics](../01-paediatrics/README.md):** general practice and health visiting deliver child surveillance, immunisation and the management of most childhood illness; the transition from paediatric to adult services is a hand-over that primary care bridges.
- **[Obstetrics and gynaecology](../02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md):** contraception, cervical screening, routine antenatal care, menopause and most gynaecological first presentations are primary care; occupational medicine assesses work in pregnancy.
- **[Psychiatry](../03-psychiatry/README.md):** primary care treats most depression, anxiety and substance misuse, manages the physical health of people with severe mental illness, and holds the mind–body interface of unexplained symptoms; psychosocial rehabilitation is rehabilitation.
- **[Geriatrics and palliative care](../04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** the frail live at home and in care homes under primary care; rehabilitation treats the geriatric syndromes; most palliative care and advance care planning happen in the community.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Primary care is the one specialty with an edge to every other, so its interfaces are the subject of an entire leaf ([how the specialties interact](how-the-specialties-interact.md)). In summary: it **refers** to every organ-system specialty of [layer 12](../../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) when diagnostic uncertainty, treatment beyond scope or a procedure requires it, and **receives back** the patient with a plan to monitor; it shares the chronic diseases with [endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md), [cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md), [pulmonology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/README.md), [nephrology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md) and [neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md) under shared-care agreements; it sends the acutely ill to [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) and receives the discharged patient from [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md), [anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) and [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) with the medicines reconciliation and follow-up that discharge requires; it performs the first assessment of skin lesions, eye and ear complaints that go on to [dermatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/11-dermatology/README.md) and [ophthalmology and ENT](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/05-ophthalmology-and-ent/README.md); and it supplies the suspected-cancer referral that starts the [oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/README.md) pathway and the palliative care at its end.

Rehabilitation medicine interfaces with neurology and stroke, orthopaedics, cardiology and pulmonology (cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation), critical care (post-intensive-care syndrome), oncology (survivorship) and [rheumatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md). Sports medicine shares the athlete with orthopaedics, rheumatology, cardiology and neurology; occupational medicine shares its patients with pulmonology, dermatology, ENT, oncology, psychiatry and the law; lifestyle medicine with endocrinology, cardiology and public health.

**Disputed territory** includes the boundary between primary and secondary care itself (which conditions are referred, which drugs are initiated by whom), the management of the multimorbid older patient (primary care, geriatrics or acute medicine), back pain (primary care, orthopaedics, rheumatology, pain medicine, physiotherapy), medically unexplained symptoms (primary care, psychiatry, neurology, gastroenterology) and the menopause (primary care, gynaecology, endocrinology). **Joint decisions** are made in shared-care protocols, community multidisciplinary meetings for the frail and the dying, and the safeguarding meeting. **Emergencies that cross the boundary** are the ones primary care recognises and sends on: sepsis, stroke, myocardial infarction, the acute abdomen, the suicidal patient, the collapsed child.

## Connections across layers

From below this area takes [Bayesian reasoning](../../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/01-probability-and-measurement/bayesian-reasoning.md) and [feedback and control](../../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/feedback-and-control.md) from the quantitative foundations (the generalist's test logic and the self-management loop), [exercise physiology](../../06-physiology/01-integrative-principles/exercise-and-stress-physiology.md) and [motor control](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/motor-control.md) from physiology, [natural history](../../08-pathology/05-disease-as-process/etiology-pathogenesis-and-natural-history.md) and [environmental injury](../../08-pathology/04-genetic-metabolic-and-environmental-disease/environmental-and-toxic-injury.md) from pathology, [behaviour change](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/behaviour-change-and-the-placebo-effect.md) from therapeutics, [prevention and risk prediction](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/README.md) from epidemiology, and the whole of the [clinical encounter](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/README.md). It consumes every specialty of [layer 12](../../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md) and [layer 13](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) as nodes in its graph. To [medicine in society](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) it gives the evidence that systems built on primary care have better outcomes at lower cost, the design problem of integration that [health systems](../../15-medicine-in-society/01-health-systems/README.md) and informatics must solve, and the finding that behaviour and disability are shaped by [social conditions](../../15-medicine-in-society/03-populations-and-the-future/social-determinants-and-health-inequity.md) more than by advice.
