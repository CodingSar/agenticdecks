## What this area is about

Psychiatry is the medicine of disorders of mood, thought, perception, behaviour and the control of appetite and impulse: the properties of a whole person rather than of an organ. It answers the question that neurology does not: what is wrong when the brain is structurally intact on every test we have, yet the person's experience and conduct have gone persistently wrong? Mental disorders account for a large share of global disability, begin mostly before the age of 25, shorten life by a decade or more through the body as well as through suicide, and are treated, for the most part, outside psychiatric services.

The unifying idea is **explanation at three levels that do not reduce to one another**. Neuroscience supplies mechanisms (dopamine and salience, monoamines and plasticity, the fear circuit, the reward-learning system) and pharmacology acts on them; psychology supplies learned patterns of belief, avoidance and relationship, and psychotherapy acts on those; and the social world supplies adversity, isolation, poverty and culture, which social intervention and policy act on. A psychiatric formulation holds all three and asks what predisposed, precipitated and perpetuates this person's illness. The brain is necessary to every explanation and sufficient for none.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Paediatrics](../01-paediatrics/README.md):** child and adolescent psychiatry sits across both areas; most mental illness begins in adolescence, neurodevelopmental disorders are diagnosed in childhood, and safeguarding is shared.
- **[Obstetrics and gynaecology](../02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md):** perinatal psychiatry manages depression and psychosis around birth and the risks of psychotropic drugs in pregnancy; infertility, loss and menopause carry psychological weight.
- **[Geriatrics and palliative care](../04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** old age psychiatry shares delirium, dementia and late-life depression; palliative care distinguishes depression from appropriate grief and manages terminal agitation.
- **[Primary care and integration](../05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** general practice diagnoses and treats most depression, anxiety and substance misuse; the mind–body interface of medically unexplained symptoms and chronic disease with depression is daily work there.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Psychiatry's closest and most disputed border is with [neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md): the two split in the nineteenth century over whether a lesion could be found, and the border is drawn through dementia (shared), epilepsy with psychosis or depression, functional neurological disorder (seizures and paralysis without structural cause, now a joint territory), Parkinson disease psychosis from dopaminergic drugs, autoimmune encephalitis presenting as psychosis, and Huntington disease. The rule of hand-over is that a new psychiatric presentation with cognitive clouding, focal signs, late onset or rapid course goes to neurology first.

**Liaison psychiatry** works inside the general hospital, where a third or more of inpatients have a mental disorder, and interfaces with every medical specialty: delirium on the [geriatric](../04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) and surgical wards, depression in [cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md), [oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/README.md) and [nephrology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md) dialysis units, capacity assessments before [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md), and self-harm and overdose in [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md), which is also the entry point for acute psychosis and intoxication. [Critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) and psychiatry share severe overdose, delirium tremens, neuroleptic malignant syndrome, serotonin syndrome and the post-intensive-care syndrome of anxiety and post-traumatic stress; [anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) delivers electroconvulsive therapy and manages the opioid-tolerant or substance-dependent patient.

Psychiatry **refers** to [endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md) (thyroid, adrenal and prolactin disorders as causes and as drug effects; the metabolic syndrome of antipsychotics), to [gastroenterology and hepatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md) and [infectious diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/README.md) for alcohol-related liver disease, hepatitis C and HIV in substance users, to [haematology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md) for clozapine monitoring and lithium effects, and to [pulmonology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/02-pulmonology/README.md) for the respiratory disease of a population that smokes heavily. It **receives** from every specialty the patient whose symptoms outrun their findings, and from obstetrics the perinatal patient.

**Multidisciplinary decisions** are the community mental health team (psychiatrist, nurse, psychologist, social worker, occupational therapist), the eating-disorder team with dietetics and paediatrics or gastroenterology, the memory clinic with geriatrics and neurology, and the mental health tribunal where law reviews detention ([consent, capacity and confidentiality](../../15-medicine-in-society/02-ethics-law-and-the-profession/consent-capacity-and-confidentiality.md)). **Emergencies that cross the boundary** are suicidal overdose (emergency, critical care, psychiatry), acute behavioural disturbance (emergency, anaesthesia for sedation, psychiatry), delirium tremens (acute medicine, critical care), refeeding syndrome (medicine, dietetics, psychiatry) and postpartum psychosis (obstetrics, psychiatry, paediatrics).

## Connections across layers

From below, psychiatry takes [synaptic transmission](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/synaptic-transmission-and-neurotransmitters.md), [higher functions](../../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/higher-functions-memory-language-and-consciousness.md), the [stress axis](../../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/thyroid-adrenal-and-stress-hormones.md) and [sleep](../../06-physiology/01-integrative-principles/sleep-and-circadian-rhythms.md) from physiology; [complex traits and heritability](../../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/01-inheritance/complex-traits-and-heritability.md) and [evolutionary medicine](../../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/02-evolution/evolutionary-medicine.md) (why fear, low mood and reward-seeking exist) from layer 04; [central nervous system drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/central-nervous-system-drugs.md), [tolerance and dependence](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/02-pharmacodynamics/tolerance-dependence-and-withdrawal.md) and [behaviour change and the placebo effect](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/behaviour-change-and-the-placebo-effect.md) from pharmacology; [randomised trials](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/02-evidence-based-medicine/randomized-controlled-trials.md) and [risk prediction](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/risk-prediction-and-stratification.md) from evidence; and [history taking](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/history-taking-and-communication.md) and [clinical reasoning](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/clinical-reasoning-and-cognitive-bias.md) from the clinical method. To the layers above it gives [medicine in society](../../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) its hardest problems in consent, capacity, compulsion and stigma, and the clearest evidence that [social determinants](../../15-medicine-in-society/03-populations-and-the-future/social-determinants-and-health-inequity.md) are causes of disease.
