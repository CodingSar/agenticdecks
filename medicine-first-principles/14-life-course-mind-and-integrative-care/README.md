# 14 Life course, mind and integrative care

> **Layer:** 14 Life course, mind and integrative care
> **Children:** [Paediatrics](01-paediatrics/README.md) · [Obstetrics and gynaecology](02-obstetrics-and-gynaecology/README.md) · [Psychiatry](03-psychiatry/README.md) · [Geriatrics and palliative care](04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md) · [Primary care and integration](05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md)

## What this area is about

The specialties of layer 12 each own an organ system and those of layer 13 own an acute setting or a technique. The specialties here own something else: a **particular body** (the child, the pregnant woman, the older adult), a **particular domain** (the mind) or a **particular relationship and setting** (the generalist across a lifetime, the rehabilitation team, the bedside of the dying). Each of them spans every organ system and uses every acute specialty, but in a host whose physiology, pharmacology, capacity to consent and goals differ from the adult of the textbook, or with a problem, the person's experience and conduct, that no organ system contains.

Two ideas unify the layer. The first is **the life course**: the body is built over two decades, runs on reserve for several more and loses that reserve at the end, so the same disease presents, behaves and is treated differently at each stage, and the stages hand patients to one another (the fetus to the neonatologist, the adolescent to adult services, the frail adult to palliative care). The second is **integration**: medicine's division of labour produces partial views, and someone must hold the whole. Primary care is that integrator, and its capstone leaf, [how the specialties interact](05-primary-care-and-integration/how-the-specialties-interact.md), describes layers 12, 13 and 14 as the graph they really are.

## Why this layer sits here

This layer sits above 12 and 13 because it uses them in a different body. Paediatric cardiology is [cardiology](../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md) applied to a transitional circulation; obstetric medicine is [nephrology](../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md), [endocrinology](../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md) and [haematology](../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md) applied to a reset physiology; geriatrics is all of layer 12 applied to a body with no reserve and a dozen drugs; a neonatal unit or an obstetric haemorrhage is [critical care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) and [anaesthesia](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) with different numbers. Psychiatry needs [neurology](../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md) as its nearest neighbour and the whole of medicine to exclude organic mimics. Primary care cannot be described until the specialties it refers to and integrates exist.

From below it takes, directly, [development and the life course](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/03-development/README.md) (growth, puberty, ageing, teratology), [fetal and neonatal physiology](../06-physiology/09-reproduction-and-the-life-course/fetal-and-neonatal-physiology.md), [reproductive endocrinology](../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/reproductive-endocrinology.md) and [pregnancy physiology](../06-physiology/09-reproduction-and-the-life-course/pregnancy-placenta-and-lactation.md), the [neuroscience](../06-physiology/02-nervous-system/README.md) on which psychiatry rests without being reduced to it, [pharmacokinetics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/01-pharmacokinetics/README.md) at the extremes of size and age, [behaviour change](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/04-non-drug-therapeutics/behaviour-change-and-the-placebo-effect.md), [prevention and screening](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/README.md), and the [clinical encounter](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/README.md), including [Bayesian reasoning at low prevalence](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/01-the-clinical-encounter/using-tests-pretest-probability-and-thresholds.md).

What the layer above needs from it is **the person and the population**: [medicine in society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) builds health systems on primary care, writes law around capacity, consent, compulsion and the end of life, allocates resources across the life course, and measures its success in maternal mortality, child survival, healthy life expectancy and the burden of mental illness. The abstraction this layer contributes to the stack is the **whole patient over time**: the unit of care is a person with a history, a trajectory, a context and a set of goals, not a diagnosis.

## Connections to neighbouring layers

- **[04 Genetics, evolution and development](../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/README.md):** supplies the timetable of growth, puberty and ageing, the mechanisms of malformation and inheritance that paediatrics and fetal medicine act on, and the evolutionary reasons why birth is hazardous and why fear and low mood exist.
- **[06 Physiology](../06-physiology/README.md):** supplies the fetal, neonatal, pregnant and ageing variants of every system, reproductive endocrinology, and the neuroscience of mood, salience, reward and fear.
- **[09 Pharmacology and therapeutics](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md):** pharmacokinetics at the extremes of age, teratology, psychotropic drugs, tolerance and dependence, behaviour change and the placebo effect are all used here more than anywhere.
- **[10 Epidemiology and evidence](../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/README.md):** screening (newborn, antenatal, cervical), prevention, the exclusion of children, pregnant women and the old from trials, and the population measures that judge maternal and child health.
- **[11 Clinical method and diagnostics](../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/README.md):** the consultation with a parent or carer, the mental state examination as a form of examination, reasoning at low prevalence, prognosis and shared decision-making; ultrasound as obstetrics' principal instrument.
- **[12 Organ-system specialties](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md):** every one has a paediatric, obstetric and geriatric face; neurology is psychiatry's nearest neighbour; the organ-system specialties are the nodes of the graph that primary care integrates.
- **[13 Acute, perioperative and procedural care](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md):** neonatal and paediatric intensive care, obstetric anaesthesia and haemorrhage, the collapsed older patient, the overdose, orthogeriatrics and withdrawal of life support are all joint territory.
- **[15 Medicine in society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md):** consent and capacity for children, the mentally ill and the dying; compulsory treatment; abortion and assisted dying law; the organisation of primary care; social determinants acting through childhood adversity, behaviour and disability.

## Connections across layers

This layer is where the knowledge base turns from diseases to people. From below it inherits the sciences of how a body is built, runs and fails, and the methods of diagnosis, treatment and evidence; from layers 12 and 13 it inherits the specialties; and it reassembles them around a child, a pregnancy, a mind, an old age or a lifetime. What it passes upward is the demonstration that outcomes depend on integration as much as on any single intervention, and the hardest problems of value and law, at the margins of viability, capacity and death, that [medicine in society](../15-medicine-in-society/README.md) must settle.
