## What this area is about

Obstetrics and gynaecology is the medicine of the female reproductive system across the life course, and of the one physiological state, pregnancy, in which a clinician has two patients inside one body. It answers two questions: how does a system that rebuilds itself monthly and can grow another human go wrong, and how is a pregnancy brought safely to a birth? It is at once a medical specialty (reproductive endocrinology, maternal medicine), a surgical one (caesarean section, hysterectomy, laparoscopy) and a public-health one (contraception, cervical screening, maternal mortality).

The unifying idea is **one hormonal axis and the tissues it commands**. The hypothalamic–pituitary–ovarian axis drives the cycle, conception, pregnancy and the menopause; the tissues it governs (endometrium, myometrium, breast, bone, vessel wall) respond in health and disease; and the placenta, a temporary organ built by the fetus, takes over the mother's physiology for nine months and writes the terms of most obstetric complications.

```
   hypothalamus --GnRH--> pituitary --FSH/LH--> ovary --oestrogen/progesterone--> tissues

        +-------------- negative feedback -------------------+
   steered OFF ........ contraception

```

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Paediatrics](../01-paediatrics/README.md):** the fetus becomes the neonatologist's patient; antenatal diagnosis, steroids for lung maturity, the timing of delivery and maternal disease all decide the newborn's state. Adolescent gynaecology and safeguarding overlap with paediatrics.
- **[Psychiatry](../03-psychiatry/README.md):** perinatal mental illness (antenatal and postnatal depression, postpartum psychosis), prescribing psychotropic drugs in pregnancy, and the psychological weight of infertility and loss.
- **[Geriatrics and palliative care](../04-geriatrics-and-palliative-care/README.md):** the postmenopausal decades bring osteoporosis, prolapse and gynaecological cancer into the older woman's multimorbidity; palliative care takes advanced ovarian and cervical cancer.
- **[Primary care and integration](../05-primary-care-and-integration/README.md):** most contraception, cervical screening, menopause care, early pregnancy and routine antenatal care happen in general practice and midwifery, with the specialist seeing the complicated minority.

## Interfaces with other specialties

Obstetrics has a dedicated interface with almost every medical specialty, because pregnancy either worsens or unmasks their diseases: **obstetric medicine** is a joint clinic with [cardiology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/01-cardiology/README.md) (valve disease, cardiomyopathy, repaired congenital hearts), [nephrology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/03-nephrology/README.md) (lupus nephritis, transplant recipients, pre-eclampsia versus intrinsic kidney disease), [endocrinology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/05-endocrinology/README.md) (pre-existing and gestational diabetes, thyroid disease), [haematology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/06-haematology/README.md) (anticoagulation, sickle cell disease, thrombocytopenia, alloimmunisation), [neurology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/10-neurology/README.md) (epilepsy and valproate), [rheumatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/09-rheumatology-and-clinical-immunology/README.md) (antiphospholipid syndrome, lupus) and [infectious diseases](../../12-organ-system-specialties/08-infectious-diseases/README.md) (HIV, hepatitis B, syphilis, group B streptococcus). The hand-over rule is that the obstetrician owns the pregnancy and the physician owns the disease, with the pregnancy's timing decided jointly.

On the acute side, [anaesthesia](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/02-anaesthesia-and-perioperative-medicine/README.md) is present at every operative birth and provides labour analgesia; [critical care](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/03-critical-care/README.md) takes eclampsia, massive haemorrhage, sepsis and amniotic fluid embolism; [emergency medicine](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/04-emergency-medicine/README.md) receives early pregnancy bleeding and the collapsed woman of childbearing age in whom ectopic pregnancy is assumed until excluded; general [surgery](../../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/01-surgery/README.md) shares the acute abdomen (appendicitis in pregnancy, ovarian torsion) and bowel involvement by endometriosis or cancer; and interventional radiology occludes uterine arteries for haemorrhage and fibroids.

Gynaecology's borders are with urology (incontinence, fistula, the urogynaecology subspecialty), [gastroenterology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/04-gastroenterology-and-hepatology/README.md) (pelvic pain, bowel endometriosis), [oncology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/07-oncology/README.md) and histopathology in the gynaecological cancer multidisciplinary team, [dermatology](../../12-organ-system-specialties/11-dermatology/README.md) (vulval disease, hirsutism), and sexual health. **Disputed territory** includes pelvic pain (gynaecology versus gastroenterology versus pain medicine), the menopause (gynaecology versus endocrinology versus primary care), incontinence (urogynaecology versus urology) and the medical management of the pregnant woman (obstetrician versus physician). **Joint decisions** are made in the fetal medicine meeting (with neonatology, genetics and paediatric surgery), the maternal medicine network, and the cancer multidisciplinary team. **Emergencies that cross boundaries** are postpartum haemorrhage (obstetrics, anaesthesia, haematology, radiology, critical care), eclampsia (obstetrics, anaesthesia, critical care, neurology) and the ruptured ectopic (emergency medicine, gynaecology, anaesthesia).

## Connections across layers

From below, the area takes [reproductive endocrinology](../../06-physiology/07-endocrine-system/reproductive-endocrinology.md) and the [reproductive physiology](../../06-physiology/09-reproduction-and-the-life-course/README.md) of layer 06 as its operating manual, [development and teratology](../../04-genetics-evolution-and-development/03-development/README.md) for the embryo, [haemostasis](../../06-physiology/08-blood/haemostasis-and-coagulation.md) and [thrombosis](../../08-pathology/02-hemodynamic-disorders/thrombosis-embolism-and-infarction.md) for the clotting of pregnancy, [neoplasia](../../08-pathology/03-neoplasia/README.md) and [virology](../../07-microbiology-and-immunology/01-microbes-and-infection/viruses.md) for its cancers, [endocrine drugs](../../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/03-drug-classes-by-mechanism/endocrine-and-metabolic-drugs.md) for contraception and replacement, [screening theory](../../10-epidemiology-and-evidence/03-screening-and-prevention/screening-principles-and-biases.md) for the cervical programme and [ultrasound](../../11-clinical-method-and-diagnostics/03-imaging-and-physiological-measurement/ultrasound.md) as its principal imaging. To the layers above it gives the newborn, the woman's lifelong cardiovascular and metabolic risk profile, and the maternal mortality ratio that [global health](../../15-medicine-in-society/03-populations-and-the-future/global-health-and-pandemics.md) uses as the sharpest single measure of a health system.

## Cross-cutting threads

- [Feedback and homeostasis](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#feedback-and-homeostasis): negative feedback runs the cycle and is borrowed by contraception; positive feedback runs labour and lactation.
- [Compensation and decompensation](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#compensation-and-decompensation): pregnancy as a stress test that reveals hidden cardiac, renal and metabolic reserve.
- [Barriers and compartments](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#barriers-and-compartments): the placenta decides what crosses (glucose, IgG, drugs, viruses) and what does not (insulin, heparin).
- [Time courses](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#time-courses): teratogenic windows, the decade from papillomavirus infection to cancer, the years of perimenopause.
- [Harm and benefit](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#harm-and-benefit): caesarean rates, fetal monitoring, hormone replacement and screening are all trade-offs measured in trials.
- [Trade-offs and evolutionary legacy](../../CROSS-CUTTING.md#trade-offs-and-evolutionary-legacy): the narrow pelvis of a bipedal, large-brained species makes human birth uniquely hazardous.
