## What this area is about

The nervous system is the body's fast signalling and control layer: it senses the world and the body, decides, and acts through muscle and gland within milliseconds to seconds, where hormones take minutes to hours. This area answers how a cell can carry a signal at up to 100 m/s without loss, how signals are passed between cells and modified in the passing, how the physical world is encoded, how movement is produced and corrected, how the viscera are run without conscious effort, what the cortex adds, and how damage is signalled as pain.

The unifying idea is that **every neural signal is an ion moving down an electrochemical gradient through a channel that something has opened**. The Nernst and Goldman equations give the potentials; voltage-gated channels turn them into a propagating spike; ligand-gated channels at the synapse turn a chemical message back into current. Everything above that, sensation, movement, autonomic reflexes, memory, is circuitry built from those two elements plus the receptor that decides what the message means.

## Connections to sibling areas

- **[Integrative principles](../01-integrative-principles/README.md):** the hypothalamus is the comparator for temperature, osmolality and energy, and the brainstem for pressure and carbon dioxide; sleep is a brain state that resets the loops.
- **[Cardiovascular system](../03-cardiovascular-system/README.md):** the baroreflex is autonomic physiology; the cardiac action potential is a variant of the neuronal one with a calcium plateau; the brain takes ~15% of cardiac output and tolerates no interruption.
- **[Respiratory system](../04-respiratory-system/README.md):** the breathing rhythm is generated in the medulla and tuned by chemoreceptors; opioids depress it through the same receptors that relieve pain.
- **[Renal system](../05-renal-system/README.md):** sympathetic nerves and vasopressin from the hypothalamus set renal sodium and water handling.
- **[Gastrointestinal system](../06-gastrointestinal-system/README.md):** the enteric nervous system is a third autonomic division with as many neurons as the spinal cord; the vagus links gut and brain in both directions.
- **[Endocrine system](../07-endocrine-system/README.md):** the hypothalamus converts neural signals into hormones; the adrenal medulla is a sympathetic ganglion that secretes into blood.
- **[Blood](../08-blood/README.md):** the brain's dependence on glucose and oxygen makes it the first organ to fail in anaemia, hypoglycaemia and hypotension.
- **[Reproduction and the life course](../09-reproduction-and-the-life-course/README.md):** myelination and synaptic pruning continue for two decades; the newborn's reflexes are the spinal and brainstem circuits before cortical control.
- **[Musculoskeletal system and skin](../10-musculoskeletal-system-and-skin/README.md):** the motor unit ends on skeletal muscle; the skin is the sensory sheet.
