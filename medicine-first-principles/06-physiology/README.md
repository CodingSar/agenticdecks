## What this area is about

Physiology is the account of how a body of some 37 trillion cells, each needing oxygen, fuel and a narrow range of temperature, pH and ionic composition, keeps every one of them alive while the whole organism runs, fasts, sleeps or fights an infection. Anatomy (layer 05) describes the machine at rest; physiology describes it running, in numbers.

Three ideas from the layers below make up almost all of it. **Gradients and flow:** oxygen, carbon dioxide, ions, water, heat and blood move down gradients of concentration, pressure, charge or temperature, and the body spends about a fifth of its resting energy on pumps that make the gradients it later uses. **Supply and demand:** every tissue consumes oxygen and substrate at a rate that varies many-fold between rest and work, and delivery must track consumption or the tissue fails within minutes (brain, heart) or hours (kidney, gut). **Feedback:** nearly every regulated variable (arterial pressure, arterial carbon dioxide, osmolality, glucose, calcium, core temperature) is sensed, compared with a set point and corrected by an effector, with the gain, delay and oscillation that control theory predicts. Ask of any physiological quantity "what is its value, what gradient drives it and what loop controls it?" and most of clinical medicine becomes the study of a quantity, a gradient or a loop that has failed.

## Why this layer sits here

Physiology takes from [00 Physical and chemical foundations](../00-physical-and-chemical-foundations/README.md) the laws it applies: diffusion and osmosis, pressure and flow, the electrostatics of membranes, buffers and thermodynamics. From [01 Quantitative and scientific foundations](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/README.md) it takes [feedback and control](../01-quantitative-and-scientific-foundations/03-systems-and-models/feedback-and-control.md), compartment models and exponential processes. From [02 Biochemistry](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/README.md) it takes the machinery: [ion pumps and channels](../02-biochemistry-and-molecular-biology/04-cell-signalling/membrane-transport-and-ion-gradients.md), receptors, second messengers and the metabolism it regulates. From [03 Cell biology](../03-cell-biology/README.md) it takes the cell as the unit kept alive, and from [05 Tissues and anatomy](../05-tissues-and-anatomy/README.md) the structures that constrain function: valves make the cardiac cycle possible, the lung's shape makes ventilation–perfusion matching a problem, the nephron's hairpin makes concentrated urine possible.

What the layers above need is a **quantitative model of the normal body with its control loops identified**, because disease is deviation from that model. [07 Microbiology and immunology](../07-microbiology-and-immunology/README.md) moves immune cells through this circulation. [08 Pathology](../08-pathology/README.md) describes a gradient, supply or loop failing: oedema is Starling forces unbalanced, shock is delivery below demand. [09 Pharmacology](../09-pharmacology-and-therapeutics/README.md) manipulates control points this layer has explained. Every specialty in [12](../12-organ-system-specialties/README.md), [13](../13-acute-perioperative-and-procedural-care/README.md) and [14](../14-life-course-mind-and-integrative-care/README.md) is one organ system's physiology failing in a characteristic way, in a particular body.

The one abstraction this layer contributes is **the regulated variable**: a quantity the body measures, defends and trades off against other regulated variables when it must.

Read 01 first: it names the variables everything else defends. Then 02, the fast controller every other system uses. 03, 04 and 08 are the oxygen supply chain. 05 closes the loop on volume, osmolality and acid that 01 opened. 06 and 07 are intake and slow control. 09 and 10 apply all of it to a different body or to the effectors that do mechanical work; the smooth muscle leaf in 10 is the effector that 03, 04 and 06 all assume.

## Connections across layers

Physiology is the hinge of the tree: below it are laws and parts, above it are failures and interventions. Know the normal value, driving gradient and control loop for arterial oxygen, pressure, sodium, potassium, calcium, glucose and pH, and every specialty leaf reads as a perturbation of something understood, every drug leaf as a lever on a loop already drawn.
